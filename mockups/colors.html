Color

The idea: ink and paper do the work. Each theme has one color, its dot, which means "something here is yours to read". Everything else is ink, grey or a word. Every sample below uses the real theme colors and the letters you just chose.

1. The star

Filled means starred, an outline means not. The shape already says it, so the color only has to look calm.

2. Delete, and errors

Today it's a red. It sits right next to Anodised's and Fountain pen's orange dot, so in those themes "Unsubscribe" and "unread" look like the same color. Errors (a refused field) should follow whatever delete does, so the app has one way of warning you.

3. The seven dots as one family

Today two dots are the same orange (Anodised and Fountain pen), some are loud and some faint, and Heather's dot is gold while its greys are lilac. The retune gives every dot the same lightness and strength and spreads them evenly, still never green or red. Original and Plain don't change.

4. nine turtles' own color, outside the app

The app icon, the preview card when someone shares a link, and emails. Inside the app the blue means "unread", so using it as the brand would give it two meanings.
