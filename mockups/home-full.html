Read people. Write back.

There are people online you'd like to know. Most of them have a blog, a newsletter or a channel. nine turtles keeps them in one quiet place, helps you write back to them, and ends when you've read what's there.

Open the app

Read people.

Follow a person, not a platform: their blog, their newsletter in your own address, their videos and their podcast, all in one calm list. Soon, one page per person gathers all of them under their name.

Write back.Coming soon

Reading someone well makes you want to answer. Next, nine turtles will let you reply by email from the article, privately, from your own address.

It ends.

No endless scroll, no counts, nothing to check. New writing waits quietly. When you've read what's there, the list says so, and you close it.

Nobody in between.

No algorithm, no likes, and no AI ranking or rewriting what people wrote. Every article links back to where it was written. It's built on RSS, the open standard, so your list is always yours to take with you.

From me.

I'm Rohit. I made nine turtles because the people I like reading are still writing, and I kept missing them in the scroll. I read in it every day. If something's wrong, or right, write to me.
