## Who we are

nine turtles is made by {{ site.maker }}. In this policy, "we" means the
people who run nine turtles, and "you" means anyone who visits this
website or uses the app. To ask anything about your data, write to
<a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a>.

## This website (nineturtles.app)

- **No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.** This website doesn't count
  visitors, set cookies or load anything from other companies. Its fonts
  are served from this website itself.
- **Hosting.** The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host,
  GitHub's servers see your IP address (your device's address on the
  internet) when they send you a page, and GitHub may keep it for a short
  time to keep the service secure. See
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Email.** If you write to us, we keep your message and address only
  to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

## The app (app.nineturtles.app)

Today the app at app.nineturtles.app is one person's private
installation: it holds only its owner's reading. Anyone can also run their
own copy of nine turtles; a copy you run yourself sends nothing to us.

When nine turtles opens for others to sign up, it will keep only what it
needs to work:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address | To sign you in, reset your password and send receipts |
| Your password, scrambled so it can't be read back (a "hash") | To sign you in |
| The feeds and folders you follow, the articles they bring, what you've read and starred, and your settings | To be your reader: this is the service |
| A sign-in cookie on each device you use | To keep you signed in; it's the only cookie, and it never tracks you anywhere else |
| Short-lived technical logs (times, errors, never what you read) | To keep the service running and secure |
| Payment details | Held by the payment company, never by us (to be decided) |

We never sell your data, never show ads, never share what you read, and
never use your reading to train anyone's AI.

### Publishers and the web

- **Checking feeds.** nine turtles' server, not your device, fetches the
  feeds you follow. Publishers see that a copy of nine turtles asked for
  their feed, not who you are.
- **Images in articles** load from the publisher's own site, as they would
  on their page, so the publisher's server sees your IP address. nine
  turtles tells them nothing about where the image is being shown.
- **YouTube videos** load only when you press Play, in YouTube's
  privacy-enhanced player. Once you press Play, YouTube's own
  [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold about you,
correct it, give you a copy, or delete it, and you can complain to your
data protection authority. In the app you can already export your feeds
(as OPML) and your starred articles (as JSON Feed) at any time. Deleting
your account will delete everything in it.

## How long we keep it

Your reading stays as long as you keep your account. When you delete it,
it's gone from the live service straight away, and from any backups
within 30 days (to be confirmed once backups are set up).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the change and its date will
be shown here, and people with an account will be told by email before it
takes effect.
