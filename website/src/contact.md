## What to write about

Anything at all: a question, an idea, a feed that won't load, something
that's hard to read or use, or a kind word. If something isn't working, it
helps to say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened
instead, and which device and browser you were using.

## Problems with accessibility

If any part of nine turtles is hard to use with a screen reader, a
keyboard, larger text or high contrast, that's treated as a bug, and a
serious one. Please write, and say what you use to read.

## Publishers

If you publish a feed and would like nine turtles to treat it differently,
for example to check it less often, write to us. nine turtles identifies
itself to every site it checks as `nine-turtles`, followed by its version.

## Privacy and security

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to the same
address (more in the [privacy policy](/privacy/)). To report a security
problem, write with "Security" in the subject line; please don't share it
publicly until it's fixed.

## Elsewhere

The code is public, and you can open an issue there too:
[nine turtles on GitHub]({{ site.source }}). We don't have social media
accounts. That's on purpose.
