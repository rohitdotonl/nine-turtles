<p class="lede">
  You can cancel whenever you like, on your own, in a few seconds. If
  nine turtles isn't right for you, ask and we'll refund you.
</p>

## Cancelling

- Cancel at any time in the app's settings (to be decided: the exact
  place). You don't need to write to anyone or give a reason.
- After you cancel, you won't be charged again. You keep using nine
  turtles until the end of the month or year you've already paid for.
- Your reading stays yours: you can export your feeds and starred articles
  before or after you cancel, and delete your account whenever you like.

## Refunds

- If you ask within the first days of a payment (to be decided: how many
  days), you get your money back in full, no questions asked.
- After that, we'll still look at every request kindly, for example if you
  were charged after you meant to cancel.
- To ask, write to <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a>
  from the email address on your account.
- Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with. Payments are
  handled by Dodo Payments, so the refund comes from them and can take a
  few days to show, depending on your bank.

## Questions

Write to <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a>. Every
message is read by a person.
