<p class="lede">
  Highlight the words worth keeping, and write down what you think. Only you
  ever see your highlights and notes.
</p>

## Highlight words

1. In an article, choose a few words, as you would to copy them.
2. Press **Highlight** in the small menu beside them, or press <kbd>H</kbd>.
3. The menu says **Highlighted**. Press **Add a note…** to write a note on
   the words there and then, or **Done**.

To write a note on them instead, press **Add a note…** (or
<kbd>N</kbd>), type it and press **Save**.

Press a highlight to see its note, edit it, copy the words, or remove it.

## Write a note on an article

Choose **More › Add a note…**, or press <kbd>N</kbd> with
no words chosen. The note sits at the end of the article, under your
highlights.

## Write a note on a person

Open the person's page (choose them in the sidebar) and press **Add a
note…**: where you met, when you last wrote. Typing a word of it in **Find a
person or folder** finds them.

## Find them again

- **Notes** in the sidebar lists every article you highlighted or wrote a
  note on. It appears after your first one.
- Search looks through your highlights and notes too. Choose **With notes** in
  **Search options** to see only those articles.
- **Settings › Your data › Export notes and highlights** saves them all as
  one Markdown file.

## Good to know

- On a video or a podcast that's playing, a new note starts with the time
  it's at. Press that time later to go back there.
- No connection? Highlights and notes are still kept on your device, and
  reach your account as soon as you're back online. A quiet line says so
  until they do.
- Articles you highlighted or wrote a note on are kept for good, like
  starred ones.
- If the writer changes the article, nine turtles finds your words again.
  Words that are gone stay in your list, marked "No longer in the article".
