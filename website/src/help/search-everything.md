  Search looks through every article nine turtles has for you, read or not,

## Search

1. Press <kbd>/</kbd>, or choose the **Search** field above the list.
2. Type a few words. Matching articles appear, with the words picked out.

Search starts in the list you're in, shown as a small label such as
**in: Cooking ×**. Press its × to search everything instead.
<kbd>Esc</kbd> takes you back to where you were.

## How words match

- Every word you type must be in the article.
- A word also finds longer words that start with it: "walk" finds "walking".
- Words in "quotation marks" must appear together, in that order.
- Articles whose titles match come first.
- Your highlights and notes are searched too: a result found by them says
  "Highlighted" or "Your note".

## Narrow it down

Press **Search options** (the sliders beside the field) to choose where to
look, to show only unread or starred articles or those with your notes, or
to pick a time: the past
week, the past month, or dates of your own.

## Good to know

- Search needs a connection.
- **Mark all as read** works on what a search found.
- Search understands English, other languages written in the Latin alphabet,
  and Hindi.
