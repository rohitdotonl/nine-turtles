  Search looks through every article nine turtles has for you, read or not,
  titles and text alike.
</p>

## Search

1. Press <kbd>/</kbd>, or the magnifier beside the list's title: the
   **Search articles** field opens under it.
2. Type a few words. Matching articles appear, with the words picked out.

Typed something in **Find a person or folder** at the top of the sidebar
instead? Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or **Search articles for “…”** under it, and
the same words are searched in every article.

Search starts in the list you're in, shown as a small label such as
**in: Cooking ×**. Press its × to search everything instead.
<kbd>Esc</kbd> takes you back to where you were.

## How words match

- Every word you type must be in the article.
- A word also finds longer words that start with it: "walk" finds "walking".
- Words in "quotation marks" must appear together, in that order.
- Articles whose titles match come first.
- Your highlights and notes are searched too: a result found by them says
  "Highlighted" or "Your note".

## Narrow it down

Search looks in the list you're in, and the field says so ("in: Tech").
Press the **×** on that to search everything instead. To search one person
or folder, open it first, then search; to search only your starred
articles, open **Starred** first. If nothing turns up there, press
**Search all articles** to look everywhere for the same words.

## Good to know

- Search needs a connection.
- **Mark all as read** works on what a search found.
- Search understands English and other languages written in the Latin
  alphabet, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, Hebrew and Korean. Thai, Lao,
  Khmer and Burmese can't be searched yet. More in <a href="/help/read-in-other-languages/">Read in other languages</a>.
