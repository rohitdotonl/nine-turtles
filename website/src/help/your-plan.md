## Trying

A new account starts with a free trial. There's no card to give, and no
countdown nagging you.

## Choosing a plan

1. Open **Settings › Account › Your plan**. It says where you are: in your
   trial, on a plan, or past the end.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**, and pay on the next page.

Choosing during the trial keeps the trial's days that are left.

Payments are made through Dodo Payments, which sells nine turtles on my
behalf. Your card goes to them, never to me.

## Cancelling

In **Your plan**, press **Billing and cancelling**. You cancel yourself,
with nobody to ask. The plan lasts until the end of what you paid for.

## If a trial or plan ends

You can still read, star, search, download and change settings, and your
newsletters keep arriving. You can't follow anyone new, and nine turtles
checks for new articles once a day instead of all day long.

## Good to know

- If a payment doesn't go through, your plan keeps going for 7 days while
  it's tried again.
- Deleting your account cancels your plan first.
- More in <a href="/refunds/">Refunds and cancelling</a>.
