<p class="lede">
  You don't need to know anything about feeds. Paste the address of a
  website you like, and nine turtles finds its feed for you.
</p>

## Follow someone

1. Press **+ Add** at the foot of the sidebar (or press <kbd>A</kbd>).
2. Paste their website's address, such as <code>example.com</code>.
   You can leave off the <code>https://</code>.
3. Press **Add**.
4. nine turtles asks what they are: "Maya Chen is…", with their site
   and newest title. Choose **A person** (someone you might write back to)
   or **A publication** (anything you only read or watch, such as a
   magazine, with no Write back). Its guess is already chosen. Press **Done**.

When a blog signs every article with one writer's name, nine turtles calls
them by that name, with the blog's title under it on their page. Want a
different name? Change it in **Edit** on their page; yours always stays.

Their page (press their name at the top of their list) keeps what's
between you: **Write to** them, your own note on them, **Lately** (their
three newest pieces) and **Your highlights**, the words you highlighted in
their writing, each with the article it's from. Press a title to read it.

They appear in your sidebar under **People** or **Publications**. Their
five newest articles wait in **Unread**; anything older is on their page,
already marked read, so following someone who writes a lot doesn't bury
everything else. From now on, everything new they post arrives by itself,
unread.

If the site offers more than one feed, such as one for everything and one
per topic, nine turtles lists them, each with its newest title, so you can
tell them apart. The first is ticked; tick any others you want and press
**Follow**. Feeds you follow together from one site become one person, like a
blog and its podcast. A feed that only repeats another in a different format
isn't listed.

Already follow their blog, and now want their channel too? Add it from
their page instead, with **+ Add another place** in Edit: see
<a href="/help/one-person-several-places/">See everything one person makes
in one place</a>.

## Podcasts and YouTube

They work the same way. Paste a podcast's website, or a YouTube channel's
address, or the address of one of their videos. More in
<a href="/help/listen-to-podcasts/">Listen to podcasts</a> and
<a href="/help/watch-youtube-videos/">Watch YouTube videos</a>.

Many newsletters, such as those on Substack, have a feed too: paste their
address like any other. One that only comes by email has none, so when nine
turtles finds no feed it shows your own nine turtles address, with **Copy**:
sign up with it and each issue arrives here. See
<a href="/help/newsletters/">Read your newsletters here</a>.

## Not sure what to paste?

On your first visit, the welcome offers three examples under **Or try one**:
a blog, a newsletter and a YouTube channel. Press **Try** on one to follow it
at once and see their newest post right there. **Start reading** takes you
into nine turtles.

## Follow from anywhere

You can follow a site you're looking at without copying its address:

- **Android:** once nine turtles is <a href="/help/install-the-app/">installed</a>,
  share the page to nine turtles.
- **iPhone and iPad:** a ready-made Shortcut for Safari's **Share** button is
  on its way. Until then, copy the page's address and paste it into
  **+ Add**.
- **A computer:** in **Settings › Install the app on any device**, on the
  **Computer** tab, drag the **Add to nine turtles** button to your
  bookmarks bar, then press it on any blog. No bookmarks bar? Press
  **Command Shift B** on a Mac, or **Ctrl Shift B** elsewhere. Can't drag?
  Paste the blog's address into **+ Add**.

nine turtles asks "Follow…?" first, and follows only when you say so.

## If it doesn't work

- **"This site doesn't seem to offer a feed."** nine turtles has already
  looked on the page, the site's home page and the usual feed addresses.
  Some sites don't have one. Look on the site for a link marked RSS, Atom or
  Feed, and paste that.
  If a site has no feed at all, a service that makes feeds from websites
  (such as RSS Bridge or OpenRSS) can give you one: paste the address it
  gives you, and nine turtles follows it like any other.
  Or ask them for one: press **Write to** under "Ask them for a feed".
  nine turtles looks on the site's home page (and its contact page) for their
  email address, and your email app opens with a short note you can change.
- **"That site didn't answer."** It may be down for a moment. Try again
  later.

## Good to know

- Nobody is followed until you press **Add** or **Follow**.
- You can follow up to 1,000 at once.
