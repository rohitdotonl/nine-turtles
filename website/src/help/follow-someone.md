<p class="lede">
  You don't need to know anything about feeds. Paste the address of a
  website you like, and nine turtles finds its feed for you.
</p>

## Follow someone

1. Press **+ Add** at the foot of the sidebar (or press <kbd>A</kbd>).
2. Paste the address of their website, such as <code>example.com</code>.
   You can leave off the <code>https://</code>.
3. Press **Add**.
4. nine turtles asks whether it's **Someone** (a person) or **Something** (a
   publication, such as a magazine), with its guess already chosen. Press
   **Done**.

They appear in your sidebar under **People** or **Publications**, and their
newest articles are ready to read. From now on, new ones arrive by
themselves.

If the site offers more than one feed, such as one for everything and one
per topic, nine turtles lists them, each with its newest title and how often
it posts, so you can tell them apart: choose one and press **Add**. A feed
that only repeats another in a different format isn't listed.

Already follow their blog, and now want their channel too? Add it from
their page instead, with **+ Add a source**: see
<a href="/help/one-person-several-places/">See everything one person makes
in one place</a>.

## Podcasts and YouTube

They work the same way. Paste a podcast's website, or a YouTube channel's
address, or the address of one of their videos. More in
<a href="/help/listen-to-podcasts/">Listen to podcasts</a> and
<a href="/help/watch-youtube-videos/">Watch YouTube videos</a>.

Newsletters are different: they come by email. See
<a href="/help/newsletters/">Read your newsletters here</a>.

## Follow from anywhere

You can follow a site you're looking at without copying its address:

- **Android:** once nine turtles is <a href="/help/install-the-app/">installed</a>,
  share the page to nine turtles.
- **iPhone and iPad:** use the Apple Shortcut in **Settings › Add from
  anywhere**.
- **A computer:** add the bookmark from **Settings › Add from anywhere** to
  your browser, and press it on any site.

nine turtles asks "Follow…?" first, and follows only when you say so.

## If it doesn't work

- **"This site doesn't seem to offer a feed."** nine turtles has already
  looked on the page, the site's home page and the usual feed addresses.
  Some sites don't have one. Look on the site for a link marked RSS, Atom or
  Feed, and paste that.
  If a site has no feed at all, a service that makes feeds from websites
  (such as RSS Bridge or OpenRSS) can give you one: paste the address it
  gives you, and nine turtles follows it like any other.
- **"That site didn't answer."** It may be down for a moment. Try again
  later.

## Good to know

- Nobody is followed until you press **Add** or **Follow**.
- You can follow up to 1,000 at once.
