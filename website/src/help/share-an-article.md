<p class="lede">
  When you share an article, you share the writer's own page, never a
  nine turtles link. Your friend reads it where it was published.
</p>

## Share it

1. Open the article.
2. Choose **More**, then:
   - **Share**, on a phone or tablet, to send it with the apps on your
     device, or
   - **Copy link**, anywhere else (or press <kbd>C</kbd>). nine turtles says
     **Link copied.** Paste it wherever you like.

## Good to know

- Tracking tags some sites add to links (such as <code>utm_source</code>)
  are taken off first.
- There are no buttons for particular social networks, on purpose: you
  choose where it goes.
