<p class="lede">
  When you share an article, you share the writer's own page, never a
  nine turtles link. Your friend reads it where it was published.
</p>

## Share it

1. Open the article.
2. On a phone or tablet, press **Share** in the bar at the bottom (a box
   with an arrow on an iPhone or iPad; three linked dots elsewhere): it opens
   your device's share menu, to send it with the apps you have.
3. On a computer, open **More** (…) at the top and choose **Copy link**, or
   press <kbd>C</kbd>. nine turtles says **Link copied.** Paste it wherever
   you like.

You can do the same without opening it: right-click its row in the list
(or press and hold it on a phone) and choose **Copy link** (**Share** on a
phone). On a computer, you can also drag its row into a note, an email or
another tab: the writer's own link goes with it.

## Good to know

- Tracking tags some sites add to links (such as <code>utm_source</code>)
  are taken off first.
- There are no buttons for particular social networks, on purpose: you
  choose where it goes.
