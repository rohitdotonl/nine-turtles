<p class="lede">
  Hiding is a list of words. Articles about them stay out of your lists,
  but nothing is deleted.
</p>

## Hide articles about something

1. Open **Settings › Hiding**.
2. Type a word or phrase in **Hide articles about…**.
3. Under the field, before anything is hidden, you see how many unread
   articles it would hide, and the newest few, each with who wrote it and
   whether the word is in the title or the text. Nothing is hidden yet.
4. Choose how long: **For a week**, **For a month** or **For good** (the
   usual one).
5. Press **Add**, or Return.

Unread articles you already have about it are hidden at once, and the line
under the list says how many. **Undo** puts them back.

## Hide one person's articles about something

In an article, open **More** (…) and choose **Hide articles about…**. Or
open someone's page and press **Hide articles about…** at its foot.
Settings › Hiding opens with "Only from" that person. Type the word and
press **Add**. Press **Everyone instead** to hide it from everyone.

## What hidden means

A hidden article is kept and marked read. It's in none of your lists, and
search still finds it, marked "· Hidden". A word for a person covers all of
their sources: their blog, their newsletter, their videos.

## See what was hidden

**Settings › Hiding › Hidden articles** lists the newest 200. Press
**Unhide** to bring one back, unread.

## Good to know

- Each word's row says whose articles it hides and for how long, such as
  "Everyone · 5 days left".
- When a week or a month is up, the word stops hiding and leaves the list.
- **Remove** takes a word away, with **Undo**. Neither brings back what it
  already hid; use Hidden articles for that.
