  Hiding is one sentence: "Hide articles about <em>election</em> from
  everyone." Hidden articles stay out of your lists, but nothing is
  deleted.
</p>

## Hide articles about something

1. In an article, open **More** (…) and choose **Hide articles about…**.
   Or open someone's page and press **Hide articles about…** at its foot.
2. Settings › Hiding opens with the sentence already set to that person.
   Type the word or phrase.
3. Change **from** to **everyone** if you'd like it hidden whoever writes
   it.
4. Press **Save**.

If unread articles you already have match, nine turtles asks whether to
hide those too. **Undo** puts them back.

You can also start from **Settings › Hiding › Hide articles about…**.

## What hidden means

A hidden article is kept and marked read. It's in none of your lists, and
search still finds it, marked "· Hidden". A rule for a person covers all of
their sources: their blog, their newsletter, their videos.

## See what was hidden

**Settings › Hiding › Hidden articles** lists the newest 200. Press
**Unhide** to bring one back, unread.

## Good to know

- Rules work on new articles as they arrive.
- Each rule has a switch to pause it, and **Edit** to change or delete it.
  Deleting a rule doesn't bring back what it already hid; use Hidden
  articles for that.
