<p class="lede">
  A rule is a sentence: "When a new article has <em>election</em> in its
  title, from all feeds, then mute it." Muted articles stay out of your
  lists, but nothing is deleted.
</p>

## Make a rule

1. Open **Settings › Rules** and press **New rule…**
2. Fill in the sentence:
   - the **word or phrase** to look for, and whether in **its title** or
     **its title or text**;
   - **by** an author, if you like;
   - **from** everyone, one folder, or one person;
   - **then** what to do: **mute it**, **star it** or **mark it as read**.
3. Press **Save**.

If unread articles you already have match, nine turtles offers to do the
same to them. **Undo** puts them back.

To make a rule for one person, choose **More › Edit person…**, then **Add a
rule…**

## What each one does

- **Mute it:** kept, but out of every list and counted as read. Search
  still finds it.
- **Star it:** it goes straight into Starred.
- **Mark it as read:** it stays in its list, already read.

## See what was muted

**Settings › Rules › Muted articles** lists the newest 200. Press
**Unmute** to bring one back, unread.

## Good to know

- Words are found whole, whatever their capital letters: a rule for "AI"
  won't catch "said".
- Rules work on new articles as they arrive.
- Each rule has a switch to pause it, and **Edit** to change or delete it.
  Deleting a rule doesn't bring back what it already muted; use Muted
  articles for that.
