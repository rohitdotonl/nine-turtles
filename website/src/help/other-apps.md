<p class="lede">
  Prefer to read in Reeder, NetNewsWire or another feed reader app? Connect
  it, and what you follow, read and star stays the same in both.
</p>

## Make an app password

1. In nine turtles, open **Settings › Other apps**.
2. Press **Make an app password**. Copy it now: it's shown only once.

The app password is used only by other apps. Never give them your own
password.

## Add nine turtles to the other app

1. In the other app, add an account of the kind called **FreshRSS** or
   **Google Reader API**.
2. For the server address, paste the **Server address** from **Settings ›
   Other apps**. It ends in <code>/api/greader</code>.
3. For the username, type anything you like, such as your email address.
4. For the password, paste the **App password**.

## What stays the same in both

Who you follow, your folders, which articles are read or unread, and your
stars. Following or unfollowing someone in the other app does the same in
nine turtles.

## Good to know

- The other app shows each blog, channel and newsletter on its own, even
  when they're <a href="/help/one-person-several-places/">one person</a> in
  nine turtles.
- Lost the app password, or a device? Press **Make a new app password**:
  apps using the old one are signed out. **Turn off other apps** signs them
  all out.
- Choosing a new account password turns the app password off too.
