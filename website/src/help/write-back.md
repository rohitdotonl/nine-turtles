<p class="lede">
  Liked something? Tell the person who wrote it. At the end of each article
  from someone you follow is a button to write back to them, by email.
</p>

## Write back

1. Read to the end of the article and press **Write back to Tomas** (with
   their first name, or the blog's whole name when nine turtles doesn't know
   theirs). You can also choose **More › Write back**, or press
   <kbd>W</kbd>.
2. Your own email app opens with their address, and a subject line of
   "Re:" and the article's title.
3. Write what you'd like to say, and send it as you send any email.

## When nine turtles doesn't know their address

Many writers put their email address in their feed, and nine turtles finds
it. When it can't, it asks: type the address into **Their email address**
(you'll often find it on their website's About page), then press **Write
back**.

nine turtles remembers it for that person, so you're asked only once. Their
page shows it under **Email**; to change it, press **Edit**
there and change **Email address**.

## Good to know

- nine turtles never sends the email or sees it. It's between you and them,
  from your own email account.
- Addresses you type are yours alone: nobody else who reads that person sees
  them.
- For a newsletter, the reply goes to the address its emails come from.
