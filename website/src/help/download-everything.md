<p class="lede">
  What you put into nine turtles is yours, and it should outlast us. One
  download holds all of it, as plain pages any browser opens.
</p>

## Download

Open **Settings › Your data** and press **Download everything**. You get one
.zip file, named for the day. Open it (double-click on a computer; tap it in
the Files app on a phone) and it becomes a folder.

## What's in the folder

- **index.html:** open it in any browser. It lists the people you follow,
  with links to their websites, and every article you starred, highlighted
  or wrote a note on, newest first, by year.
- **articles:** one page for each of those articles, with its text, a link
  to the original, and your highlights and notes.
- **follows.opml:** everyone you follow, in their folders. Any feed reader
  can import it.
- **starred.json:** every starred article as a JSON Feed file, which other
  feed readers can import. This includes stars from people you've since
  unfollowed.
- **notes-and-highlights.md:** your notes on people, and every article you
  highlighted or wrote a note on, as one plain text file any notes app opens.

## Good to know

- The pages need no internet and no nine turtles. Pictures in articles stay
  on the writer's website: they show when you're online.
- Downloads work even after a trial or plan has ended.
- Newsletters aren't in the OPML file: they arrive by email, not from a
  feed.
- nine turtles keeps articles you haven't starred for a year after they
  arrived. Starred and highlighted articles are kept for good, so they're
  always in your download.
