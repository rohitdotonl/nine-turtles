<p class="lede">
  Installed, nine turtles has its own icon and opens in its own window, like
  any other app. There's nothing to download from an app store, and it
  updates itself each time you open it.
</p>

**Settings › Install the app** shows the steps for the device you're on.
Here they all are.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open <a href="{{ site.app }}">app.nineturtles.app</a> in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**: the square with an arrow pointing up. In the newest
   Safari, it's under **More** (…).
3. Scroll down if you need to, and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Android

1. Open <a href="{{ site.app }}">app.nineturtles.app</a> in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu: the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to home screen**, or **Install app**.
4. Tap **Install**.

## Mac (Safari)

1. Open <a href="{{ site.app }}">app.nineturtles.app</a> in Safari.
2. In the menu bar, choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
3. Click **Add**.

This needs macOS Sonoma or later.

## Chrome or Edge on a computer

In **Settings › Install the app**, press **Install nine turtles**. Or:

- **Chrome:** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  If it isn't there, open the menu (⋮), then **Cast, save and share**, then
  **Install page as app**.
- **Edge:** open the menu (…), then **Apps**, then **Install this site as an
  app**.

Then click **Install**.

## Firefox

Firefox on a computer can't install web apps. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari
instead, or pin the tab.

## Good to know

- You sign in once on each device.
- The installed app opens without a connection, with your
  <a href="/help/read-offline/">articles kept for reading offline</a>.
- On Android, nine turtles appears in your share menu once it's installed.
