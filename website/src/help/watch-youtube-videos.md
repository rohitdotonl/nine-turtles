<p class="lede">
  Follow a channel and its new videos arrive like articles. Nothing from
  YouTube loads, and nothing plays, until you press Play.
</p>

## Follow a channel

1. Press **+ Add**.
2. Paste the address of the channel, or of any video of theirs. For a video,
   nine turtles asks whether to follow the channel that made it. For a
   playlist, it asks whether you'd like only the playlist or every video
   from its channel.

Following lots of channels already? In **Settings › Your data**, **Import
YouTube subscriptions…** takes the <code>subscriptions.csv</code> file that
Google Takeout gives you.

## Watch

Open a video and press **Play video**. If you stopped part of the way
through, the button says where you'll pick up, such as **Resume from 4:00**,
on any of your devices.

When the description lists times, they appear as **Chapters**: press one to
jump there.

## Speed and captions

Use the settings in YouTube's own player. The next video on the same device
plays at the speed you chose last; choose 1× to go back to normal. YouTube's
player remembers whether you like captions.

## Good to know

- Shorts and live streams are left out. To include them for a channel,
  open the channel's page, press **Edit** (and › beside the channel, if
  it's one of several sources), and turn on **Include Shorts** or
  **Include live streams**.
- Nothing plays by itself, and when a video ends the player closes. YouTube's
  suggestions never appear.
- Videos play from YouTube's privacy-enhanced address
  (youtube-nocookie.com).
- A channel's feed lists only its newest 15 videos.
