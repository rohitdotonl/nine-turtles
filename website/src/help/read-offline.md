<p class="lede">
  There's nothing to set up. Each device you use keeps a copy of your newest
  unread and starred articles, ready for when the connection goes.
</p>

## What's kept

- The first 200 articles in your Unread (or in All, if you've switched
  <a href="/help/unread-on-or-off/">Unread off</a>).
- Your newest 200 starred articles.

Only the words are kept. Pictures, videos and podcast episodes show a line
saying they'll appear when you're back online.

The copies are brought up to date whenever you open the app, when the
connection comes back, and when you refresh.

## Reading offline

Just open nine turtles. The list says **Offline. Showing the articles saved
on this device.** You can read, mark articles read or unread, star them and
undo, as usual. Everything you do is sent as soon as you're back online.

## What needs a connection

Search, adding someone new, Settings, refreshing and the Notes list.

## Good to know

- On an iPhone or iPad, <a href="/help/install-the-app/">add nine turtles to
  your Home Screen</a> first. Safari can clear what a website keeps if you
  haven't visited for about a week; the installed app keeps it.
- To stop keeping copies on a device, open **Settings › Your data** and turn
  off **Keep articles for reading offline**. That device's copies are
  deleted. Signing out deletes them too.
