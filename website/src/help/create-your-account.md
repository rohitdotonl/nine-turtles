<p class="lede">
  All you need is an email address and a password. nine turtles sends one
  email to check the address is yours, and that's it.
</p>

## Sign up

1. Press **Start reading** on [nineturtles.app](/), or go to
   <a href="{{ site.app }}/sign-up">app.nineturtles.app/sign-up</a>.
2. Type your **Email** and choose a **Password** of at least 12 characters.
   Press **Show** in the field to see what you typed.
3. If you like, tell me how you heard about nine turtles. It's optional,
   and only ever counted, never shown with your name.
4. Press **Sign up**.
5. Open the email called **Confirm your email for nine turtles**, press its
   link and press **Confirm email**. If you open it in another browser or on
   another device, type your password first.

You're in, and signed in on that device. The link works once, within 24
hours.

No email? Look in your spam folder, or press **Send again**. Typed the
address wrong? Press **Use another email**.

## Sign in on another device

Go to <a href="{{ site.app }}/sign-in">app.nineturtles.app/sign-in</a>, type your email and
password, and press **Sign in**. Each device stays signed in for a year
after you last used it.

Where your device allows it, **Sign in with a passkey** lets you sign in
with your face, fingerprint or screen lock instead. Add one in **Settings ›
Passkeys**; your password keeps working too.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot?** beside Password.
2. Type your email and press **Email me a link**.
3. Open the email called **Sign in to nine turtles** within 15 minutes, and
   press **Sign in**.
4. Choose a **New password** and press **Set password**.

Your other devices are signed out, so they'll ask for the new password.

## Change your password or email

- **Password:** there's no separate setting. Sign out (**Settings ›
  Account › Sign out**), then use **Forgot?** as above.
- **Email:** in **Settings › Account**, press **Change…** beside Email, type
  the new address and your password, and press **Send link**. Open the link
  sent to the new address within 24 hours and press **Confirm email**. The
  old address gets a note saying it changed.

## Good to know

- Signing out signs out only the device you're on.
- After five wrong passwords, nine turtles asks you to wait a minute before
  trying again, and longer after each further try.
