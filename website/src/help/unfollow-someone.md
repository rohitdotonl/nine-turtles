<p class="lede">
  Unfollowing takes someone out of your sidebar and their articles out of
  your lists. Anything you starred or highlighted can stay.
</p>

## Unfollow

1. Choose them in the sidebar, and open their page: press their name at
   the top of their list, or press <kbd>I</kbd>.
2. Press **Edit**.
3. Press **Unsubscribe from …** at the foot.
4. If you starred, highlighted or wrote notes on any of their articles, or
   wrote a note on them, nine turtles asks what to do with it: **Keep them**
   or **Remove them too**.

nine turtles says "Unsubscribed from …" with **Undo**. Undo brings them
back exactly as they were, with what you'd read and their folders. It lasts
ten minutes, or until you do something else.

If they have several sources (a blog and a channel, say), Unsubscribe leaves
them all. To let go of one source only, press › beside it in Edit, then
**Take out of …**, and unsubscribe from that one.

## Unfollow many at once

Open **Settings › Your feeds**, press **Edit**, tick everyone you'd like to
let go, and press **Unsubscribe**.

## Good to know

- Starred articles and articles you highlighted or wrote a note on are kept for good, unless you chose **Remove them too**.
- Following someone again later starts afresh, with their articles unread.
- For a newsletter, nine turtles also asks the sender to stop, once the ten
  minutes to undo have passed. See
  <a href="/help/newsletters/">Read your newsletters here</a>.
- **Settings › Your feeds** can also show who's been quiet for six months or
  a year, if you'd like to tidy up.
