<p class="lede">
  A podcast is followed like a blog. Its episodes arrive in your lists, and
  each one plays right there, from the podcast's own address.
</p>

## Follow a podcast

Press **+ Add** and paste the podcast's website, or the feed address it
gives. See <a href="/help/follow-someone/">Follow someone from their
website's address</a>. Episodes show headphones and their length in the list.

## Play an episode

Open the episode. Under its title is the player:

- **Back 15 seconds**, **Play** and **Forward 30 seconds**.
- **Speed**: press it to go from 1× to 1.25×, 1.5× and 2×. Each device
  remembers your speed.
- A slider to move through the episode, with the time played and the time
  left.

While it plays, a bar at the bottom of the app keeps the controls at hand,
so you can go on reading other things. Press the episode's title there to
go back to it, or **Close the player** to stop.

## Pick up where you left off

Your place is saved as you listen, and on your other devices too. When
you're within the last 30 seconds, the episode counts as finished and is
marked read.

## Up Next

To line up episodes, open one and choose **More › Add to Up Next**. The next
one starts by itself when the one playing ends. **Up Next** in the sidebar
lists them; in the player, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove** change
the order.

## Good to know

- The sound comes straight from the podcast's own website. nine turtles
  never downloads or stores it.
- The lock screen and headphone buttons work where your browser allows.
- Episodes need a connection to play.
