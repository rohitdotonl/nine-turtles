<p class="lede">
  An article counts as read as soon as you open it. Anything you mark can be
  put back with Undo.
</p>

## Mark one article unread

Open it, then press <kbd>M</kbd>, or choose **More › Mark unread**. It gets
its dot back and waits in Unread again.

Or, without opening it: right-click it in the list (on a phone, press and
hold it) and choose **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**. The same menu
stars it, writes back, or opens the writer's page.

## Mark a whole list as read

1. Open the list: Unread, a person, a folder or a search.
2. Open the list's **More** menu and choose **Mark all as read**
   (or press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd>). Or, once you've reached the
   end of the list, press **Mark all as read** under "That's everything."

It happens at once, with no question first. Only the articles in the list
you're looking at are marked: anything that arrived since stays unread.

## Undo

After you mark or star something, a note at the bottom of the list says
what happened, with an **Undo** button. Press it, or <kbd>U</kbd>, to put
things back as they were. If you're reading with the list out of sight (an
article on a phone, or the list hidden on a computer), the same note shows
at the bottom of the article instead. With nothing to undo, <kbd>U</kbd>
says "Nothing to undo."

The note goes when you open another article (on a computer; on a phone or
tablet it stays, as Undo is your only way back there), press its **×**, or
leave the list; it never disappears on a timer. Until you leave the list, <kbd>U</kbd>
still undoes, even after the note has gone. Undo works for marking read and unread, starring, unfollowing someone and
deleting a folder.

## Let old articles go

nine turtles shows no unread counts, so a long list isn't a debt. If it
still weighs on you, **Mark all as read** is the one way to set it down:
nothing is deleted, and everything stays in its feed and in search.

## Good to know

- Without a connection, what you mark is kept on your device and sent when
  you're back online.
- If marking fails, the note offers **Try again**. If it failed because
  you've been signed out (on another device, say), it says so and offers
  **Sign in** instead.
