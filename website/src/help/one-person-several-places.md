<p class="lede">
  Many people write in more than one place: a blog, a YouTube channel, a
  newsletter. Each of these is one of their sources. Join them, and they're
  one row in your sidebar, with everything they make in one list.
</p>

## Someone or something

Everyone you follow is either a **person** (someone) or a **publication**
(something, such as a magazine). The sidebar lists them under **People** and
**Publications**. nine turtles makes a guess when you follow them, and asks
"someone or something?" with its guess already chosen. Got it wrong? Change
**This is** in Edit (below).

## Their page

Choose them in the sidebar, then press their name at the top of their list
("Alpha ›"), or choose **More › About Alpha**, or press <kbd>I</kbd>. On a
computer their page opens beside the list; on a phone, as a screen of its
own.

It shows whether they're a person or a publication, their folders, when you
started following them, each of their sources with a link to its own
website, and their **Email**, once nine turtles knows it.

## Edit them

Press **Edit** beside their name (or <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>). There
you can:

- change their **Name**,
- add their **Email address**, for
  <a href="/help/write-back/">writing back</a>,
- choose whether **This is** a person or a publication (while they have one
  source),
- see their **Sources**, and press › beside one for its own settings,
- choose their **Folders**,
- and, at the foot, **Unsubscribe from Alpha**.

Press **Done** to save, or **Cancel** to leave everything as it was.

## Join someone's sources

1. Open their page and press **Edit**.
2. Press **+ Add a source**.
3. Choose someone you already follow, or paste the address of their blog,
   channel or newsletter.

If the two were in different folders, nine turtles asks which folders to
keep. Their list now holds everything, newest first, each article marked
**Blog**, **Video**, **Newsletter** or **Podcast**. Changed your mind?
Press **Undo**.

nine turtles never joins anyone by itself, and never asks: only you do.

## A source's own settings

In Edit, press › beside a source. There you choose its kind, inbox or river,
how often to check, whether articles open on their website, and pausing for
a while. With one source, these settings are in Edit itself.

To split a source off again, press **Take out of Alpha** at the foot. It
becomes a person or publication of its own, with **Undo**.

## Make a folder one person

If you've kept someone's sources together in a folder, choose the folder,
then **More › Edit folder…**, tick **This is one person** and press
**Save**. The folder becomes one person with its name. **Undo** brings the
folder back.

## Good to know

- Joining puts all their sources in the folders you chose.
- Other feed reader apps, such as Reeder, still show each source on its own.
