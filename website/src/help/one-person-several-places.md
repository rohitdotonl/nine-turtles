<p class="lede">
  Many people write in more than one place: a blog, a YouTube channel, a
  newsletter. Each of these is one of their sources. Join them, and they're
  one row in your sidebar, with everything they make in one list.
</p>

## A person or a publication

Everyone you follow is either a **person** (someone you might write back
to) or a **publication** (anything you only read or watch, such as a
magazine). The sidebar lists them under **People** and **Publications**.
nine turtles sorts them from their feed when you follow them: one name on
every article is a person, several names a publication. When the feed
doesn't say who writes it, they **could be either**: they wait with
Publications until you say, in **Everyone you follow**, in their
settings, or at the end of one of their articles ("Is Maya Chen someone you
might write back to?"). Got it wrong? Change **This is** in their settings
(below); the line under it says why they're filed as they are.

## Their page

Choose them in the sidebar, then press their name at the top of their list
("Alpha ›") or their name under one of their articles' titles, or choose
**More › Info about Alpha**, or press <kbd>I</kbd> (in any list, it opens the
page of whoever wrote the chosen article). On a
computer their page opens beside the list; on a phone, as a screen of its
own.

Their page starts with their name and, under it, where they publish, each
place a link to its own website. Under a person's name is **Write to Maya
Chen**, which opens your own email app to write to them, with their address
under it once nine turtles knows it, and **Change** beside the address to
correct it (see <a href="/help/write-back/">Write back to a writer</a>).
Then, in one card, what's yours of them: your note, and the words you
highlighted in their writing. Last comes one row, **Settings**. On a
computer, the **×** at the top right (or <kbd>Esc</kbd>) takes you back to
their list.

## Their settings

Press **Settings** at the foot of their page, or <kbd>E</kbd> in their
list. Their settings open as a page of their own, with **‹** and their
name above the title; press it (or <kbd>Esc</kbd>) to go back to their
page. There's no Edit and no Save: each setting changes the moment you
touch it, as in Settings.

- **Name**: type a new one and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or just move on;
  "Saved." shows under it. <kbd>Esc</kbd> while typing puts the old one
  back.
- **This is**: a person or a publication (while they have one source; a
  publication has no Write to or Write back), with why under it ("nine
  turtles chose this: one name signs every article.", or "Your choice."). For
  someone who could be either, neither is chosen until you choose.
- **Folders**: shows the folders they're in; press it to tick or untick
  any, or type a name under them to make a new folder (it opens in the
  sidebar).
- **Pause for a while**: their new articles arrive already read. While
  they're paused, their list and their page say so, "Paused since 23 Oct.
  Their new articles arrive already read.", with **Resume** to undo it in
  one press.
- **Hide articles about…**: opens Hiding with them chosen; its back button
  brings you back to their settings.
- At the foot, **Unsubscribe from Alpha**. If they were the last one in a
  folder, the folder goes too; **Undo** brings both back.

## Join someone's sources

When you add someone whose site offers several feeds (say, a blog and a
podcast), tick each one you want and press **Follow**: they arrive already
joined, as one person.

To join sources you already follow:

1. Open their page and press **Settings**.
2. Under the settings, press **+ Add their newsletter, videos or podcast**
   (or, for someone with two or more sources already, **+ Add a source**).
3. Choose someone you already follow, or paste the address of their blog,
   channel or newsletter.

Or drag one onto the other in the sidebar: while you hold them over
someone's row, the name you're holding says **One person with …**. Let go
and they're one person, under the name of the one you dropped them on,
in every folder either was in.

If the two were in different folders, + Add a source asks which folders to
keep. Their list now holds everything, newest first, each article marked
**Blog**, **Video**, **Newsletter** or **Podcast**. Changed your mind?
Press **Undo**.

nine turtles never joins anyone by itself, and never asks: only you do.

## Their sources

For someone with two or more sources, their settings end with **Sources**,
each by its name and what it is. A YouTube channel has **Include Shorts**
and **Include live streams** under it (for a channel on its own, they're
in its settings).

To split a source off again, press **Take out** on its row. It becomes a
person or publication of its own, with **Undo**.

## Make a folder one person

If you've kept someone's sources together in a folder, choose the folder,
then **More › Edit folder…**, tick **This is one person** and press
**Save**. The folder becomes one person with its name. **Undo** brings the
folder back.

## Good to know

- Joining puts all their sources in the folders you chose.
