<p class="lede">
  Many people write in more than one place: a blog, a YouTube channel, a
  newsletter. Join them, and they're one row in your sidebar, with
  everything they make in one list.
</p>

## Join someone's places

1. Choose the person in the sidebar.
2. On a computer, their page shows beside the list. Press **Add another
   place**. (On a phone, choose **More › Edit person…**, then **Add another
   place**.)
3. Either choose them from **Someone you already follow** and press
   **Join**, or paste the address of their blog, channel or newsletter and
   press **Follow and join**.

Their list now holds everything, newest first, each article marked
**Blog**, **Video**, **Newsletter** or **Podcast**. Changed your mind?
Press **Undo**.

## "Is this the same person?"

nine turtles never joins anyone by itself. When two things you follow are on
the same personal website, their page may ask once: "Is … the same person
as …?" Press **Yes, they're one person** or **No**. After No it never asks
about them again.

## Edit a person

Choose them, then **More › Edit person…** (or press <kbd>Shift</kbd>
<kbd>E</kbd>). There you can:

- change their **Name**,
- add their **Email address**, for
  <a href="/help/write-back/">writing back</a>,
- see their **Places**, and split one off with **… isn't the same person**,
- choose their **Folders**,
- and change the **Settings** for each place: inbox or river, how often to
  check, whether articles open on their website, and pausing for a while.

Press **Save** when you're done.

## Good to know

- Joining puts all their places in every folder any of them was in.
- Other feed reader apps, such as Reeder, still show each place on its own.
