<p class="lede">
  Saving keeps an article. Saved articles stay for good, even if you stop
  following the person who wrote them.
</p>

## Save an article

Open the article and press **Save** (the bookmark) in the bar at its top or
under its last words (on a phone, the bar at the bottom), or press <kbd>S</kbd>.
The bookmark fills in. Press it again to take the article out of Saved. In a
list, you can also right-click an article (on a phone, press and hold it, or
swipe it all the way left) and choose **Save**. On a computer, you can also
drag an article from the list onto **Saved** in the sidebar.

## Find your saved articles

Choose **Saved** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>S</kbd>.

## Good to know

- Took an article out of Saved by mistake? Press <kbd>U</kbd> or **Undo**.
- If you've been signed out (on another device, say), an article can't be
  saved: the note says so, with **Sign in**.
- Saved is the one place to keep articles. Until November 2026 it was called
  Starred, and its old address still opens it.
- Saved articles can be read without a connection: each device keeps the
  newest 200.
