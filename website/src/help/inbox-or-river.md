<p class="lede">
  Some people like to read everything; others like to dip in. nine turtles
  has a way for each, and you can mix them.
</p>

## Inbox (the usual way)

Every new article waits in **Unread** with a dot beside it, until you've read
it. Good for the people you never want to miss.

## River

There's no Unread and no dots. The app opens on **All**, and every title
looks the same. You read what catches your eye, and nothing piles up. Good
for busy sites you only skim.

## Choose one for everything

1. Open **Settings** and find **Reading**.
2. Under **Reading mode**, choose **Inbox** or **River**.

## Choose one for one person

You can keep the inbox for most people and let a busy one flow past.

1. Choose the person in the sidebar, and press their name at the top of
   their list to open their page.
2. Press **Edit** (or press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>). If they have
   several sources, press › beside the one you'd like.
3. Under **Reading mode**, choose **River**, then press **Done**.

Their articles then skip Unread and never get a dot. You still find them in
their own list, in All, in their folders and in search.

## Good to know

- nine turtles still remembers what you've read while you're in the river,
  so nothing is lost when you switch back to the inbox.
- In the river, **Mark unread** (<kbd>M</kbd>) still works, but marking a
  whole list as read is left out: there's nothing to clear.
