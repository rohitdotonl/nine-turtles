## Connect Claude

1. In nine turtles, open **Settings › Connected assistants** and press
   **Copy** beside **Server address**.
2. In Claude, open **Settings › Connectors**, choose **Add custom
   connector**, and paste the address.
3. Claude opens a nine turtles page that lists what it will and won't be able
   to do. Press **Connect**.

ChatGPT works the same way, as a custom connector in its developer mode.

## What it can and can't do

It can read what you follow, your articles, stars and rules; mark articles
read or starred; and follow, rename, move and unfollow, and make folders and
rules.

It can't see your password or email, see or change your plan, or change or
delete your account. Reading an article through it doesn't mark it read.

## Disconnect

In **Settings › Connected assistants**, press **Disconnect** beside it. It
stops at once. Pressed it by mistake? **Undo**, on the line that appears,
brings it straight back, with no need to connect it again.

## Good to know

- If an assistant unfollows someone, they're kept for 30 days, listed under
  **Recently unsubscribed** in **Everyone you follow** with
  **Bring back**, as your own unfollows are.
- For an assistant that can't open the Connect page, press **Make an
  assistant key** and give it that key instead. It's shown once.
