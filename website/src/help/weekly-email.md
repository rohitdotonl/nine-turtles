## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings › Account**.
2. Under **Weekly email**, choose **Weekly** (Saturday mornings) or
   **Twice a week** (Wednesday and Saturday mornings).

It arrives soon after 8 in the morning, your time. It's called **New from
the people you read**.

## What's in it

Who wrote, A to Z, with up to three titles each and the first line of
each, linked to the writer's own page. Only articles you haven't read, and
never anything hidden. When nothing's new, nothing is sent. No numbers, no
pictures, and nothing to track whether you opened it.

## Stop it

Press the link at the bottom of any of the emails, then **Stop the weekly
email**. Or choose **Off** in **Settings › Account**. No more will come.

## Good to know

- It goes only to a confirmed email address. If Settings says yours isn't
  confirmed, sign in once with **Forgot?** on the sign-in page, which
  confirms it.
- Moved to another time zone? Choose Weekly or Twice a week again, so it
  comes at 8 where you are now.
