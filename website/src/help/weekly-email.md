<p class="lede">
  nine turtles never sends notifications. The one exception is an email you
  choose: once a week, the people you read, their titles and a line of
  each, in plain text.
</p>

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings › Account**.
2. Switch on **Weekly email**.

It arrives on Saturday, soon after 8 in the morning, your time. It's called **New from
the people you read**.

## What's in it

Who wrote, A to Z, with up to three titles each and the first line of
each, linked to the writer's own page. Only articles you haven't read, and
never anything hidden. When nothing's new, nothing is sent. No numbers, no
pictures, and nothing to track whether you opened it.

## Stop it

Press the link at the bottom of any of the emails, then **Stop the weekly
email**. Or switch it off in **Settings › Account**. No more will come.

## Good to know

- It goes only to a confirmed email address. If Settings says yours isn't
  confirmed, sign in once with **Forgot?** on the sign-in page, which
  confirms it.
- Moved to another time zone? Switch it off and on again, so it comes at
  8 where you are now.
- It used to come twice a week too. If you'd chosen that, it now comes on
  Saturdays, and your next one says so. If you miss it, write to
  hello@nineturtles.app.
