<p class="lede">
  Some people like to read everything; others like to dip in. One switch
  decides which kind of reader nine turtles is for you.
</p>

## Unread on (the usual way)

Every new article waits in **Unread** with a dot beside it, until you've read
it. While anything is new, **Unread** in the sidebar has a small dot too.

## Unread off

There's no Unread list and no dots. The app opens on **All articles**, and every
title looks the same. You read what catches your eye, and nothing piles up.

## Switch it

1. Open **Settings** and find **Reading**.
2. Switch **Unread** on or off.

It applies on all your devices.

## Good to know

- nine turtles still remembers what you've read while Unread is off, so
  nothing is lost when you switch it back on.
- With Unread off, **Mark unread** (<kbd>M</kbd>) still works, but marking a
  whole list as read is left out: there's nothing to clear.
- The switch is for everything at once. To see less from one busy person,
  [hide the topics](/help/hide-topics/) you don't want, or pause them in
  their settings (**Settings**, at the foot of their page).
