<p class="lede">
  A folder is a group of people and publications: Friends, Cooking, Read at the weekend.
  Someone can be in more than one. In the sidebar, the people in a folder are
  set a step in under it; everyone in no folder lines up with the folders.
</p>

## Choose their folder as you follow them

When you add someone with **+ Add**, nine turtles asks what they are, and
under it, their **Folder**. Choose one, or **New folder…** to make one with
them in it, and press **Done**. If you pressed + Add while you were in a
folder, it starts on that folder. The folder opens in the sidebar, so you see
where they went.

## Drag someone into a folder

In the sidebar, drag a person or publication onto a folder. They move there,
out of the folder you dragged them from.

- To keep them in both folders, hold **Option** on a Mac (**Ctrl** on
  Windows and Linux) as you let go.
- To take someone out of a folder, drag them onto the heading **People**
  or **Publications**. Let go on someone's name instead and the two become
  one person (the name you're holding says **One person with …** first;
  see <a href="/help/one-person-several-places/">One person, several
  places</a>). Undo puts them back.
- On a phone or tablet, press and hold their name until it lifts, then move
  it. A quick swipe still scrolls.
- While you hold someone, the bottom of the sidebar offers **Pause** and
  **Unsubscribe** to drop them on (see
  <a href="/help/unfollow-someone/">Unfollow someone</a>).

Each move is said at the bottom of the sidebar, with **Undo**.

## Put your folders in order

Drag a folder up or down in the sidebar: a line shows where it will land.
Your order is the same on every device. A new folder goes at the bottom, and
renaming one keeps its place. People and publications are always A to Z.

**Without dragging:** press **Edit** beside **Folders** (with a mouse it
shows when the pointer is on the sidebar). Each row gets a handle at its end.
Press a folder's handle and choose **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to the
top** or **Move to the bottom**; press a person's to choose their folder.
Press **Done** when you've finished. This works with the keyboard and screen
readers too.

## Put one person in a folder from their page

1. Choose the person in the sidebar, and press their name at the top of
   their list to open their page.
2. In their settings at the foot of the page, press **Folders**.
3. Tick the folders they belong in; each one counts at once. To make a new
   one, type its name in **New folder…** and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

There's no "new folder" button: a folder is made when you put someone in it,
as you follow them, from their page, or with **Move to…**.

## Move many at once

1. Open **Settings › Everyone you follow** and press **Choose…**.
2. Tick everyone you'd like to move.
3. Press **Move to…**, choose a folder (or **New folder**, or **No folder**),
   and press **Move**.

Moving takes them out of the folders they were in. **Undo** puts them back.

## Rename or delete a folder

Choose the folder, then **More › Edit folder…** Change its **Name** and
press **Save**, or press **Delete**. Close it any other way and your
changes wait there, marked **Not saved yet.**, until you press Save or
**Cancel**.

Deleting a folder never unfollows anyone: they simply leave the folder.
**Undo** brings it back as it was.

## Good to know

- Folders start closed in the sidebar. Press a folder's arrow to show the
  people in it; each device remembers which you opened. A folder you just
  made or chose on their page opens, so you can see them in it.
- Unsubscribing from the last person in a folder, or moving them out of it
  in the sidebar, removes the folder too; **Undo** brings it back, and so
  does **Bring back** under Recently unsubscribed, for 30 days.
- Someone in a folder is listed in the folder, not under **People** or
  **Publications**, which hold everyone who isn't in one.
- A folder can hold people and publications alike.
