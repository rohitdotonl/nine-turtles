<p class="lede">
  A folder is a group of people and publications: Friends, Cooking, Read at the weekend.
  Someone can be in more than one.
</p>

## Put one person in a folder

1. Choose the person in the sidebar, and press their name at the top of
   their list to open their page.
2. Press **Edit**.
3. Under **Folders**, tick the folders they belong in. To make a new one,
   type its name in **New folder…**
4. Press **Done**.

There's no "new folder" button in the sidebar: a folder is made when you put
someone in it.

## Move many at once

1. Open **Settings › Your feeds** and press **Edit**.
2. Tick everyone you'd like to move.
3. Press **Move to…**, choose a folder (or **New folder**, or **No folder**),
   and press **Move**.

Moving takes them out of the folders they were in. **Undo** puts them back.

## Rename or delete a folder

Choose the folder, then **More › Edit folder…** Change its **Name** and
press **Save**, or press **Delete**.

Deleting a folder never unfollows anyone: they simply leave the folder.
**Undo** brings it back as it was.

## Good to know

- Folders start closed in the sidebar. Press a folder's arrow to show the
  people in it; each device remembers which you opened. A folder you just
  made or chose in Edit opens, so you can see them in it.
- Unsubscribing from the last person in a folder removes the folder too;
  **Undo** brings both back.
- Someone in a folder is listed in the folder, not under **People** or
  **Publications**, which hold everyone who isn't in one.
- A folder can hold people and publications alike.
