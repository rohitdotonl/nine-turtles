<p class="lede">
  A collection is a named shelf for articles you want to keep together:
  Recipes, Long reads, Things to try. An article can be on more than one.
</p>

## Add an article to a collection

1. Open the article.
2. Choose **More › Add to collection…** (or press <kbd>L</kbd>).
3. Tick the collections it belongs on. To start a new one, type its name in
   **New collection…**
4. Press **Save**.

Your collections are listed in the sidebar under **Collections**, A to Z.

## Take an article off

Open the article, choose **Add to collection…** again, untick the collection
and press **Save**. A note says what was taken off, with **Undo**.

## Rename or delete a collection

Open the collection, then its **More** menu: **Rename…** or **Delete**.
Deleting a collection never deletes its articles, and **Undo** brings the
collection back.

## Good to know

- Articles on a collection are kept for good, even if you stop following the
  person who wrote them.
- To search only one collection, type <code>in:recipes</code> (its name) in
  the search field.
- Collections need a connection to open.
- You can download your collections: see
  <a href="/help/download-everything/">Download everything you've saved</a>.
