## What you get

- A calm reader for the blogs, newsletters and sites you choose to follow,
  through their RSS, Atom or JSON feeds.
- The app on the web, installable on your phone, tablet and computer.
- Search, stars, folders, reading settings and exports of everything you
  put in (see the [tour](/tour/)).
- No ads, no tracking, and nothing sold or shared. More in the
  [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## How paying works

Payments are handled by **Dodo Payments**, which sells nine turtles on our
behalf as its "merchant of record". That means Dodo Payments takes the
payment, adds and pays any sales tax (like VAT or GST) where you live, and
sends your receipt. Your card details go to Dodo Payments, never to us.

Your plan renews automatically at the end of each month or year until you
cancel. You can cancel yourself at any time, in the app, without having to
ask anyone. How cancelling and refunds work is on the
[refunds and cancelling](/refunds/) page.
