# A quiet reader for the open web

nine turtles gathers the blogs and sites you follow through
their feeds (RSS, Atom and JSON Feed) and sets them out like the pages of a
book. No notifications, no badges, no endless scrolling. You read what you
came for, and close it.

## What it does

- **Reads like a book.** Calm type, generous margins and themes for day and
  night.
- **Ends.** Your unread list is finite. When it's empty, you're done.
- **Respects publishers.** It shows what each feed shares, always credits
  and links the original, and checks feeds politely.
- **Stays yours.** Your feeds come in and go out as a standard OPML file,
  and your starred articles export whenever you like.
- **Works everywhere.** On a computer, a tablet or a phone, and it installs
  to your home screen.
