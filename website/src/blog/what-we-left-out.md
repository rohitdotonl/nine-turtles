---
title: What we left out, on purpose
description: No notifications, no infinite scroll, no recommendations. The features nine turtles will never have, and why.
date: 2026-10-25T10:00:00Z
---

Most software grows by adding things. A lot of what makes nine turtles
what it is comes from the things it doesn't do, and won't.

## No notifications

Not for new articles, not for anything. A notification is someone else
deciding that now is the moment you should be thinking about them. nine
turtles never tries to bring you back. It's there when you open it, and
quiet when you don't.

## No endless scrolling

At the bottom of your list, the list ends. If you want older articles,
there's a row that says **Show older items**, and it loads them only when
you press it. Infinite scroll is the single best tool ever invented for
keeping people looking at a screen longer than they meant to. We'd rather
you didn't.

## No recommendations

There's no "trending", no "for you", no "readers also liked". You choose
every source, and articles arrive in the order they were published.
Nothing jumps the queue because it's loud.

## No likes, comments or followers

nine turtles isn't social. Nobody sees what you read, and there's no
number anywhere to make you feel behind. Sharing an article shares the
publisher's own link, never ours: Copy link on a computer, your phone's
usual share sheet on a phone.

## No badges and no stats

No streaks, no "articles read this week", no year in review. Reading
isn't a score. The one number we keep is the count of unread articles
beside each feed, in a muted grey, because people with hundreds of feeds
need to know what they've seen. If you'd rather not see even that, **river mode** takes it
away, along with the unread list, and lets everything simply flow past.

## No motion

Nothing on screen animates. Views switch instantly, and nothing moves
unless you moved it. Animation makes an interface feel slower, and it
makes some people feel unwell.

## No scraping

Some feeds carry only a summary, hoping you'll visit the site for the
rest. Plenty of readers fetch the full page anyway. nine turtles doesn't:
it shows what the publisher chose to share and links you to the rest. The
people who write are the reason there's anything to read.

---

Each of these is written into nine turtles' design rules, above any
feature anyone might want to add. When a new idea comes along, it has to
get past them first.
