# nine turtles: the brand sheet

*Every brand decision on one page. Last updated: 3 November 2026 (decisions 0141, 0148, 0153).*

**How to use this page.** Every change a reader can see (in the app, on the
website, in an email, in a link preview) follows this page. This page is
part of [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md) and has its standing: DESIGN.md Part 1 (the
floors and the principles) wins over it, and it wins over everything else
about the brand. Each line says one decision; the measurements behind it
live where the decision is built (mostly DESIGN.md Part 2), and this page
links there instead of copying them.

**Changing it.** Something new for the brand (a color, a typeface, a drawing,
a new use of the turtles, a new line of marketing copy) is added here first,
with Rohit's yes and the reason, before it's built. Never as a one-off.

**Live** means the app or website shows it today. **Being built** means Rohit
has decided it and the work is under way: build towards it, and don't build
anything that goes against it. Until it lands, DESIGN.md Part 2 describes
what's live.

## Name and words

- **The name** is *nine turtles*, always lowercase, even at the start of a sentence. At nineturtles.app. (Live)
- **The headline** is "Read people. Write back." (Live: the website's front page, the sign-in pages and the share picture.)
- **The first line under it:** "There are people online you'd like to know. Most of them have a blog, a newsletter or a channel." The rest of the paragraph and the website's points are in [`RnD/positioning.md`](RnD/positioning.md), used word for word. (Live on the website's front page; its six points are there as a heading and one line each.)
- **Who speaks:** "I" when Rohit speaks (website, emails, About, the 404 page). Never "we". The app itself speaks without a pronoun. Details: DESIGN.md → "Voice". (Live)
- **How it sounds:** warm, never cute. True, specific facts, never invented feelings. No stories or metaphors (letters, fountain pens) told about the brand: it wins on craft. (Live)
- **Marketing copy** isn't written fresh by an agent: use settled copy word for word, and ask Rohit before adding any. (Live)

## Typefaces (Live, decision 0141)

- **Literata** for everything written: reading, the article list's titles, page titles and the wordmark (weight 600).
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** (400, 500, 600) for the whole interface, on every device. It replaced the device's own font and Inter.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono** for code in articles. It replaced JetBrains Mono.
- The reader can still pick another reading font in the Aa panel: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next or the device's own serif.
- All served by nine turtles itself, never by a font service.

## Color

- **Four themes**, each with a day and a night version: **Original**, **Plain**, **Fountain pen** (the warm one) and **Signal**. Readers on Brushed move to Original; readers on Anodised or Heather move to Fountain pen. (Live, decision 0145.)
- **Each theme has one color, its accent:** the unread dot (beside an article, and a smaller one at the end of a sidebar row with something new, decision 0148) and the faint tone behind a search match, nothing else. Never a button, a link or a focus outline. (Live)

  | Theme | Accent by day | Accent by night |
  |---|---|---|
  | Original | ink blue `#2C4F8C` | `#94B2E6` |
  | Plain | the text color | the text color |
  | Fountain pen | old-ink brown `#8C5500` | `#DCAA6A` |
  | Signal | violet `#6953A0` | `#B8A8EE` |

  (Live: Fountain pen's brown and Signal's violet replaced burnt orange and indigo on 3 November 2026.)

- **The dot is never green or red** (green and red are the colors most often confused). (Live)
- **The star** is drawn in the theme's own ink: filled when starred, an outline when not. Green retired. (Live, decision 0143)
- **Delete and errors** are oxblood, `#8C2F39` by day and `#E39AA4` by night, always with words that say what. (Live, decision 0143)
- **No brand color outside the app.** The website, icons and share picture use ink only: near-black `#141414` and light `#EFEFF1`. The reader's theme color is theirs, not the brand's. (Live)
- **No more colors.** A new feature uses ink strengths, the greys, the one accent and oxblood. Tags and folders get names, never colors. (Live)

## The logo

- **The drawing:** nine small turtles seen from the side, in three rows of three, the middle row a little to the right. One ink, the page's text color, never colored, never cut off, never moving. (Live)
- **The redrawn nine (D2)** is the logo wherever the name is: heavier, so the turtles stay turtles at small sizes. Beside the name it sits on the name's baseline, the name in Literata 600 (in the sidebar: the nine 27px tall, the name 22px, 9px apart, the most that fits on one line beside the sidebar button; the mockup's 28 and 23 wrapped the name). All nine turtles stay in the sidebar. (Live)
- **A single turtle** appears only in the browser-tab icon. (Live)
- **The app icon:** a black tile `#141414` with the nine in light `#EFEFF1`, the same by day and by night. (Live)
- **The tab icon:** the app icon's black square, always, with one light turtle filling most of it, so it shows on any tab, light or dark. (Live)
- **The share picture** (the preview when someone shares a nineturtles.app link): a black tile 1200 by 630 with the nine (132px tall), "Read people. Write back." in Literata 600 in light ink, and "nineturtles.app" small in grey. Its description for screen readers: "nine turtles: Read people. Write back." (Live)

## The turtles' only job

The turtle is the logo's drawing, nothing else. It never acts, speaks,
sleeps, celebrates or stands for a feeling. No mascot.

- **May appear:** the logo (sidebar, sign-in, the website's band, app icon, tab icon), the share picture, and the two drawings that already exist, unchanged: "Wrong turn?" (the 404 page) and the end of the About page.
- **Never:** empty lists, the end of a list, the reading pane, emails, buttons, loading, errors, confirmations, settings, or any new drawing that tells a story.
- **When it appears:** the logo's own drawing, at most once per screen, still, in the page's ink, and skipped by screen readers, because the words beside it say it.

## Icons (Live)

- **A thin line everywhere** (1.5px on a 20px grid): today's set, tidied.
- **One solid part only where the real object has one:** the palette (Appearance), the heart with a pulse (Feed health), the bookmark (Add from anywhere), Newsletters, Readers, the shelf (To read slowly), and the headphones (Field recordings). The card (Plan) stays line only, with its chip and number drawn in.
- **Small dots stay solid** where a line would be too thin to see: the unread dot, More's three dots, the feed's dot, the alert's dot, the keyboard's keys and Up Next's play mark. These are part of the locked set, not a breach of the rule above.
- **Never solid on plain signs** (close, back, search, plus, check and the like).
- "Open original" is an arrow with no box. AI assistants keep the robot.
- A new icon follows the same rule. If Rohit dislikes one later, offer alternatives one at a time.

## Empty screens and the ends of lists (Live, decision 0142)

- **One shape for every empty list:** on the list's left edge, never centred. First the fact in Literata, in the second ink ("Nothing in this folder yet."). Then, only if there's something to do, how to do it in Atkinson, muted ("Add an article from its More menu."). A button only where one action is the obvious next step. No picture, no turtle, no icon.
- **One pattern for the words:** "Nothing … yet." A feed says when it was last checked; a search says where it looked ("Nothing found for "walking" in Starred.").
- **The end of a list:** Unread ends "That's everything for now."; every other list "That's everything."; search "No more results."
- **The reading pane with nothing open:** one quiet line in Literata, at 40% of the pane's height.
- **The welcome:** "Hello." then "Who would you like to read?"
- **"Wrong turn?"** (the 404 page): everything on one left edge, "404 error" in sentence case, the turtle drawing unchanged.
- Exact sizes: `spec/06-item-list.md` for lists, DESIGN.md Part 2 for "Wrong turn?".

## Emails (Live, decision 0142)

- **Plain text,** no pictures and no logo: the name is text.
- **From** "nine turtles <hello@nineturtles.app>"; replies reach Rohit.
- **Signed** on two lines: "Rohit", then "nine turtles".
- The "your email was changed" note ends: "Reply to this email and I'll help you get your account back."

## The website

- **Look:** the same as the app: ink only, Literata and Atkinson, one left edge, generous space. The app's grey sidebar band on every page (the direction Rohit chose, "G set as C"), with the logo beside the name at the app sidebar's sizes; a grey bar across the top on a tablet or phone. One text size for the front page (20px). (Live, decision 0153; sizes in DESIGN.md Part 2.)
- **Copy:** the settled positioning copy, word for word (above, under "Name and words").
