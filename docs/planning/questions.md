## Still open

- **Unread titles under a name in Unread: semibold or regular?** (14 November
  2026, decision 0271). In Unread by person, each title under a name is
  semibold while unread, as built and as the written rule in DESIGN.md's
  Exceptions log says (the unread dot and a semibold title). The picture you
  approved showed them regular. The names are semibold either way, and the
  dot still marks what's unread without color alone. Keep semibold, or make
  titles under a name regular? Not yet answered.

- **Read one thing at a time on a computer?** (14 November 2026, decision
  0269). A trial switch in Settings (yours only, on the device you switch it
  on) shows the list alone, then the article alone in the whole window, with
  "‹ Unread" or Escape to go back, as a phone reads. Live with it, then
  choose: keep three panes (the switch goes), or make one thing at a time
  the way nine turtles reads on a computer (it changes DESIGN.md's "What's
  fixed" and replaces full-screen reading, `f`).

- **Finishing the website's privacy policy and terms** (25 October 2026).
  They're on nineturtles.app as drafts, marked "to be decided" where they
  wait on: the prices, the email service for sign-in and receipts,
  where Render keeps the data, the business details and the law that
  applies, the minimum age, and how long backups keep deleted data. Sign-up
  is now open to anyone (decisions 0115 and 0154), so someone qualified should read
  both soon.

- **A warning for assistants that send you back to your own computer**
  (11 November 2026, decision 0257). The MCP standard suggests the
  "Connect this assistant?" page warn more when an assistant's only return
  addresses are on the reader's own computer (`localhost`), since anyone
  can claim those; desktop assistants do this. Today the page names the
  website the reader goes back to, as it does for every assistant. A
  stronger warning would change that screen: yours to choose, whenever.

- **Payments, built in test mode** (26 October 2026, decision 0094). Each

  - **The price**, monthly and yearly (`NINE_TURTLES_PRICE_*`, and the
    products in Dodo). Today: "Price to be decided". Decided so far (1
    November 2026): in dollars, the yearly price shown first, Dodo's
    automatic regional (PPP) prices, no supporter price. The amounts are

  - The trial's length and what ends with a plan were answered on 1

  - **Readers from before payments** (your testers) stay free for good.
  - **The refund window** on the website's refunds page.
