# Applying to Dodo Payments: the checklist

*Written 25 October 2026. The owner will delete this file before applying,
so it isn't on the public repository while Dodo reviews nine turtles.*

nine turtles will take payments through **Dodo Payments**, a "merchant of
record": a company that sells nine turtles on the owner's behalf, takes the
payment, pays sales tax (VAT, GST) in each country, handles refunds and
disputes, and pays out to the owner's bank. The owner signs up as an
**Individual**; no registered company is needed. Why Dodo and not Paddle,
Polar, Lemon Squeezy, Stripe or Razorpay: decision 0086 and the
[comparison](https://claude.ai/artifact/ALb8Z7n4zdDv6JpGkdx5p4).

## When to apply

Only when the app and the website are finished and the owner is happy with
them. Dodo reviews the live website, and turns down "coming soon" pages and
waiting lists. Until then, payments are built and tested in Dodo's **test
mode**, which needs no approval and uses no real money.

## 1. The website must show these (Dodo checks each one)

A visitor without an account must be able to find, from nineturtles.app:

- [ ] **Pricing**: the real price ([/pricing/](https://nineturtles.app/pricing/)).
- [ ] **Terms of service**, with the owner's name ([/terms/](https://nineturtles.app/terms/)).
- [ ] **Privacy policy** ([/privacy/](https://nineturtles.app/privacy/)).
- [ ] **Refund and cancellation policy** ([/refunds/](https://nineturtles.app/refunds/)).
- [ ] **A contact address the owner reads**: hello@nineturtles.app ([/contact/](https://nineturtles.app/contact/)).
- [ ] No "Draft" notes left on any of these pages, and no "to be decided".
- [ ] A way to actually subscribe and use the app on the day of applying
      (the sign-up switch set to Open, or at least Invite link with a
      working checkout).

## 2. The application form

- [ ] Choose **Individual** (a creator or sole proprietor with no company
      registration). Choose "Registered Entity" only if the owner later
      gets a GSTIN.
- [ ] The product name, description, price and delivery method in the form
      match the website **exactly**. Mismatches are a common reason for
      rejection.
- [ ] Describe it plainly as software delivered online, never as a service.
      Suggested wording: *"nine turtles is a calm reading app, delivered
      online, for the blogs and newsletters you choose to follow. It shows
      only what each website publishes in its public feed (RSS, Atom, JSON
      Feed), always credits and links to the original, and never copies or
      scrapes websites. One subscription, no ads, no tracking."*
      (Dodo bans scraping tools and pirated content; this rules both out.)
- [ ] Optional: email compliance@dodopayments.com with that description
      before applying, and ask whether it fits.

## 3. Identity and bank (the owner's part)

- [ ] The **original** government photo ID, not a photocopy or screenshot.
- [ ] A selfie (liveness check, done in the browser).
- [ ] **PAN card**: Indian individuals need one.
- [ ] A **personal** bank account in exactly the same name as the ID.
- [ ] Payouts come in US dollars (or pounds or euros), twice a month by
      default, minimum $50 to $100; the bank converts to rupees. Payouts
      under $1,000 cost $5.

## 4. During the review (usually 1 to 3 working days)

- [ ] Reply quickly and fully to every email from Dodo's compliance team.
      "On hold" means they want more information, not a no. Not replying is
      a common reason for refusal.
- [ ] If rejected, read the reason in the email or the dashboard, fix it,
      and only then appeal: **there is one appeal, and its answer is final.**
- [ ] Keep copies of the approval email and every message with compliance.

## 5. Staying approved (from founders' reports)

- [ ] **One account, one product.** Never take payments for anything else
      through this account; several founders were suspended for that.
- [ ] **Never test with a real card in live mode.** Dodo suspends accounts
      for "self-test" payments. Test in test mode; for one real check, use a
      100% discount code.
- [ ] Finish the technical setup, including the return address buyers land
      on after paying (one account was closed partly for missing it).
- [ ] Avoid dispute triggers: cancelling is easy and self-serve, refunds on
      request, steady prices. Freezes often followed a sudden large payment
      or a few disputes.
- [ ] Keep payouts frequent, so little money ever sits with Dodo.
- [ ] Keep nine turtles' own record of subscribers, so moving to another
      company stays possible (Dodo also offers a card export).
