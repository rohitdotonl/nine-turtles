# 0314: Faster on a slow phone connection

**In one line:** After the speed check of 18 November 2026, opening nine turtles asks the server for its first screen all at once, a list fetched in the last minute and an article whose whole text is kept on the device open without waiting for the server, fonts carry a content label and come from the service worker's copy at once, Settings and other rarely used pages are files of their own fetched after the first screen, the offline copies start once that screen is up, and the scroll edge no longer forces an extra layout.

*19 November 2026. From the speed report (a cloud copy of the live version with 300 feeds and 45,000 articles, timed on a phone-sized Chrome with its processor slowed four times and Chrome's slow 4G). The owner asked for every fix as suggested ("Fix everything as per your suggestion").*

**Why.** The server answers every list in under 15 ms; the time a reader waits on a slow connection is round trips. Each tap waited about 0.7 s for the server, even for an article already kept on the device, and opening asked three questions one after another (who's signed in, then settings and feeds, then the list).

**What changes.**
1. **One round of questions when opening** (`web/src/lib/early.ts`). Before anything is drawn, `main.ts` asks for `/api/session`, `/api/settings`, `/api/feeds` and, once this device knows the reader's sort order (kept beside Unread on or off, `reading.ts`), the first page of the list the address opens. Each screen asks as before; `api` (updates.svelte.ts) hands it the early answer instead, once, within 10 seconds, and never after the server says nobody is signed in.
2. **Lists just fetched** (`recent.ts`). The first page of each list fetched in the last minute (Unread and Saved are fetched as the app opens, for the offline copies) shows again at once. Any change the reader makes (a mark, a setting, a follow), sent or still waiting on the device to be sent, forgets them all and any early answer not yet used (`changed` in updates.svelte.ts), and a page asked for before such a change is never kept after it. Refresh always asks the server.
3. **Articles kept on the device** (`articles.ts`). An article whose kept copy is its whole text (no pictures, which copies leave out, section 32) opens from that copy at once; the server's answer, with any highlight made on another device, replaces it quietly when it comes. A copy with pictures waits for the server as before, so a picture never appears late in the text.
4. **Fonts** (`server/src/assets.rs`, `web/public/sw.js`). The page and the fonts carry an `ETag` from their content, so checking them costs a few bytes ("unchanged", 304) instead of the whole file; a weak label (`W/`, as Cloudflare passes it on after compressing) counts too. The service worker gives a kept font at once and checks the server behind it, so text never waits for a font or changes face as it arrives; a new font shows from the next opening.
5. **Pages in files of their own** (`laterPages.svelte.ts`, `later.ts`). Settings and its pages, Everyone you follow, Muted, Readers, a folder's edit sheet and the keys sheet are fetched once the first screen is up (or at once when the address opens one), and each shows when it's here; until then its place says "Loading Settings…" after half a second, or, if its file can't be fetched, when it will open (`PageWait.svelte`), and the keyboard's place waits for its heading (`landing.ts`). The service worker keeps them with the app, reading the main file for their names. The first screen's code went from 198 KB to 177 KB (compressed), and a release that doesn't touch Settings no longer makes phones download it again.
6. **The offline copies wait for the first screen** (`Shell.svelte`): they start when the browser is next idle, at most 3 seconds after the settings arrive, so they never hold up the reader's first taps.
7. **The scroll edge** (`edge.ts`) is checked just before the next frame instead of while a screen is being built, which made the browser lay the page out an extra time.

**Measured** (cheap phone on slow 4G, before → after, middle of 3 runs): reopening 2.7 s → 1.9 s; opening Unread or Saved 0.8 s → 0.13–0.19 s; opening an article kept on the device 0.65 s → 0.16 s; data each reopening 69 KB → 14 KB. A list or an article not just fetched and not kept still waits one round trip (about 0.7 s on slow 4G).

**Considered and not taken.** Showing kept copies of every list at once, then the server's: rows would change under the reader's eyes, which the app never does (SPEC section 13). Using a kept copy with pictures and adding them later: the text would jump as they arrived. Fetching the hidden pages' code only when they're opened: Settings would then fail to open offline on a device that had never opened it.

**Revisit if** the first screen's own work on a cheap phone becomes the main wait: drawing 300 people on Home takes about 0.6 s of such a phone's time, and drawing them in steps would cut it; or a reader sees a list that's out of date after a change made in another tab (each tab forgets only its own changes); or publishers editing posts makes text change under readers' eyes: a kept copy's words are those of when it was kept (the copies are brought up to date for read, saved and muted only), so an edited article opens as it was and the server's version replaces it a moment later.
