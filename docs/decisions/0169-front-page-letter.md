# 0169: The front page is Rohit's letter

*3 November 2026. Step 4b of the owner's review plan. Rohit chose the bold
front page in the owner's review of the website, and handed copy, visuals and
rules to the owner's critique thread, which approved this wording and the
page's words on 3 November.*

**Why.** The front page whispered its one idea: a headline at the paragraph's
size, six equal points, and nothing about why nine turtles exists. Rohit's
story is the one thing nobody else can copy, and a page that is itself a
letter you can answer shows what nine turtles is about before anyone signs up.

**Decision.**

- **The front page is a signed letter from Rohit:** "Hello,", his story, what
  nine turtles is (one line set large), a picture of reading, highlighting
  and writing back, how it will be paid for, signed "Rohit", then "Start reading" and
  "P.S. Write back to me". The words are a draft he edits himself.
- **For this page only,** decision 0153's one 20px text size and the grey
  band go: the page has the name and the website's places along its top, one
  centred column 640px wide, and its own sizes. 0153 stays for every other
  page. "On this page" goes with the band.
- **The brand sheet's "no stories or metaphors (letters, fountain pens)"**
  stays, with one exception: this page is Rohit's own letter, his true story
  in his voice. Nothing else takes a letter's form.
- **Two honesty checks.** The picture shows only what Write back does today
  (SPEC section 37): the "Write back to Asha" button with her address under
  it opens the reader's own email app, addressed and with "Re:" and the
  title; nine turtles never sends. The highlight is drawn as the app draws
  it. The price isn't set, so the page says so, with no trial, and the
  button is "Start reading"; when Rohit sets a price and a trial, the
  paragraph and button change with it.
- **"Read people. Write back."** stays the headline on the sign-in pages and
  the share picture; it's no longer on the front page. The app's screenshots
  go with the old page.
