# 0221: Indent means inside; the phone's toolbar in two groups, by use

*9 November 2026.*

**Decision.** Two changes from the Silvio Rizzi critique (`critique-24`,
suggestions 5 and 6), which Rohit asked to build on 9 November 2026:

1. **Indent means inside** (SPEC section 5). In the sidebar, only the people
   and publications inside a folder are set a step in (the 46px names'
   edge). Those in no folder, under the People and Publications headings,
   start at the 20px margin, as folders and the headings do. This replaces
   decision 0123's "every feed's name on the one names' edge": then a person
   in no folder looked as if they belonged to the row above them, and on a
   phone a folder's last person ran into the People list. Found by name (the
   find field), folders and feeds stay on the 46px edge together, as before.
2. **The phone's article toolbar in two groups, in order of use** (SPEC
   section 20): More · Aa · (Write back) · Star, a short faint upright
   hairline, then Previous · Next. Acting on the article sits on the left,
   the rarest furthest out; moving through the list sits on the right,
   where a right thumb rests, since Next is pressed after almost every
   article. The places still share the bar evenly and each is a 44px tap;
   the hairline is only a mark (hidden from screen readers). Rohit chose
   this order after first seeing Previous and Next on the left.

**Cost.** Readers who already use the app will reach for the old places a
few times. DESIGN.md's "Never" asks not to rearrange what people have
learned once the look settles; the app is young and the owner chose it.
