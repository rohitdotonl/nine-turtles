# 0158: The starter packs come off the website

*3 November 2026. Replaces decision 0100. The owner had dropped the starter
packs from the plan; on 3 November he asked for them to be removed.*

**Decision.** The pages at nineturtles.app/packs/, their downloads, the
footer's link, and the made-up test pack the checks built are gone. SPEC
section 31 is retired; its number isn't reused.

**What stays.** Newcomers still have the app's own five starter feeds in
the first-visit welcome (SPEC section 2, decision 0079), "New to feeds?",
the feed finder, and Help (decision 0157).
