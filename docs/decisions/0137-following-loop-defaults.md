# 0137: The first steps and Move to… answer before you ask

**In one line:** Small defaults from the loop walk-through: a phone opens on the welcome while you follow no one; the welcome's "Bring your list from another app…" opens the file chooser at once; Move to… starts with nothing chosen

*1 November 2026.*

**Decision.** From a walk-through of adding, editing, folders, importing and
unfollowing, three small changes, the safe option of each, chosen under the
ten principles (decision 0134) once the owner said the agent could settle
what the principles answer:

- **On a phone, a reader who follows no one opens on the welcome**, not on
  the home screen's empty lists; Back goes to the home screen. (Clear at a
  glance: the screen answers "what do I do here?" before it's asked.)
- **The welcome's import link opens the file chooser at once** and reads
  "Bring your list from another app…": the reader's own words, not the
  file format. Before, it led to Settings › Your data, where a second
  button opened the chooser. What came in is said in Unread's line.
- **Move to… starts with nothing chosen.** "No folder" was chosen at first,
  so pressing Move straight away took feeds out of every folder.
  (Forgiving: a slip shouldn't be the default.)

**Not done here.** Calling things "follow" and "people" instead of "feed"
waits for the brand and design-language work. The bolder options (a
welcome under the phone's home lists, a drop area for the file, "No
folder" set apart at the end) were left for now.
