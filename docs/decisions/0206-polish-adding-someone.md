# 0206: Polish adding someone

*6 November 2026. The owner's onboarding review, findings 9, 12, 13 and 16
(finding 4, the welcome's order, landed with decision 0198), and the owner's answers in that thread (6 November, 15:32 UTC).*

**Decision.**

- **The welcome's heading stands still on a computer:** it sits 28% down
  the reading area (where centring put it) instead of being centred, so
  feeds offered or a message arriving grow the page downward and never
  move the heading. A phone's and a medium window's welcome was already
  anchored at the top.
- **Several feeds, any number followed at once:** the feeds a site offers
  are tick boxes, not round marks, the first ticked. **Follow** follows
  every ticked one, one after another, from the copy already looked at, so
  the publisher isn't asked again. Two or more followed together **become
  one person** (a blog and its podcast) and their list opens, with nothing
  more asked, since a source joined to others is always a person (section
  36). One followed asks "… is…" as before. Nothing ticked says "Tick at
  least one first." Any that couldn't be followed are named in the list's
  line. In the welcome, the field's own Add steps aside while feeds are
  offered: Cancel and Follow sit at the list's foot (on a phone, Follow
  alone at the list's full width), normal size; in a sheet, the foot's Add
  reads Follow. A YouTube address's question (its channel, or only the
  playlist) stays a choice of one, with round marks.
- **On a phone (and a medium window), the welcome's cards are cards:** the
  feeds offered, what was found and the two tiles sit on the page's color
  (by night a step lighter), as a phone sheet's cards do, so they no longer
  look like oddly indented text on the list's grey.
- **"How often it posts" is said nowhere:** not in the several-feeds
  choice, not on the website's feed finder, not in the AI assistants' feed
  health answers. Readers didn't need it to tell feeds apart (the newest
  title does that) and assistants asking about broken feeds need what's
  wrong. The `frequency` module, which only made those words, is deleted.
  How often nine turtles checks each feed is unchanged: it was always
  worked out separately, in `checking::rhythm`.
- **One quiet line about sharing**, only in the Add sheet on a phone where
  nine turtles is installed: "Reading them in another app? Share the page
  to nine turtles." under the field, at 12px in the faintest grey that keeps
  4.5 : 1 (checked in `contrast.test.ts`).
- **`/sign-in` is a real address**, the sign-in page's own, as `/sign-up`
  is. Signed in already, either opens nine turtles' home instead of "Wrong
  turn?". Sign-up's "Sign in" goes to `/sign-in`; any other address still
  shows sign-in when signed out and opens where it pointed after.

**Why.** Each is a stumble the owner's review found on the way in. Ticking
several answers "I want their blog *and* their podcast" in one step, and
joining them saves a trip to Edit's "+ Add a source".

**Not done here.** The starter list's clickable names and addresses, and its
"Add all five", are the starter-writers pull request's.
