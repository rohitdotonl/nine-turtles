# 0033: One mark per row, one field style, steadier sheets

_The selection part is refined by [0036](0036-apple-books-type-and-selection.md): the fill stays, with the keyboard's ring outside it._

*24 October 2026. From the outside critic's round 6 (`ui-polish/critic-round6.md`
in the project files, majors 1 to 5 and 8, minors 1, 2 and 5), under the
owner's "taste first" direction and their choice of bigger batches.*

**Why.** While reading with `j` and `k`, the selected row showed a tint, a
2px near-black outline and the accent bar at once: the loudest shape on
screen. Off-white's selection tints were cool grey on warm paper. The two
search fields looked like two design systems. Counts, header lines and
Settings headings were a few pixels off each other, a focus outline was cut
off at the top of the article toolbar, sheets jumped when an error appeared,
Add a feed had no Cancel, and the Undo note sat mid-pane under short lists.

**What changed.**

- **Rows:** the keyboard's outline is the text color at 65% (a unit test
  keeps it at 3 : 1 or more on every area), and on the selected row it
  replaces the fill. The accent bar stays at the pane's edge.
- **Tints:** off-white's are warm (`#E4DED2` list, `#DBD4C6` sidebar).
- **Fields:** both search fields are a faint tone, no edge, a search icon,
  44px tall and the same text size.
- **Edges:** the list's and the article's top lines are both 64px, and
  both search fields start on one line; the sidebar's counts share one
  column with the folders' (the feeds leave room for the folder arrows);
  Settings headings sit flush with their cards; the article toolbar keeps
  4px above its buttons so their outlines are never clipped.
- **Sheets:** anchored 12% down the window instead of centred, every sheet
  has Cancel, and Unsubscribe or Delete is an ordinary button under the line
  that says what it does.
- **Messages:** Add a feed's message is the same small size as sign-in's,
  and Edit feed's labels are all semibold.
- **The note** (Undo, new items) sits at the foot of the list pane however
  short the list.
- **The list's end:** a short hairline and "That's everything." when there's
  nothing older.
