# 0133: Our own eight design principles

*1 November 2026.*

**Decision.** The owner adopted eight ranked design principles, replacing
the seven first modelled on Apple Books: nothing pulls › the writer comes
first › clear at a glance › honest by default › made by a person ›
instant and still › power is findable, never in the way › built to last.
Their wording and tests are in `docs/DESIGN.md` Part 1; their sources in
`docs/RnD/design-principles.md`.

Also settled:

- **Breaking a principle on purpose:** allowed when clearly better, never
  by accident; every break goes in DESIGN.md's Exceptions log with its
  reason and limits.
- **No feedback through motion** (no springs, no pull-to-refresh).
- **One system** (after Vignelli): one spacing scale, a short type scale,
  the same thing always looks and is named the same.
- **The goal line:** cited for taste and coherence, recognisable from one
  screenshot. No outside app is a reference.
- **The three-pane layout** stays fixed unless the owner decides
  otherwise after careful mockups.

**Still open:** the tiebreaker, the brand idea, colors, type, the accent
and the turtles, all decided from mockups.

**Why.** The old principles came with the Apple Books reference. The new
ones say what makes nine turtles its own: it doesn't pull, it puts the
writer first, and it is honest and made with care.
