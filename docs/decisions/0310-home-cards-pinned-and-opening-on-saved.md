# 0310: Home on cards, Pinned, and opening on Saved

**In one line:** Home's lists sit on white cards on the sidebar's grey, each under its heading and sign (the top card: Saved, Notes, All articles, Unread, Everyone you follow); the reader can pin up to nine people to a Pinned card from their page; a folder's people hang from it on square branches; Settings takes the same cards; and the app opens on Saved, not Unread.

*18–19 November 2026. The design round on Home from the second whole-app critique (round two), tried on the owner's Mac over six rounds of versions behind `?home=…`; the owner locked the final look ("g3" with People and Publications and their signs), chose Settings in Home's look over Home in Settings' look, and chose Saved as where the app opens.*

**What changes.**
1. **Cards.** Each group of rows is a white card (the page's color; by night the page's black) on the sidebar's grey, its corners the held radius, with no lines between rows, under its heading. Headings are the one label with a sign before them in full ink: Pinned (a pin), Folders (a folder), People (a person), Publications (a newspaper). The top card has no heading. Where you are inside a card is the line grey, so the pill shows on white. The rows' own look, sizes and the find field don't change.
2. **The top card's order:** Saved, Notes, All articles, Unread, Everyone you follow. What the reader chose to keep comes first; what's new comes last.
3. **Pinned** (structure change 56, `subscriptions.pinned_at`, `server/src/pins.rs`, `web/src/lib/pins.ts`). A pin button beside their cog on a person's or publication's page pins them to the top of Home, or unpins them (`aria-pressed`, the pin filled while pinned). Up to nine, the first pinned first; nine turtles never pins anyone. A person's pin is kept on every place of theirs, as Write back's switch is. Pinned always shows, so pinning is found; empty, it says "Pin someone from their page, with [pin] at its top." Pinned people leave People and Publications and stay in their folders. A pinned row carries Unread's dot while something of theirs is new: the one exception to decision 0242's "no dot on people", because the reader chose those few to hear from.
4. **Folders' people** hang from the folder: a line falls from under the folder name's first letter, with a square-cornered branch to each person, ending at the last, on phones too. They start a step in, in the second ink. This replaces decision 0123's faint straight line.
5. **Settings** takes the same look: its page is the sidebar's grey and its groups are white cards, so a card is the same everywhere it's met.
6. **Opening on Saved.** The app, and "/", opens on Saved: what the reader chose to read, not what arrived (DESIGN.md's "nothing pulls"). Unread stays on the top card with its dot, and `/unread` still opens it. Following several people at once, an import and the first visit's starters still open on what's new (Unread, or All articles with Unread off), where the new writing is. This replaces SPEC section 10's "the app opens on Unread".

**Considered and not taken.** Faces or initials before names, two-line tiles, folders as tiles or as their own cards, closed folders, a hairline between rows (all tried in the rounds, and dropped by the owner). Home in Settings' look (tinted cards on the page's white): the sidebar then shares the reading pane's white on a computer and the two run together. Opening on Home with nothing chosen: an empty page to start the day.

**Revisit if** nine pins is too few or too many in use, or readers miss opening on what's new.
