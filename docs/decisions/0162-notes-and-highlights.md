# 0162: Notes and highlights

*3 November 2026. V1 item 8 (`docs/PLAN.md`), shaped with the owner from
high-fidelity mockups; SPEC section 42.*

## Decision

- **Highlights and notes on articles, and a note on each person,** private
  to the reader. Search finds them, Notes lists the marked articles, Your
  data exports them as one Markdown file, and AI assistants can read and
  change them (other apps never see them).
- **The look is soft pencil:** a faint tone of the ink behind the words and
  a fine underline, in every theme. No new color (the owner chose the safe
  option over a marker yellow).
- **Notes in the sidebar,** under Starred, only once there's a first mark.
- **Marked articles are kept like starred ones.** Unsubscribing asks
  "Keep them / Remove them too", only when something is kept (the owner's
  change, 3 November 2026); Remove them too is done when the unsubscribe is
  cleared, so Undo still brings everything back.
- **A highlight keeps its words, not a position,** with a little text either
  side, so it's found again if the publisher edits the article.
- **Without a connection** highlights and notes are made and kept on the
  device, with one quiet line until they reach the account (the owner's
  wording, 3 November 2026).
- **Video and podcast notes** start with the time playing, and a time in a
  note takes the player back there (the owner's ask).

## Why

The owner wants nine turtles to be where people keep what they read and
what they think about the people they read, without it ever becoming
public or social. Keeping marked articles follows the rule set for stars
and collections; asking first on unsubscribe gives readers the choice.
