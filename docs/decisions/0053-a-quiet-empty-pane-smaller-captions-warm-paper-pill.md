# 0053: A quiet line in the empty pane, smaller captions, a warm Paper pill

*24 October 2026. From the seventh Apple Books critique
(`ui-polish/critic-applebooks-7.md` in the project files, section 2(a),
gaps 1 to 3). Apple Books is the only design reference. Replaces 0044's
blank empty pane; adjusts 0050's caption size and 0051's Paper day pill.*

**Why.**

- The empty reading pane was blank paper (decision 0044, after the fourth
  critique). In the screenshots it read as an empty slab, 640px of pure
  white or black beside the list: a Mac app with nothing chosen shows one
  quiet, centred line instead, so you can tell the pane is waiting, not
  broken or still loading.
- A caption at seven-eighths of the text (about 17.5px under 20px on a
  phone), in the same serif, read like another paragraph. Apple Books sets
  captions clearly smaller.
- Paper's day selection pill was the ink-blue accent mixed into a warm
  pane, which came out a cool slate (#d6d9da on #edece7): the one cool thing
  on a warm page. Apple Books' Paper selection stays in the paper's family.

**What changed.**

- **"No article open"**, one line in the middle of the empty reading pane:
  the serif at the large size (20px), in muted grey (4.5 : 1 on the page in
  every theme), no period, never the accent, no picture. Screen readers
  still hear, when the list has something to open, "Choose one from the
  list, or press j." (on touch screens just "Choose one from the list.").
  The welcome on a slowfeed with no feeds is unchanged.
- **Captions** are 13/16 of the text (0.8125em), at a 1.4 line height, 6px
  under their picture, still in the second ink at 7 : 1 (AAA) on the page.
- **Paper's day pill** is Paper's own text color at 10% over the list or
  the sidebar (#d9d8d3 and #d1d0ca), as the night versions lift the page
  (decision 0041), instead of the accent at 12%. Text keeps 7 : 1 (10.9 and
  10.1), muted grey 4.5 : 1 (5.0 and 4.6), and it stays clearly deeper than a
  row under the pointer. A new check says a warm day pane (Paper, Calm,
  Focus) keeps a warm pill.

**Choices made.**

- *Blank paper or a line?* Critique 4 asked for blank paper; critique 7
  asked for the line. We chose the line: blank paper looked like a fault in
  the screenshots, and one muted serif line is as calm as a Mac app's empty
  state while saying what the pane is for. It is never the accent, so
  nothing in the empty pane calls for attention. The hint about j stays for
  screen readers only, so the page shows a single line.
- *Accent plus ink, or ink alone, for Paper?* The critique suggested a little
  accent over an ink mix. Worked out, that came to #d1d2d0, neutral rather
  than warm, and muted grey fell to 4.33 : 1 on the sidebar's pill (below
  AA). Ink alone at 10% gives the suggested warm tone (#d9d8d3, close to
  #d9d8d6) and passes every check. The pill still says where you are with
  the semibold name and, in the sidebar, the accent icon, so it never
  depends on color alone.
- *Quiet too?* Quiet's day panes are a cool grey (#e0e0e3), so the accent's
  blue-grey pill (#cbced6) stays in their family; it is unchanged. Original
  and Bold are neutral white and keep Apple Books' blue-grey selection.
