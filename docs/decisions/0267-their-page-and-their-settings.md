# 0267: Their page, and their settings one press away

**In one line:** A person's or publication's page is about them only (name, where they publish, an outlined Write to, and one card holding Your note and Your highlights), ending in one row, Settings ›, which opens their settings as a page of its own at `…/settings` with "‹ their name" above the title; still no Edit mode, and every setting still changes as it's touched. Revisit if two pages make settings feel hidden.

*14 November 2026. The owner's choice from a now-and-suggested picture, in a design critique of the whole app ("3, 5, and 6: Implement them"). The critique said plainly that it goes against decision 0254, made three days earlier.*

**What was wrong.** One long page was half about a person (who they are,
your note, their words you kept) and half a form (Name, This is, Folders,
Pause, Hide, Sources, Unsubscribe). The eye had nowhere to rest, the form
was as loud as the person, and Unsubscribe sat one scroll under your
highlights.

**Decision.**
1. **Their page** (`/people/12/about`) is them, then what's yours of them:
   - their name, where they publish, a paused or failing source's line;
   - **Write to**, now an outlined button (as Write back already is at an
     article's end, decision 0242), so their name is the one thing that
     leads the page;
   - **one card** (a grouped list's tone and corners) holding Your note
     and, under a hairline, Your highlights;
   - one row built as Settings' own rows are: the cog, **Settings**, ›.
2. **Their settings** (`/people/12/settings`) are a page of their own, in
   the same place: "‹ their name" above the title **Settings**, set as
   every place's title is. It and Escape go back to their page, with the
   keyboard on the Settings row, as one of Settings' own pages does
   (decision 0120). On a computer the bar keeps its ×, back to their list.
   The rows are the same as before, and so is how they behave: Name, This
   is, Folders, Pause for a while, a channel's two switches, Hide articles
   about…, Sources with Take out, + Add a source, and Unsubscribe.
3. **`e`, `/edit` and `/sources/N`** open their settings, with the keyboard
   on the title (as every page starts) and Name one Tab on. Landing in the
   Name field was right for one long page; on a short page of its own, a
   screen reader should hear where it is first.
4. **Words that pointed to "their page" for a setting** now say "in their
   settings" ("You can change this later in their settings.").

**What 0254 settled that stays.** No Edit mode, no Cancel or Done; every
fact once, where it's shown (the address only under Write to, sources only
in their settings); settings that change as they're touched; the large
name; Hiding opened from their settings leads back there.

**Why.** The writer comes first (principle 2) and clear at a glance
(principle 3): a page about a person should read as a person. Their
settings are still one visible press away, with a word and a familiar
sign (principle 9).

**Changes.** Decision 0254 (one page with the settings at its end, and `e`
landing on Name) and decision 0183 (Write to as the one solid button; an
address box's own Write stays solid, decision 0259). BRAND.md's line on the
paper plane says so.
