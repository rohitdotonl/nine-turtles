# 0139: One brand sheet

**In one line:** Every brand decision on one page, `docs/BRAND.md`, part of DESIGN.md with its standing; each line marked Live or Being built; a new brand decision goes there first, with the owner's yes

*2 November 2026.*

**Decision.** Every brand decision lives on one page, `docs/BRAND.md`:
name and words, typefaces, color and the four themes, the logo, app icon,
tab icon and share picture, the turtles' only job, icons, empty screens,
emails and the website. It is part of `docs/DESIGN.md`, with its standing:
DESIGN.md Part 1 (floors and principles) wins over it; it wins over
everything else about the brand. Each line is marked Live or Being built.
DESIGN.md Part 2 keeps the measurements and describes the live app until a
"being built" decision lands.

**Why.** The owner asked for one page that writes all of it down, so that
every future change, by a person or an agent, follows it (2 November 2026).
The decisions were spread over working notes outside the repository, where
agents can't read them.

**Changing it.** A new brand decision (a color, a typeface, a drawing, a new
use of the turtles, a new line of marketing copy) goes on the sheet first,
with the owner's yes and the reason, before it's built.
