# 0131: Our own design language, not Apple Books

**In one line:** Apple Books is no longer the design reference (replaces 0059); nine turtles gets its own design principles and design language, being written with the owner; until then the floors, then DESIGN.md as it stands

*1 November 2026.*

**Decision.** The owner dropped Apple Books as nine turtles' design
reference, replacing decision 0059. nine turtles will have its own design
principles and design language, with design treated as a first-class part
of the product. The aim: cited for taste and coherence, recognisable from one
screenshot (wording settled in decision 0133).

**Until the new language is written:** the floors come first, then
`docs/DESIGN.md` as it stands. The current look stays; nobody changes
things only to come closer to Apple Books, and redesign work waits for the
owner.

**Why.** Following one outside app made nine turtles look like that app,
not like something made by a person. The owner's new ideal reader (someone
who writes on the small web, `docs/RnD/ideal-reader.md`) judges a tool on
craft and care first, so the look has to be nine turtles' own.
