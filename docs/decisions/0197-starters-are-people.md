# 0197: The starters are five people's own blogs

**In one line:** "New to feeds?" offers five people's own blogs (James, Derek Sivers, Herman Martinus, Jeff Kaufman, Ava), each filed as a person

*6 November 2026. The owner's onboarding review, finding 6; the owner chose the five.*

**Decision.** "New to feeds?" (decision 0079) now offers five people who
write their own blogs, in place of five publications: James
(jamesg.blog), Derek Sivers (sive.rs), Herman Martinus (herman.bearblog.dev),
Jeff Kaufman (jefftk.com) and Ava (blog.avas.space). Each is added by its
own feed address, as before, and filed as a person (decision 0163) rather
than left to the guess, which can take a blog named after its writer for a
publication.

Each writer's name and site's address are one link to their site, in a tab
of its own, so a newcomer can look before they follow. With nothing ticked
the main button reads "Add all five" and adds all five (in place of "Choose
a feed first."); once any is ticked it reads "Add 2 chosen" and adds only
those. Nothing is ticked for the reader (the owner kept "none ticked").

**Why.** nine turtles is for reading the people worth knowing, and writing
back to them. A first list of people shows that on the first visit; a list
of magazines and image feeds showed the opposite. The list stays short,
fixed, the same for everyone and never ranked (DESIGN.md's Exceptions log).
