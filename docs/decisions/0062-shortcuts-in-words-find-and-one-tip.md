# 0062: Shortcuts in words, a list you can search, and one welcome tip

> **Partly replaced by [0064](0064-a-beautiful-empty-page-and-capital-keys.md):** "Shortcuts" became "Keys", and "No article open" became "Time to read something beautiful."

*25 October 2026. The owner asked for these after a fresh-eyes review: "A
label, not just an icon", grouping, a welcome tip and searching the list.
Changes 0041's rule that nothing on screen mentions shortcuts.*

**Why.** The keyboard icon at the sidebar's foot didn't say what it was, so
someone new to nine turtles might never find the shortcuts. The list was
grouped by kind of key rather than by what you're doing, and there was no
quick way to find one shortcut.

**What changed.**

- **Shortcuts in words** at the foot of the sidebar, between Add feed and
  Settings. In a sidebar narrowed below 15rem, where "Add feed" and
  "Shortcuts" can't both fit whole, it becomes the keyboard icon again.
  Its tooltip still names `?`.
- **Groups by moment:** Reading (j/k, open, open original, text size, star,
  back), Getting around (go to, search, the panes), Tidying up (mark
  unread, mark all as read, undo, show new items) and Anywhere (add feed,
  this list). Reading comes first because it's what the app is for.
- **Find a shortcut:** a field in the list's top line. It matches the
  words ("star") or the key as printed or spaced ("s", "g u", "shift a"),
  filters the search filters too, and says when nothing matches. A letter
  typed anywhere in the open list goes into the field, so `?` then "star"
  just works. Escape empties it first, then closes the list.
- **One welcome tip, once:** under "No article open", in small muted type,
  "Press J to open the first article, or ? for every shortcut." It shows
  only on screens with a keyboard and only until you open any article or
  the shortcut list; then never again on that device. It hangs below the
  line, so "No article open" stays in the pane's middle.

**Why this doesn't make it a feed.** Nothing blinks, counts or comes back:
one line, once, in the muted grey, never the accent.
