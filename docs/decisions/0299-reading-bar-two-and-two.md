**What changes** (refines 0298's points 9 and 10).

The two sides are equal columns, so the middle is the text's own middle, and the first and last icons mirror each other over the text's edges (`.over-text`'s padding is now the same on both sides).
2. **Nothing moves or goes as you read.** The bar is the same at the top and further down; only the middle changes.
3. **The running title** is the article's title alone, one line in the interface's face and the muted grey, cut short with …, fading in as 0298 set; the source's name under it is gone (it's under the title already). A hairline shows under the bar once the title has gone.
