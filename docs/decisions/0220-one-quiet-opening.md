# 0220: One quiet opening, in every list

**In one line:** Every list opens the same quiet way: no begun first article or Start reading on a phone or a computer; the newest is simply the first row, and the reading area shows its quiet line (replaces 0210)

*9 November 2026. The owner's pick from the Jony Ive critique (`critique-24/designer-12-jony-ive.html`). Replaces 0210.*

**Decision.** The begun first article and its Start reading are gone, on a
phone and on a computer. With nothing open and nothing to pick up, the
reading area shows its quiet line ("Time to read something beautiful."), in
every list, whether its first article is read or not. On a phone, the first
row is an ordinary row: no "Newest unread" label, no larger title, no
opening paragraph, no drawn button. Pick up where you left off (0175) is
unchanged and still comes first.

**Why.** Start reading only ever appeared when a list's top article was
unread, so the same list (All articles, a feed, a folder) changed shape
depending on whether one article had been opened, and Unread nearly always
looked different from the rest. It saved no presses: choosing the first row
opens the article, as Start reading did. It said things twice (the title in
the list and again in the reading area; a drawn button inside a row that was
already the button), its black button was the heaviest thing on screen
rather than the writer's words (principle 2), and a teaser paragraph every
time you arrive is a small pull (principle 1). One opening for every list is
cohesive (principle 6) and less, but better (principle 10).

**Changes.** `ReadingLead`, `RowOpening` and `leadOpening.ts` are removed;
the byline's `authorBesides` moves to `byline.ts`. Decision 0219's column
still holds for an article and a person's page. 0216's second point (a
summary's first paragraph in Start reading) no longer applies.
