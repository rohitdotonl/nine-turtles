# 0268: Calm rows for who isn't working

**In one line:** At the top of Everyone you follow, Not working and Having trouble are each one grouped list of calm rows instead of a card each: the person or publication icon, their name, the same sentence in regular weight, then Find new address, Write to (a person under Not working) and Unsubscribe (oxblood) as plain underlined words; same words, same steps. Revisit if readers miss what to press.

*14 November 2026. The owner's choice from a now-and-suggested picture (flow 7, "Follows that aren't arriving"), in the design critique of the whole app.*

**What was wrong.** Each follow that stopped arriving was a card of its
own, with a bold first sentence, a solid Find new address and, for a
person, a bordered Write to with a paper plane under it. Three broken feeds
made a stack of loud boxes at the top of a calm page, the loudest thing on
it, for something nine turtles mostly sorts out by itself.

**Decision.**
1. Each group (Not working, Having trouble) is **one grouped list**, its
   rows parted by the grouped lists' hairline, inset from the left; the
   group's name and the line under it stay.
2. Each row: the **person or publication icon** (the two drawings Add
   asks with; one nine turtles couldn't sort shows the publication's,
   since that's where it's kept), named in its tooltip and to screen
   readers ("Person: ", "Publication: " before the name); their **name**;
   the **same sentence** as before, all in regular weight; then **what can
   be done** as plain underlined words: Find new address, Write to them (a
   person under Not working only), Unsubscribe in oxblood.
3. What a press brings (Find new address's question or choice, Write to's
   "Opening your email app…" line or its address box) takes a line of its
   own **straight after the word that brought it**, so the eye and the
   keyboard meet things in the same order. The address isn't repeated in
   the row: their page shows it, with Change.
4. A row that works again after Find new address says so and **offers
   nothing more**; the keyboard rests on its name. An unsubscribed row
   offers Undo, the same underlined word.

**Why.** Nothing pulls (principle 1) and less, but better (principle 10):
the page is about the people you follow, and a broken feed is a fact with a
next step, not an alarm. Clear at a glance (principle 3): the icon says who
it is before the name is read, and every action is still a visible word,
44px to touch. The colors are checked: oxblood words at least 6.6 : 1 on
the group's tone in all three pages, its underline 3.2 : 1 or more, the
muted icon 5.9 : 1 or more.

**Changes.** Decisions 0118 (the cards and their filled main button), 0186
(a card each) and 0212 (Write to as an everyday bordered button with the
paper plane under the card's buttons; the address box's Write stays
bordered). BRAND.md's line on the person and publication icons says where
they now also stand.
