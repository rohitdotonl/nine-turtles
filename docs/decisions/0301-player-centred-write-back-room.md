# 0301: The podcast's Back, Play and Forward sit in the middle; Write back gets equal room

**In one line:** The episode player's Back 15, Play and Forward 30 sit in the middle of its panel, the length on their left and the speed on their right; at an article's end on a phone, Write back has the same room above it as under it.

*17 November 2026. The owner, from his phone: why aren't the podcast controls in the centre, and shouldn't Write back have more space, the same above and below?*

**Why they weren't centred.** No one chose it: the player was built as one row read from the left (Back, Play, Forward, then the length and the speed pushed to the right edge). Once an episode starts the length steps aside, so the three sat on the left with an empty gap before 1×.

**What changes.**
1. **The player.** Three columns: the length on the left (until it starts), Back, Play and Forward in the true middle, the speed on the right. The grey panel stays (the owner declined a finer, wider player without it).
2. **Write back.** 2rem above it, the same as the room under it at the page's end. On a computer it stays 0.75rem under Save and View original, which it finishes as one group.

**Revisit if** a long episode's length ("1 hr 32 min") crowds the left column on a narrow phone.
