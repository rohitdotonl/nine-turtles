# 0211: Their page holds your reading of them

**In one line:** Their page: name and Write to, your note, Lately (three newest), Your highlights from their writing (at most 20), then Source; a read-only list of a person's highlights for the signed-in reader

*6 November 2026.*

**Decision.** A person's or publication's page reads in order of what
matters: their name and Write to; your note on them; **Lately**, their
three newest pieces, each one press from reading; **Your highlights**, the
words you highlighted in their writing (newest article first, at most 20,
since Notes has them all), each set as at an article's end with "From
{title} · {day}" and your note on it; then Source and Hide articles
about…, where settings belong. Lately and Your highlights show only when
they have something. A new read-only address,
`GET /api/feeds/{id}/highlights`, lists the highlights from every place of
the person's, for the signed-in reader only. The owner chose this on 6
November 2026 from the "aha" mockups (idea 3), and said ship it.

**Why.** The "aha" work's first rule: get the reader to real reading and
writing in as few presses as possible. A person's page was a settings card;
now it's the conversation so far: what they wrote lately, what moved you,
and the button to write to them.

**Changes.** The order of 0146 and 0163's page: the note moves above the
sources.
