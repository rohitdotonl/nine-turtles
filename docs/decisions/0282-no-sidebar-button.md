# 0282: No button to hide the sidebar

**In one line:** On a computer the sidebar always shows beside the list; the sidebar-shaped button that hid it is gone, because with one thing at a time (decision 0275) the sidebar already steps away whenever an article or a person's page opens.

*16 November 2026. The owner asked whether the button still earned its place now that nine turtles shows two areas at a time, not three, and said to remove it.*

**Why.** The button made sense with three panes side by side: hiding the
sidebar gave the list and the article more room. Since decision 0275 an
opened article (or a person's page) has the whole window, the sidebar and
the list away, so the only thing the button still did was widen the list,
which decision 0277 already holds to a reading column. One less control in
the top line, and one less way to lose the sidebar (power is findable,
never in the way; less, but better).

**Decision.**
1. On a wide or medium window the sidebar shows whenever the list does.
   Its width can still be dragged (unchanged).
2. A phone is unchanged: the feeds are its home screen, reached with Back.
3. No reader is left with it hidden: whether it was hidden was never kept
   on the device, only carried across a switch to a new version (decision
   0085), and the new version ignores that part of an older page's note.
4. The space kept clear for the button goes too: the wordmark's line, the
   list's column and the top lines of Settings and its pages no longer leave
   64px for it.
