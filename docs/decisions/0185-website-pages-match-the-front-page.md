# 0185: Every website page matches the front page

*5 November 2026.*

**Decision.** Rohit asked for the rest of the website to look like the
front page. Every page now has the front page's top: the logo and name on
the left and the places (Help, Open the app) on the right, along one 640px
column centred on the page. The grey sidebar band of decision 0153 ("G set
as C") is gone. Each page's text takes the letter's sizes: 21px on a 1.65
line (19px on a phone), titles 48px (36px on a phone) 80px under the top,
section headings 28px, the opening paragraph 24px. The front page keeps its
own larger sizes (decision 0169). Sizes are in DESIGN.md Part 2, "The
website's top".

**Why.** Two looks on one small site read as two sites. The front page is
the one Rohit chose most recently, and the letter's larger text is easier
to read.

**Instead of.** Keeping the band on the inner pages (0153), or bringing a
band to the front page.
