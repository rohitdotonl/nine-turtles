# 0140: The V1 feature list, and the rules it changes

**In one line:** The V1 list: person pages, write back, no unread counts and YouTube first-class (musts); the weekly email in Settings, installing said plainly and "How did you hear?" (shoulds); tags and a note to bloggers to discuss; trial and pause last; blogroll dropped; new mission line; the weekly email the one exception to "no notifications"

*2 November 2026. Settled with the owner, after the positioning
(`docs/RnD/positioning.md`) and the ideal reader
(`docs/RnD/ideal-reader.md`).*

## Decision

- **V1 is what the headline "Read people. Write back." promises,** built one
  at a time, each only when the owner says so, each with its SPEC section
  first. The list and its order are in `docs/PLAN.md` → "V1".
- **Musts:** people (one row and one page per person, over all the places
  they publish; folders are groups of people; details in `docs/PLAN.md`), write back (opens the reader's own email app; nine turtles
  never sends or reads that email), no unread counts, and YouTube as a
  first-class citizen (YouTube's own feeds and player, never scraping).
- **Shoulds:** the weekly email (off by default, turned on or off any time
  in Settings, not offered once at setup), installing the app said plainly,
  and "How did you hear about nine turtles?" (totals only, in the owner
  report).
- **Settled the same day:** no tags (folders stay folders); the note to
  bloggers whose feed breaks is written by Rohit by hand, from a list in
  the owner report, never sent automatically.
- **Last, with prices and launch:** the 30-day trial, after-trial checking
  and pause instead of cancel: there's no subscription to pause until
  payments are built for real.
- **Dropped:** a blogroll to show, everywhere (the owner: it feels like
  social media). The DESIGN.md parking lot's "public reading list page"
  goes with it.
- **New wording, everywhere** (the owner's yes, 2 November 2026):
  - The mission line: "nine turtles is a reader for the small web: a quiet
    place to read the people worth knowing, and write back to them." It
    replaces "… and lets you finish", which the positioning dropped.
  - "No notifications, ever" now names its one exception: the weekly email,
    chosen by the reader in Settings, plain text, off in one press. Principle
    1 already allowed it; AGENTS.md, DESIGN.md's Never list and SPEC.md now
    agree.

## Why

The app is already built far past V1; what's missing is what the home page
promises. Pricing features wait because they belong to subscriptions,
which go live last. Everything else waits for real readers.
