# 0312: Broken states say what's true, and nothing spills off the screen

**In one line:** After the broken-states check of 18 November 2026, nine turtles says "You're offline." only when the device has no connection, otherwise "nine turtles can't be reached right now."; the can't-be-reached page tries again by itself and has Try again; a post with no title opens without a big "Untitled"; a name or title with no space to break at breaks where it must; and a few failure lines say the true thing.

*19 November 2026. From the broken-states report (empty, loading, error and offline screens, very long names, huge lists, broken feeds and the first run, on phone and computer). The owner asked for every fix as suggested ("Fix everything as per your suggestion"), which includes the change to BRAND.md's "Messages".*

**What changes.**
1. **Offline, or nine turtles can't be reached.** Every line said while nine turtles gets no answer starts with why (`away()` in `connection.svelte.ts`): "You're offline." only when the device itself says it has no connection (`navigator.onLine` false), and otherwise "nine turtles can't be reached right now.", as during a new version going live or an outage, when the reader's wi-fi is fine. The end follows: "…once you're back online" or "…once it's back". The kept articles show either way. Without a connection, Everyone you follow says so in one calm line instead of two red errors with Try agains that couldn't work, and loads by itself once it's back; Saved with nothing kept says one line, not two that disagreed.
2. **The can't-be-reached page is never a dead end.** Opened while nine turtles can't be reached and nothing is kept on the device, the page says so in oxblood with the alert icon and Try again, asks again by itself every 10 seconds and as soon as the device comes back online, and opens the app on its own once nine turtles answers. It said "Reload the page to try again."
3. **A post with no title** opens without a big heading: the byline leads and the words follow, as its row shows its opening words (decision 0196). Screen readers still hear a heading: its opening words, which the bar also shows once scrolled. It showed "Untitled" in the title's large type.
4. **Signed out while reading** (an expired sign-in): the article says "You're signed out, so the article couldn't be loaded." with **Sign in** (a reload, as decision 0230), not "couldn't be loaded" with a Try again that could never work. nine turtles asks the server whether the reader is still signed in only after a load fails.
5. **No space to break at.** A title, name or byline with no space in it (a pasted link, a site named by its address) breaks where it must (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`, Tailwind's `wrap-anywhere`, which replaces `break-words` everywhere) instead of being cut off at the list's edge, spilling out of Write to, or pushing Everyone you follow wider than a phone. The article's own display title is left as it was. The welcome's example addresses break after their "/", so a phone never leaves "S" alone on a line.
6. **Small things.** A list that couldn't load says "The articles couldn't be loaded." in oxblood with the alert icon and Try again (it said "Try again" twice, in the text's color). A site that answered with an error, or not at all, is "The site couldn't be read just now. Try again later." (it said "didn't answer" when it had). Names sort with numbers as numbers: Blog 2 before Blog 10. Pressing Sign in with an empty field says "Type your email and password." or "Type your password too." under the fields, not the browser's grey bubble.

**Considered and not taken.** A Stop button while Add looks for a feed: stopping would only stop the waiting on this device, while the server could still follow the site a few seconds later, so the reader would be told something untrue. The wait already says "It can take up to a minute." Keeping the "You're offline." words and only adding the page's Try again: the words would still blame the reader's connection during every new version going live.

**Revisit if** a reader on a connection with no internet (a hotel's wi-fi before signing in) is confused by "nine turtles can't be reached", or a name breaks in an odd place where it used to fit.
