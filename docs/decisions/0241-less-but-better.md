# 0241: Less, but better: one way to search, each setting in one place

*10 November 2026.*

Rohit's "Implement all suggestions, including one in small notes" (10
November 2026), for the critique through the principle "Less, but better"
(`critique-24/principle-05-less-but-better.html`, in the project's files).
Its method: where the app offers the same thing twice, keep the better copy
and remove the other. DESIGN.md's principle 10 asks of every element:
would removing it make the task harder?

**Decision.**

- **Search has no options panel** (P05-1, replacing decision 0189's
  menu): where you start is the filter. The chip ("in: Tech") says where a
  search looks and its × searches everything; a search from Starred finds
  only starred articles. The count of results is said once, above them.
  The sliders icon goes with the panel. DESIGN.md's floor no longer names
  the search options' 36px controls, as they are gone (Rohit's request is
  the owner's yes to that wording).
- **Each setting in one place, where it acts** (P05-2): sort order only in
  each list's More menu; text size and font only in the Aa panel above an
  article. Settings keeps Unread and Page (which colours the whole app),
  and the line under each group says where the others are. `+` and `-`
  still change the text size anywhere.
- **No words that repeat the screen** (P05-3): Edit loses "Listed under
  People." ("Type a name to make a folder." stays: the field's placeholder
  disappears once you type, so the line is its visible name then, the
  first floor); the paragraph and "+ Add
  another place" become one link, "+ Add their newsletter, videos or
  podcast"; Everyone you follow loses its closing paragraph. Lines that
  say something the screen can't show stay ("Write back opens your own
  email app with this address.", "Their new articles wait until you untick
  this.").
- **The first screen: one way in** (P05-4): the field and its three
  examples sit together; "New to feeds? Start here · Bring your list from
  another app…" share one quiet line at the foot (on a phone, one under
  the other).
- **One layout button** (P05-5, from the small notes): the list's own
  sidebar-shaped button goes, as it looked the same as the sidebar's and
  did another job. The sidebar's button hides the sidebar; full-screen
  reading (`f`, or "Full-screen reading" in an article's More menu, so it
  is never only a key) hides the sidebar and list together, and the one
  button brings both back. A list hidden on its own before this comes back.
- **Settings' notes** (P05-5): each line under a group was checked against
  the same test; the two rewritten above are the only changes.

**Not done, and why.**

- The critique's idea of hiding the sidebar's empty lists until the first
  follow conflicts with decision 0222 (Notes and the other lists show from
  the first day, so the sidebar is the same for everyone) and with
  principle 3, "quiet must never become hidden". The newer, deliberate
  decision stands.
- On a phone, the list's Search button stays while the search is open: it
  is also how the search is closed there (it's a toggle), so it isn't a
  second copy.

**Why.** Each double was small, and together they made a calm screen
busy. Almost every removed copy has a better one that stays: searching
starred articles is searching Starred, and the text settings change beside
the text they change. Two things go for good, on purpose: searching only
the starred articles of one folder or person (search Starred instead and
look at the bylines), and hiding the list alone while the sidebar stays
(full-screen reading hides both).
