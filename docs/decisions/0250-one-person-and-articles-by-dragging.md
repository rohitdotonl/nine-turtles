# 0250: One person by dragging, and articles you can pick up

**In one line:** in the sidebar, someone dropped onto someone else's row becomes one person with them, under the name of the one dropped on, with Undo; on a computer an article's row can be dragged, carrying the publisher's link, and let go on Starred it's starred.

*10 November 2026.*

The last two of Rohit's four "pick up and drop" ideas (the first two are
decision 0248), shipped on his word: "Ship 1-4".

## What was decided

1. **Drop someone onto someone.** Held in the sidebar (decision 0207) and
   let go on another person or publication's row, the two become one
   person, as + Add a source joins them (section 36, decision 0163): every
   source of both, in every folder either was in, under the name of the
   one dropped on. The row takes the drop tone and the lifted name reads
   "One person with …" before letting go, so it's never a surprise; Undo
   puts each source back. The lists' headings and empty space keep their
   old meaning (out of a folder, or between People and Publications).
2. **Pick up an article.** On a fine pointer, a list row is draggable
   natively. It carries the original's link (text/uri-list and text/plain),
   never nine turtles' own address, so wherever it lands the publisher is
   credited and linked (the second floor), and an id of its own type that
   only Starred reads. Let go on Starred, it's starred through the list's
   own `s`, with its note and Undo. Touch screens keep the long press for
   the row's menu.

## Why

Joining took Edit, + Add a source and a choice; dropping one name on
another is the gesture people already expect from piles and folders, and
the lifted name says what will happen. Dragging an article out is the
one honest way to share it that needs no button: what travels is the
publisher's address.

## What would make us revisit it

Readers joining people by accident often (Undo being pressed soon after a
join), or a browser that lets a page read a dragged row's data before it's
let go, which would need the article type re-checked.
