# 0212: No dead ends

*6 November 2026.*

**Decision.** Two places where nine turtles stopped the reader now offer a
next step. In Everyone you follow, a person's **Not working** card ends its
sentence "Ines Duarte may not know." and offers **Write to Ines Duarte**
under Find new address and Unsubscribe: an everyday bordered button with
the paper plane, working exactly as Write to on their page (the address
when known, else the same small box, with the one look on their site of
decision 0168). Publications get neither. A search that found nothing in
one place offers **Search all articles**, the same words everywhere, at
once. And when Add finds no feed on a site, **Ask them for a feed** offers
**Write to june.example**: nine turtles reads that site's home page and,
only if that shows no address, the one contact or about page it links to
(the blog checkup's visit, decision 0178, under the feed finder's limits),
never the page given, which may be an article. One reader may ask only a
few times in half an hour, so an account can't be used to gather
addresses from many sites; unlike the blog checkup, the address found is
shown, because the reader is about to write to it,
and the reader's email app opens with a short request they can change.
Nothing is kept. The owner chose all three on 6 November 2026 from the
"aha" mockups (idea 4), and said ship it; that is their yes to the new
visit, the fourth the second floor allows (DESIGN.md, AGENTS.md).

**Why.** The "aha" work's second rule: turn every dead end into a next
step. A broken feed is often something its writer doesn't know about, and
a search in the wrong place is one press from the right one.

**Changes.** The second floor's list of visits gains one (DESIGN.md Part 1,
AGENTS.md). Nothing earlier is replaced: Find new address stays the main
button, and Write to's address and site visit are 0147's and 0168's.
