# 0239: Deleting sits apart, removals say what goes, a pause shows

*10 November 2026.*

Rohit's "Implement every suggestion from round 1. From round 2, implement
10, 11, 12, 13, 14" (10 November 2026), suggestions 4, 8, 11 and 12 of the
critique through the principle "Forgive mistakes"
(`critique-24/principle-04-forgive-mistakes.html` and its round 2, in the
project's files). DESIGN.md's Forgiving principle asks that destructive
actions sit apart from frequent ones and that a message says how to fix
what happened.

**Decision.**

- **Leaving** (suggestion 4): Delete data… and Delete account… leave
  Settings › Account for a group of their own at the very end of Settings,
  "Leaving", with "Each one says exactly what goes before anything is
  removed." under it, then Help and the version. Account keeps Your plan,
  Email, Password, Weekly email and Sign out (SPEC section 18).
- **Messages that say the fix** (suggestion 8): "The highlight couldn't be
  saved. Choose the words and press Highlight again in a moment.", "The
  highlight couldn't be removed, so it's still here. Try again in a
  moment.", "The highlight couldn't be copied. Select its words and copy
  them yourself.", "The word couldn't be added. Press Add again in a
  moment." (the word already stays in the box) and "This isn't here any
  more; it may have been removed on another device."
- **Keep or remove** (suggestion 11): Keep them and Cancel sit together;
  under a hairline, "Or, if you don't want them any more:" and the oxblood
  button naming exactly what goes, "Remove the 2 starred articles too"
  (SPEC section 42).
- **A pause shows** (suggestion 12): above a paused source's list and on
  their page, "Paused since 23 Oct. Their new articles arrive already
  read." with Resume, which unticks Pause for a while. Second ink, no
  color, no badge (SPEC section 15). The critique wrote "posts"; the app
  says "articles" everywhere else, so the line does too (Cohesive).

**Why.** Space is the cheapest guard against a slip; a button that names
what it removes is its own warning, so no dialog is added; and a lapse (a
pause forgotten for weeks) is cured by keeping the state where the reader
already looks.
