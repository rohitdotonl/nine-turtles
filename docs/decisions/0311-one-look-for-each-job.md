# 0311: One look for each job, and a plain zero in every number

**In one line:** After the consistency check of 19 November 2026, every job in the interface has one look and one word: one label for every group, one wordmark, one Undo, one order for a box's buttons, one word for each act (Unsubscribe, Mark as unread, remove from Saved, Text and page), an icon before every row of every menu, and Atkinson's plain zero in every number, while addresses and codes keep the crossed zero.

*19 November 2026. From the consistency check report (colour, buttons, words, icons, placement, spacing and type, on phone and computer, by day and night). The owner asked for each fix as suggested, chose **Unsubscribe** as the one word for leaving someone, chose "Icons in every menu", and said yes to the plain zero in every number, which changes BRAND.md's Typefaces.*

**What changes.**
1. **Words.** One word for each act: Unsubscribe (never Unfollow, in the app and in Help), Mark as unread, "remove from Saved" (the swipe keeps its short "Unsave"), Text and page, Find new address. Every empty list says "Nothing … yet."; failures say what couldn't be done and "Try again in a moment."; a successful undo says "Undone."; the lines said in many places (not an email address, an account that exists, too many sources) are written once in `messages.ts`; whoever runs the installation is always "whoever runs this nine turtles". The weekly email opens "Hello," and its subject is "New from the people you read on nine turtles".
2. **Looks.** One group label (`.label`, also on grouped lists' heads and a list's groups); one wordmark (Literata's display cut, semibold, `Wordmark.svelte`), on Connect an assistant too; Undo always the underlined text button; a quiet icon button (Copy, Rename, Remove) never has an edge; a box inside a page orders Cancel before its main action, as a sheet does; a button with nothing to act on yet goes muted (`.unavailable`); questions inside a page sit in the group's tone, small forms stay outlined; a folder in the sidebar opens with ⌄ and closes with ⌃, since › means another page; a failed part of a page says so in oxblood with the alert icon and Try again.
3. **Menus.** Every row of every menu has its icon before its words, from the drawings already in use (BRAND.md → "Icons"): the row's menu, the list's More, the article's More and the chosen words' menu. "Info about…" shows the person or the newspaper, whichever they are.
4. **The plain zero** (replacing 0246's "dates only"). Atkinson's zero without its slash comes first in `--font-sans`, so every number in the interface has it: dates, times, counts. Addresses and codes keep the crossed zero through `.address` and `--font-addresses`: a web or email address on its own or found inside a sentence (`addressed.ts`, which replaces `dated.ts`), address and email fields, a password being shown, an assistant's key. The plain zero is as wide as Atkinson's other figures, so tabular figures still line up. An article keeps its own font's zero.
5. **Small things.** The back button's name is never cut short beside its arrow; the tab names the sign-in and emailed-link pages (and no longer names the list after signing out); the website's main button matches the app's (88% ink, full ink under the pointer, 8px corners, 44px tall).

**Considered and not taken.** Icons only in some menus (the article's More already had them, so the others looked unfinished beside it). Keeping the crossed zero everywhere but dates: counts and times then looked like code beside a date with the plain one.

**Revisit if** a reader mistakes 0 for O somewhere an address isn't marked, or a menu feels busy with its icons.
