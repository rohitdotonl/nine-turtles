# 0029: Literata semibold instead of bold

*24 October 2026. From the outside critic's round 5 (`ui-polish/critic-round5.md`
in the project files), under the owner's "taste first" direction (decision 0027).*

**Why.** The critic's main point was weight: at 700, Literata's titles read like
newspaper headlines in an app meant to feel like a book.

**What changed.**

- slowfeed now ships Literata **600** (latin and latin-ext, from the same
  Fontsource release as before) in place of its 700. The download is the
  same size, so the 400 KB font budget is unchanged.
- Titles, headings and bold words in articles are semibold. Atkinson
  Hyperlegible keeps its bold, since that font is chosen for legibility.
- The article title is about 1.55 times the text size.

**Not done.** A medium (500) interface weight is still left out, for the font
budget (decision 0026).
