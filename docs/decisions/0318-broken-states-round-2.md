# 0318: Broken states, round 2: no blank pages, nothing untrue, calm without a connection

**In one line:** After the second broken-states check of 19 November 2026, a busy answer for everyone the reader follows no longer leaves lists, people and folders blank; Connected assistants never says "Nothing connected yet." when it couldn't load; every group that couldn't load for want of a connection says so calmly; signing in without a connection says "You're offline." and keeps the password; and pictures that didn't load one after another share one line.

*19 November 2026. From the round-2 broken-states report (the live version broken on purpose in Chrome's and Safari's engines: server errors, outages, offline, slow answers, an empty account, very long names, 1,000 people and 112 folders). The owner chose items 1 to 5 ("Build 1-5, merge"); item 6, hiding Pinned before the first follow, was not taken.*

**What changes.**
1. **No blank pages.** Every list, person and folder is drawn from the list of everyone the reader follows (`/api/feeds`). When that answered with an error (as during a restart), Unread, Saved, All articles, Notes, a person's page, a folder and an article showed only their heading, forever. Now where the list would be says "The articles couldn't be loaded." with Try again, as a list does (`LibraryFailed.svelte`), beside the sidebar's "Everyone you follow couldn't be loaded."; with no connection and nothing kept, both say calmly "You're offline. Your reading shows once you're back online." and load by themselves once it's back. The two lines ask again once between them (`once` in `LibraryFailed.svelte`), and stay while they ask, so the keyboard keeps its place. Everyone you follow says its failure once, not "Who isn't working couldn't be checked." as well, even while its Try again asks for both.
2. **Nothing untrue.** Connected assistants showed "Nothing connected yet." above "The connected assistants couldn't be loaded.": it doesn't know. All readers said "Only whoever runs this nine turtles can see them." under every failure, the owner's own included: now that's said only when the server refuses a reader who doesn't run it ("Only whoever runs this nine turtles sees Readers.", as Settings says).
3. **Calm without a connection, everywhere.** `LoadFailed` takes `shows` ("All readers shows"): when nine turtles got no answer at all, the group says "You're offline. All readers shows once you're back online." (or "nine turtles can't be reached right now. … once it's back"), in the text's color, with no Try again, and asks again by itself once the connection is back (decision 0312's rule, now on All readers, Hidden articles, Hiding's list, Connected assistants, a person's Replies you started and highlights, the list's place above, and Everyone you follow, whose own copy of the line it replaces).
4. **Signing in without a connection** said "nine turtles can't be reached right now." and emptied the password. Now it says "You're offline. Sign in once you're back online." when the device has no connection, and only a wrong password empties the field (finishing a sign-up too). The other forms that send to nine turtles say "You're offline. Try again once you're back online." (`unreachable()` in `messages.ts`).
5. **Pictures that didn't load, one after another,** with no description and nothing between them, share one line: "11 pictures didn't load. See them on the original" (`joinRun` in `missing.ts`; pictures fail in any order, so a note joins the one before it and after it). A picture with a description keeps its own line, which says what it showed. A note joins only a paragraph or link holding nothing else, so a picture that did load beside it always stays.

**Considered and not taken.** Making every `LoadFailed` calm on its own when offline: the page that opens while nine turtles can't be reached, and a page whose code couldn't load, rightly keep Try again (decision 0312), so a group opts in with `shows`. Hiding Pinned for a reader who follows nobody yet (item 6 of the report): not chosen this round.
