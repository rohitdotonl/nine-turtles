# 0307: Replies you started, their settings in a cog, and Write back in full ink

**In one line:** a person's page lists the replies the reader started to them (any way back pressed at an article's end; the article and the day, never what was written, removable, off in Settings › Reading), their settings move to a cog at the top right, the note and highlights get icons, and Write back at an article's end is back to the full ink at the usual size.

*18 November 2026. From the whole-app critique and the discussion that followed it with the owner, who agreed each of these in the mockups ("what we agreed", round two). The purpose the owner gave: nine turtles connects people, first by reading them deeply, then by writing back, which starts a conversation.*

**What changes.**
1. **Replies you started** (`server/src/write_back/started.rs`, structure change 54, SPEC sections 36 and 37). Pressing any way back at an article's end notes the article and the day in a new `replies` table: one line per article, the latest day. The web app tells the server; nine turtles never sends or reads the email, so it can't know whether anything was sent, and the list says so in one line. Write to on their page, with no article, notes nothing. Their page lists the lines first in the card of what's yours of them (only once there's one): a paper plane, the title (opens the article), the day. Each line has a quiet bin; Undo writes it back with its day (nothing deletes without undo). **Settings › Reading › Replies you started** (on unless switched off, kept with the reader's other shared settings) off notes nothing new and hides the list; lines already there are kept for when it's on again. A line goes with its article when that leaves the reader's library. Deleting one's data or account removes them (`people::PER_READER_TABLES`, `DATA_TABLES`). Your data's export doesn't include them yet.
2. **Their settings are a cog** at the top right of their page (on a computer beside the ×; on a phone at the right end of the line with ‹), named "Settings for {name}" with `e` in its tooltip, and the keyboard comes back to it. Replaces decision 0267's row at the page's end: the row read as the page's last word, and settings are housekeeping, not part of who someone is.
3. **Icons for what's yours:** your note has Notes' page beside it, and each highlight on their page a new **highlight** icon, a marker's tip over the line it leaves (never Rename's pencil), in place of decision 0244's 2px line. A reply is the paper plane. Same size and second ink, so the card scans by icon.
4. **Write back in full ink** at an article's end, at the usual size, as Write to is on their page. Replaces decision 0298's "small and soft" (the second ink, the meta size, a faint edge): writing back is what nine turtles is for, so its button shouldn't apologise. Still outlined, never solid.

**Considered and not taken.** A line at the top of the next article saying "You started a reply to …" (the owner: noise; new replies aren't clustered with old ones in chat or email either). Saying how each reply was made (Email, Copied address): the date and the icon say enough. A messages-style ledger with what was written: nine turtles never sees it, and isn't a messaging app.

**Revisit if** readers take the list for proof that a message was sent, or find it watchful rather than useful.
