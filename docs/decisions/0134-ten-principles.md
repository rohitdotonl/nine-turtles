# 0134: Ten principles, warm never cute, three panes and the logo kept

**In one line:** Ten ranked principles (forgiving and cohesive added; clear answers before you ask; instant shows changes live; beauty a goal; less but better and light); "useful" and "challenges the status quo" as feature checks; warm, never cute; three panes and the logo kept

*1 November 2026.*

**Decision.** After checking every chapter of his design field guide and
Rams' ten principles (updated form), the owner said yes to:

- **Ten ranked principles** (replacing 0133's eight): nothing pulls › the
  writer comes first › clear at a glance › forgiving › honest by default ›
  cohesive › made by a person, to the last detail › instant and still ›
  power is findable, never in the way › less, but better, and built to
  last. New: *forgiving* and *cohesive* (Vignelli's "one system", promoted
  from a rule); *clear at a glance* answers the reader's question before
  they ask and keeps familiar ways unless broken on purpose; *instant and
  still* shows changes as you make them, no Apply buttons; *made by a
  person* makes beauty a goal; *less, but better* covers as little design
  as possible and a light, long-lived app.
- Rams' **"useful"** and **"challenges the status quo"** become checks in
  DESIGN.md's feature checklist, not ranked principles.
- **Warm, never cute:** warmth in copy comes from true, specific facts
  (names, dates, the writer's own words), never invented feelings or
  stories about people. Short, never verbose.
- **The three panes stay.** Design experiments happen inside the third
  (article) pane.
- **The logo stays** as it is.

**Still open:** colors (ten variations being compared), then the article
pane, type, the tiebreaker and the brand idea, all from mockups.

**Why.** The owner wants principles complete enough to guide future
decisions without daily design sessions.
