# 0220: Notes in the sidebar from the first day

*9 November 2026.*

**Context.** Suggestion SW-2 of the "Show what's possible" critique (an
agent's critique of the app against the principle that people can only use
what they can find), which Rohit asked to have built on 9 November 2026.
Notes was the one list in the sidebar that hid until the reader had
highlighted or noted something (decision 0187). Highlighting starts only
when a reader happens to choose some words, so a reader who never did that
never learned that Notes, or highlighting, existed. Starred, by contrast,
shows from the first day and its empty list says how to fill it.

**Decision.** Notes always shows in the sidebar, between Starred and All
articles, on every device. Before any highlight or note, its list says
"No notes yet." with how to make one (the empty list it already had,
section 6). Unread still goes while it's switched off. Nothing else
changes: Settings › Your data still offers the notes export only once
there's something to export.

**Why.** "Quiet must never become hidden" (DESIGN.md, principle 3) and
"power is findable, never in the way" (principle 9). One more quiet row
costs little; a feature nobody can find costs the feature. It also makes
decision 0187's rule true without an exception: the sidebar is the same
for everyone, so Help and the screenshots always match.
