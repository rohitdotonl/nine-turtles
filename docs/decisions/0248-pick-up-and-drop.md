# 0248: Pick up and drop

*10 November 2026.*

**In one line:** someone held in the sidebar can be dropped on Pause or Unsubscribe at its foot (Unsubscribe asks Edit's keep-or-remove question first when something of theirs was kept); a page's address dropped on nine turtles, or a link from an article dropped on the sidebar, opens "Follow …?" (onto a folder files them there), and an OPML file dropped on it is imported.

Rohit's idea (10 November 2026): "When the user picks a feed, another option
should show up: unfollow the feed. Simply drag and drop to unfollow." He
chose four ideas of that kind from the mockups (the "Pick up and drop"
page) and said "Ship 1-4 … with asking the user if they want to delete
notes and highlights." This note covers the first two; 3 (drop one onto
another to make one person) and 4 (drag an article) follow in their own
change.

**Decision.**

1. **Pause and Unsubscribe at the sidebar's foot.** While a person or
   publication is held (decision 0207's drag), the foot trades + Add, Keys
   and Settings for two places to drop them: **Pause** and **Unsubscribe**.
   Unsubscribe is in the oxblood words, the one color for removing things.
   Both are words alone, with no icon: the bin is already Delete data's and
   the pause bars the player's, and an icon has one meaning (BRAND.md →
   "Icons"). They're for the pointer only; the keyboard keeps Edit.
2. **The same unsubscribe as Edit's, with its question.** Dropping on
   Unsubscribe leaves every source of theirs at once, with Undo and 30 days
   of Bring back (decision 0240). When the reader starred, highlighted or
   noted anything of theirs, the foot first asks Edit's question (section
   42): Keep them, Cancel, or the oxblood button that names what it removes.
   Rohit asked for that rather than keeping everything without asking, as
   the bulk Unsubscribe does.
3. **Pause** pauses every source not paused yet, with Undo (resume).
4. **Drop a link to follow.** On a computer, another tab's address dropped
   anywhere on nine turtles, or a link dragged from an article and dropped
   on the sidebar, opens "Follow <name>?" (decision 0138); dropped on a
   folder, they're filed there once followed. An OPML file dropped the same
   way is imported (decision 0238's Undo). This visits only the page the
   reader dropped, as pasting it into + Add does (decision 0016), and
   nothing until the question opens: it joins the list of visits in
   AGENTS.md's hard rule and ENGINEERING.md. A link nudged inside the
   article visits nothing, since a link from the page itself counts only on
   the sidebar, and nine turtles' own links never count.

**Why.** Arranging the sidebar already meant picking someone up; letting go
of them is the most direct way to let them go, and the foot is where the
thumb and the pointer already are. Dropping a link is how people move
things between windows everywhere else.

**Not done.** A folder can't be dropped on Unsubscribe (deleting a folder
stays in Edit folder); swipes on list rows stay out (SPEC section 20).
