# 0305: Fewer words, and four icons for words

**In one line:** the app's longer lines are cut to what the reader needs (about twenty places, mostly Settings), and Keys, Copy, Rename and a quiet Remove become icons alone, each still named to screen readers and in its tooltip.

*18 November 2026. The owner asked to remove verbosity from the app (not the website): fewer words wherever fewer suffice, without losing context, and icons where words aren't needed. He picked from a list of 25 and turned down two (the Add sheet's line under A publication, and the install page's opening line).*

**What changes.**
1. **Shorter lines.** Settings (Unread, Page, Account, Your data, Passkeys, Connected assistants, Newsletters, Your plan, Hidden articles), a person's settings and folders ("You can undo it." instead of "You can undo it straight afterwards."), sign-up and sign-in, the weekly email's stop page, the phone's Aa sheet and the first-visit try line. Each says the same facts, fewer words. Cancelling a plan stays said on the Plan page (the honesty promise).
2. **Two fixes on the way.** An article with no text in its feed said "View original to read it…", but View original is now an arrow with no words (decision 0299): it now says "Only the title is in the feed." with **Read it on {site} ↗**, as a shortened article ends. And Forgot? said "We'll email you a link", but the app never says "we" (DESIGN.md → Voice).
3. **Icons for words.** The sidebar's **Keys** is the keyboard alone (it was its icon and word, decisions 0062 and 0064). **Copy** beside an address or key is two overlapping pages; a passkey's **Rename** is the pencil; a hidden word's **Remove** and a highlight's **Remove** in an article's notes are the bin. Each keeps its full name for screen readers and in its tooltip, and stays a 44px target. A passkey's Remove stays a word: it's oxblood, and oxblood is always with words (BRAND.md → Color).

**Considered and not taken.** Turning error messages shorter: the house style needs the "what to do" part (BRAND.md → Messages). The delete sheets' warnings: they protect the reader's data (the third floor).

**Revisit if** a reader can't find the shortcut list or doesn't recognise one of the icons.
