# 0179: The original turtles are back

**In one line:** The logo is the original nine turtles again (half-circle shells, round heads); the heavier redrawn nine (D2) is dropped; sizes, baseline, black app icon and tab icon unchanged

*4 November 2026.*

**Decision.** The logo goes back to the original drawing of decision 0067:
half-circle shells and small round heads. The heavier redrawn nine (D2),
live from 2 to 4 November 2026, is dropped. The owner asked for it.

**What stays.** Everything else about the logo on the brand sheet
(`docs/BRAND.md` → "The logo"): its sizes beside the name, its feet on the
name's baseline, the black app icon, the tab icon's black square (its one
turtle is the original turtle again too) and the share picture's layout. The original drawing now has a box that ends at the
turtles' edges, so it stands on the name's line the way D2 did.

**Why.** The owner prefers the original turtles. D2's reason, that the
turtles stay turtles at small sizes, is weighed against that: at the
sidebar's 27 pixels each shell is still about 4 pixels tall and solid ink,
and contrast is unchanged.
