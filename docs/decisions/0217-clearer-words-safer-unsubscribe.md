# 0217: Clearer words and a safer Unsubscribe

*9 November 2026. The owner's choice, from the Don Norman critique.*

On 8 November 2026 the owner asked for design critiques of the app, each from
one designer's or principle's point of view, with mockups (kept outside the
repository, in the project's files). From the Don Norman critique, the owner
picked three changes on 9 November 2026 and asked for "just these 3":

1. **‹ Home, not ‹ Feeds** (the critique's DN-5). On a phone, the back button
   to the first screen said "Feeds", the system's word, which the reader meets
   nowhere else (people and publications are named instead, SPEC section 36).
   It now says "‹ Home"; screen readers hear "Back to Home". The button is the
   same one decision 0191 kept on the welcome; only its word changed.
2. **Search articles** (DN-6). The list's search field looked exactly like the
   sidebar's "Find a person or folder" field but did a different job. At rest
   it now says "Search articles", and its label (what screen readers and voice
   control use) says the same, so the name matches what's shown (WCAG 2.5.3).
3. **Unsubscribe apart from Move to…** (DN-7). In Everyone you follow's bar,
   Unsubscribe sits alone at the left; the count and Move to… at the right.
   The frequent action and the one that removes things are at opposite ends,
   as every sheet already places its destructive button.

Considered and left: "‹ nine turtles" and "‹ Back" for the button (the owner
chose Home), and "Search Reading Room", naming the list (the owner chose one
wording everywhere). A reviewer noted that on an installed app "Home" could
suggest the phone's Home Screen; the owner's choice stands.
