# 0176: Themes named by the page they give

*4 November 2026.*

**Decision.** The four themes (Original, Plain, Fountain pen and Signal)
and the separate Appearance setting (Light, Dark, Match device) become one
setting, **Page**, with four choices:

- **White**: a plain white page, its unread dot ink blue `#2C4F8C`.
- **Paper**: a warm cream page (`#F7F3EA`) with warm-black words, its dot
  old-ink brown `#8C5500`.
- **Night**: near-black (`#121212`) with soft white words, its dot pale
  blue `#94B2E6`.
- **Match device** (the default): White by day and Night by night, as the
  device is set, switching live.

Each choice is the page you get, so there are no day and night versions to
think about. Fonts, sizes and line widths stay in the Aa panel. In the Aa
panel the page is four swatches, each with its name under it; in Settings ›
Look and feel it is a row of four words, Page, in Appearance's old place.
"Font, theme and line width" becomes **Font and line width**. Plain's blue
article links go with Plain: links are the text's own color everywhere.
Brand decision in `docs/BRAND.md` → "Color".

**Why.** The owner's review, item 3 (step 10 of the review plan, chosen by
Rohit on 3 November 2026 over Milestone 3's "four themes"): names like
"Fountain pen" and "Signal" told a reader nothing about what they'd see, and
two settings (theme and Appearance) did one job. A safe version (White and
Paper, each with a day and a night, Appearance kept) and a bold one (this)
were shown as pictures; the owner's review thread chose the bold one: one
setting instead of two, each choice the page you get (less, but better;
clear at a glance). The cost, accepted: Paper has no night of its own; a
Paper reader who wants it dark picks Night.

**Accent rule.** White's and Night's dots are at AAA (7 : 1). Paper's brown
is a mark held to 3 : 1 on every area (DESIGN.md → Exceptions log), as the
Braun themes' accents were (decision 0136); it is never text.

**Saved choices.** No moving of old choices (Rohit, 4 November 2026: act as
if there are no readers yet). A device that saved a theme from before opens
on Match device, keeping its font, size and width. The choice is kept in
the browser, so the database doesn't change.

Replaces decisions 0145 (four themes) and 0063's Plain theme (System serif
stays), and the Appearance setting of 0056.
