4. Dodo Payments (https://dodopayments.com/pricing): CORRECTED. Base 4% + $0.40 (US cards); international cards/APMs +1.5% on top (so ~5.5% + $0.40); India domestic 4% + 15 cents; subscriptions +0.5% (so a subscription from abroad can be ~6% + $0.40); PayPal/BNPL +3%; refund $1; dispute $30; USD SWIFT payout $25 (a $5 payout fee under $1,000 appears in FAQ, conflicts - confirm with Dodo); no setup/monthly fee. Adaptive currency: customers pay 2-4% FX fee. Treat "4% + $0.40" as a floor, not the all-in.
   - Discount codes: YES, % or flat, expiry, usage limits, per-customer limits, first-time-customer-only, stackable (20), cycle limit for subscriptions. https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/discount-codes
   - PPP/regional pricing: YES, built in (needs Adaptive Currency; per-country 30-100% of list; opt-in per product; discount codes stack). https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/purchasing-power-parity
   - Affiliates: YES via integrations only (Affonso, Dub, Rekomi), not native; cost not stated. https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/affiliates
   - Free trials without card: YES ("Start the trial without a card"; paid trials need card). Monthly + yearly intervals: YES; plan change with proration; customer portal. https://docs.dodopayments.com/features/subscription  (Note: two separate products/prices needed for monthly vs annual is my inference; docs describe plan-change API.)
   - Gift purchases: UNVERIFIABLE - no native gifting feature found in docs/search. Assume absent; would need custom (e.g., one-time-code via discount code at 100%, "card-optional at zero price" exists).
