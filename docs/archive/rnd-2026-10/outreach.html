Real names, pages and prices for the outreach parts of the playbook: newsletters, podcasts, creators, bloggers, Indian outlets, the "best reader" articles AI assistants read, and the directories worth listing in.

  7. Ask Tedium for a quote (about 10,000 to 12,000 readers, text-only sponsorship).
