### Observations for pitching
- Zapier lists the same three (Feedly, NewsBlur, Inoreader) every year. The authors (Harry Guinness, Justin Pot) have no public email. Their social profiles are the route.
- TechCrunch's authors publish email and Signal. The Pocket article was refreshed Aug 2026, so a note about a calm paid reader with a finite list has a live news hook.
- Feeder's pages are competitor content marketing. They do mention "calm" (Feedbin), so a contact request is plausible but low-yield.
- Easiest wins are small calm-reader lists (Artemis minimal list, the Bear Blog post), where nine turtles is the exact subject.

### Suggested order
1. AlternativeTo (verified, free, drives the Feedly/Inoreader/Pocket "alternatives" pages that AI assistants cite).
2. ALL-about-RSS PR, awesome-selfhosted PR (if a self-host guide exists), OpenAlternative, opensourcealternative.to.
3. SaaSHub, Slant, Minimal Gallery, Typewolf.
4. Calm Tech Institute: send an email and ask about software and the fee.
5. Outreach to the calm-reader authors (Artemis list, Bear Blog post) and to TechCrunch's Pocket piece.
6. Product Hunt quietly, by the owner's personal account only.

### Rule conflicts with the project
- Product Hunt company accounts are banned, so it must be a personal account.
- Product Hunt, Peerlist and Uneed reward upvote-chasing, which conflicts with the "calm over engagement" principle. Treat them as optional.
- The repo is public, so any contact details used in submissions must be product ones, not personal.
