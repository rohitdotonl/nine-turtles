## 9 November 2026: See what a word will hide

- **Settings › Hiding shows what a word would hide before you add it.** As
  you type, it says how many unread articles it would hide and lists the
  newest five, each with who wrote it and whether the word is in the title
  or the text. Nothing is hidden until you press Add (decision 0216).
- **Start reading no longer runs paragraphs together.** When a feed sends
  only a summary, the newest unread now shows its first paragraph, as it
  does for full articles, instead of "…dinner.There is…".
