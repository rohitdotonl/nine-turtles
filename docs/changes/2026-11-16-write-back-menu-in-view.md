## 16 November 2026: Menus stay in view

- A menu with no room under its button (Write back's arrow at an article's end, above a phone's bottom bar or a short window's edge; low in the sidebar) opens upwards instead, so every option shows.
- On a phone, touching the article while a menu or the Aa sheet is open only closes it: it never pulls you on to the next article or swipes you back.
