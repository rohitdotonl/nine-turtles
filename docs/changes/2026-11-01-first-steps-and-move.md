## 1 November 2026: Easier first steps, and a safer Move to…

- On a phone, someone who follows no one yet now opens straight on the welcome, instead of a home screen of empty lists. Back still goes to the home screen.
- The welcome's import link now reads "Bring your list from another app…" and opens the file chooser at once, instead of sending you into Settings. What came in is said at the top of Unread.
- In Settings › Your feeds, Move to… no longer starts with "No folder" chosen, so pressing Move by accident can't take feeds out of their folders.
