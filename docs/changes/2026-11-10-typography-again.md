## 10 November 2026: Typography, again

- An article's title is larger and lighter, in Literata's display cut, the version drawn for big type, as a book sets a chapter's title. Every page's title uses the display cut too.
- Headings inside an article step down clearly (26px, 22px, then the text's size), so a long essay's parts are easy to see.
- Dates in the list, the byline and on pages read "20 Oct", with a plain zero, instead of the crossed "2Ø Oct". Addresses and codes keep the crossed zero.
- A writer's bold inside italics is now real bold italic, and highlighting, keys, definitions, number tables and footnotes are all set properly.
- Larger text sizes get a little less room between lines; Settings' row names are regular weight; and text no longer shifts while the fonts arrive.
