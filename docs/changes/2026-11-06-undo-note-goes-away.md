## 6 November 2026: The Undo note goes away

- The note at the bottom of the list ("Unsubscribed from … · Undo") no longer stays forever: it goes when you open another article, press its new × button, or leave the list. Never on a timer. `u` still undoes until you leave the list.
- Long messages no longer push Undo onto a line of its own: the words sit on the left with room above and below, and the buttons stay on the right.
