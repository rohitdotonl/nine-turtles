## 1 November 2026: Five new themes

- Five themes after Braun's colors join Original and Plain: Brushed, Anodised, Fountain pen, Heather and Signal, each with a day and a night version (decision 0136).
- In each, the only color is the unread dot, never green or red. Choose one in Settings or the Aa panel.
- The app's download limit is now 200 KB (was 150 KB).
