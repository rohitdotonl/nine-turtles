## 14 November 2026: A trial for you: read one thing at a time

- **Only for you, and only where you switch it on.** Settings → Your nine turtles has a new switch, **Read one thing at a time**. On a computer it shows the list alone, then the article alone in the whole window, with **‹ Unread** at the top left (or Escape) to go back, the way a phone reads. Off, nothing changes; nobody else sees it. Live with it, then tell me whether to keep it (decision 0269).
