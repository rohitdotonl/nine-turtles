## 3 November 2026: Write back finds more addresses

- Write back now also finds a writer's address in their posts: a "Reply by
  email" or "Email me" link, or the same email link signed at the end of two
  or more posts. Nothing extra is fetched; it reads only what the feed brings.
- When the address still isn't known, the box asking for it has **Find it
  on their site**, which opens their website in a new tab so you can copy
  the address from their About or Contact page and paste it in.
