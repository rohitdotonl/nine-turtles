## 10 November 2026: A fix you can press for typos

- In **Add**, a mistyped address gets its obvious fix: type `gardenletters,blog` and nine turtles says "Did you mean gardenletters.blog, with a dot?" with a **Use gardenletters.blog** button. The same for a mistyped `https://`, doubled dots, `.con` for `.com`, pasted quotes, and an email address pasted instead of a website. What you typed is never changed by itself; press Add again to keep it.
- On **Sign up** and **Change email**, an email like `asha@gmial.com` gets "Did you mean asha@gmail.com? Use that" under the field. On **Sign in** it's offered only after a sign-in fails. Pressing the button again sends what you typed.
