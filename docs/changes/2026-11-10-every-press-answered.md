## 10 November 2026: Every press is answered

- Starring or marking an article unread while you read on a phone now says so at the bottom of the article, with Undo. Before, the note was hidden behind the article.
- Refresh says "Nothing new has arrived since 14:05." when nothing has come in, instead of looking as if nothing happened.
- Buttons that wait for nine turtles say what they're doing: "Signing in…", "Saving…", "Following…".
- After you press Write back, a line says "Opening your email app…", with Copy their address in case nothing opened.
- On a phone, a row or button you're pressing shows a faint tone under your finger.
- A key with nothing to do says why, such as "Nothing to undo." or "That's the first article here."
