## 18 November 2026: Pulling to the next article shows an arrow; lists scroll freely again

- In an article on a phone, pulling past the end (or top) now shows only an arrow, ↓ (or ↑), in a thin circle. Once the circle fills in, letting go opens the next (or previous) article. No more "Next", "Let go for the next" or the article's title there.
- A list no longer settles with a row at the top after a flick: it stops wherever it stops, as before.
