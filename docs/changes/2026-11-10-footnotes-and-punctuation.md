## 10 November 2026: Footnotes where you are, and proper punctuation

- Pressing a footnote's number now opens its note in a small card just under the line, so you never lose your place. Escape, × or a press anywhere else closes it; the notes are still at the end of the article too.
- In English articles, the straight quotes, double hyphens and three dots many feeds send are shown as curly quotes, dashes and ellipses, as in a printed book. Code stays exactly as written, other languages keep their own marks, and the stored article (and search) is unchanged.
