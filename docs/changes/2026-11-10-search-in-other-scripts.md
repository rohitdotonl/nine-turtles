## 10 November 2026: Search in Chinese, Japanese, Arabic and Hebrew

- Search finds Chinese and Japanese words: type two or three characters and every article with them side by side is found, anywhere in a sentence, with them marked in the result.
- Arabic and Hebrew words are found whether or not their short vowels are written.
- Hiding a word finds it the same way.
- Just after this went live, search was filled again in the background, so for a few minutes older articles might not have been found.
