## 9 November 2026: Clearer words and a safer Unsubscribe

From the Don Norman critique (decision 0215):

- On a phone, the back button to the first screen says **‹ Home** instead of **‹ Feeds**, the reader's word rather than the system's.
- The search field above a list says **Search articles**, so it can't be mistaken for the sidebar's field that finds a person or folder by name.
- In Everyone you follow, while choosing several, **Unsubscribe** sits alone at the left of the bar at the bottom, away from **Move to…** at the right, as in every sheet, so a slip can't land on it.
