## 11 November 2026: Write back always answers, and names notes

- Pressing the paper plane at the top of an article (or `w`) now says "Opening your email app…" at the bottom of the screen, with **Copy their address**, instead of at the article's end, out of sight.
- Writing back about a post with no title (a short note) gives the email a subject: "Re: " and the note's first words, instead of none.
- Escape puts the "Write back to…" address box away, as Not now does.
