## 27 October 2026: Shortcuts work the same with Caps Lock on

- With Caps Lock on, plain letter shortcuts used to act like their Shift versions: `j` jumped a whole feed, and `a` marked everything as read. Now Caps Lock makes no difference, and choosing your own key records the key you meant.
- While typing Chinese, Japanese and similar languages, Escape and other keys no longer trigger shortcuts mid-word.
