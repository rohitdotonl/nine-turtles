## 15 November 2026: Swipes on a phone

- Swipe right from anywhere on the screen to go back, as the back button does: from an article to its list, from Settings and every other page, and from a list's title to Home.
- Swipe left in an article for the next one; keep pulling past an article's end for the next one, or past its top for the previous one.
- Swipe a row right to mark it read, or left to star it, as in Mail, with Undo. A quick flick counts too.
- Swipe the Aa sheet or an opened picture down to close it.
- Before you let go, a small card says where you'll land; slide back to change your mind.
