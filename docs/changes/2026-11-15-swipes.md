## 15 November 2026: Swipes on a phone

- Swipe right from anywhere on the screen to go back, as the back button does: from an article to its list, from a list to Home, from Settings and every other page.
- Swipe left in an article for the next one; keep pulling past an article's end for the next one, or past its top for the previous one.
- Swipe a row left: a little way marks it read, all the way stars it, with Undo.
- Swipe the Aa sheet or an opened picture down to close it.
- The screen follows your finger, with the screen you're going back to under it, or a small card naming the next article; slide back to change your mind.
- Let go and the screen slides the rest of the way, or springs back: the one motion in nine turtles, and none with your phone set to reduce motion.
