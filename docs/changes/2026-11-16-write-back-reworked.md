## 16 November 2026: Write back, reworked

- Write back at the end of an article has an arrow beside it with every way back: your email app, Gmail, Outlook.com, copying their address, or commenting on their site.
- Whichever you choose becomes the button for that person: Write back, Copy address or Comment. Where your email opens is kept on each device.
- Each person's Settings has a **Write back** switch: off takes Write back, the paper plane and Write to away from them, while they stay one of your people.
