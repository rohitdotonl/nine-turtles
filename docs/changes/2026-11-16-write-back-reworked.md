## 16 November 2026: Write back, reworked

- The line under Write back now ends in **Copy address**, for writing from an email nine turtles can't open.
- When a blog's feed says where an article's comments are (WordPress and many others do), its end has a quiet **Comments** link beside View original; on a phone it's **Open comments** in the article's … menu.
- Each person's Settings has a **Write back** switch: off takes Write back, the paper plane and Write to away from them, while they stay one of your people.
