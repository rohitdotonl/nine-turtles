## 10 November 2026: Every wait is answered

- On a slow connection, a list says "Loading Outdoors…" where its articles will be, and an article says "Loading the article…" under its byline, instead of a blank space.
- Pressing Play on a video keeps its box on screen, saying "Starting the video…", until YouTube's player is ready.
- If you've been signed out, a star or mark that couldn't be saved says so, with Sign in, instead of a Try again that couldn't work.
- After you follow someone, their list says "You're following …" with Undo.
- On a computer, pressing s, m, w or n with no article chosen says "Choose an article first. j chooses the first one."
