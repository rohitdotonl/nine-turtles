## 5 November 2026: Clearer icons, round two

- On a phone, Previous and Next article are now arrows up and down, so Previous no longer looks like Back.
- The sidebar's "All" list is now called **All articles**, with a new icon.
- Starred's star in the sidebar stays an outline when you're on it; a filled star now only means an article is starred.
- In Settings, Email has its own icon (a person with an @) and Your data a storage box.
- The podcast player's skip buttons match the other icons' line, and the › at the end of rows is one size everywhere.
