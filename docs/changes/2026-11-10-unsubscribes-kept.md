## 10 November 2026: Unsubscribes kept 30 days

- Unsubscribing from someone is no longer final after a few minutes: for 30 days they wait under **Recently unsubscribed**, at the end of Settings › Everyone you follow, with **Bring back**, which brings them back with their articles and what you'd read.
- What an assistant unsubscribes from is listed there too ("Unsubscribed by an assistant"), instead of in Connected assistants.
- If you chose "Remove them too" when unsubscribing, those starred articles and notes still go once Undo has passed.
