## 28 October 2026: A calmer, simpler look

- The article list has no lines between articles any more: a little more space parts them, and the publisher's name reads a shade stronger than the time.
- In the sidebar, "Folders", "Feeds" and each folder's name are in small spaced capitals, and an open folder's feeds have a faint line down their left.
- A rule is now written as one sentence: "When a new article has ___ in its title, by any author, from all feeds, then mute it."
- The Aa panel is simpler: one quiet line per setting, with three small swatches for light, dark and match device.
