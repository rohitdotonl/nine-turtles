## 28 October 2026: A calmer, simpler look

- The article list has no lines between articles any more: a little more space parts them, and the publisher's name reads a shade stronger than the time.
- In the sidebar, "Folders" and "Feeds" are in small spaced capitals. A folder's name starts where the icons of Unread, Starred and All start, and its feeds sit under it with a faint line down their left. The show-or-hide arrow waits at the row's right end, and the whole row works as the toggle: clicking a closed folder opens it and shows its articles, clicking it again closes it.
- Newsletters show just their name, without the sender's email address, in the sidebar and the article list.
- A rule is now written as one sentence: "When a new article has ___ in its title, by any author, from all feeds, then mute it."
- The Aa panel is simpler: one quiet line per setting, with three small swatches for light, dark and match device.
