## 19 November 2026: Faster on a slow phone connection

- Opening nine turtles asks the server for everything its first screen needs at once: on slow mobile data, reopening went from 2.7 s to 1.9 s.
- Unread and Saved, and articles kept on your device for reading offline, open at once instead of waiting for the server: about 0.15 s instead of 0.8 s on slow data.
- Fonts stay on your device between visits, so text no longer flickers into its typeface, and each reopening downloads 14 KB instead of 69 KB.
- Settings and other pages you rarely open load just after the first screen, so the app starts with less to download; opened before they've arrived, they say "Loading Settings…" instead of showing an empty page.
