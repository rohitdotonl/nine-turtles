## 10 November 2026: Less, but better

- **Search** has no options panel any more. The field says where it looks ("in: Tech"); press its × to search everything. To search only starred articles, search from Starred. The number found is said once.
- **Settings** is shorter: sort order is in each list's More (…) menu, and text size and font are in **Aa** above any article, beside the text they change. Settings keeps Unread and the page colour.
- **Edit** and **Everyone you follow** lose lines that only repeated what the screen already said. "+ Add another place" now says what it adds: "+ Add their newsletter, videos or podcast".
- **The welcome**: the address field and its three examples sit together; "New to feeds? Start here · Bring your list from another app…" share one line at the foot.
- **One layout button**: the list's own button (which looked just like the sidebar's) is gone. Press F, or choose **Full-screen reading** in an article's More menu, to hide the sidebar and list together; the one button brings them back.
