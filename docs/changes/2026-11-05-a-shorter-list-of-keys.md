## 5 November 2026: A shorter list of keys

- The keyboard shortcut list is now two calm columns that fit without scrolling, sorted by what matters most. Write back is second.
- Cut keys: `O`, `[`, `]`, `Shift E`, `G` then `F`, `N` and `Shift N`. Home and End still work.
- Full-screen reading is now `F`, and row names carry their letter where they can, such as View original (it was Open original) and Refresh (show new articles).
- `Shift J` and `Shift K` now walk every row of the sidebar, lists and folders included, so you can jump from folder to folder and read everyone in one. `E` expands or collapses the folder you're on.
- `N` now adds a note (it was `Shift H`), and `G` then `N` goes to Notes.
- The first-time tip now says "Press J to read. Press W to write back."
- Keys look the same everywhere and on every computer: capitals, and "Shift" as a word instead of ⇧.
- Typed search filters (`is:`, `feed:`, `folder:`, `after:`, `before:`) are gone. Searching is typing words; everything else is in Search options.
