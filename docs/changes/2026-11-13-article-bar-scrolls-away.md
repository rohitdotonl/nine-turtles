## 13 November 2026: The article's buttons make room for the writing

- On a computer, the buttons above an article (View original, Aa, Star, Write back, More) now scroll away with its title, so the whole pane is the writing while you read. Their keys still work all the way down.
- At the end of an article, Star and View original are there again, above Write back.
- "Link copied." and the like now show at the foot of the article, where you'll see them.
- On a phone, nothing moves by itself; the Aa sheet now says that a tap on the text hides the bars.
