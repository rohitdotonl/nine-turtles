## 3 November 2026: The weekly email

- Settings › Account has a new row under Email: **Weekly email**, Off by default, or Weekly (Saturday morning) or Twice a week (Wednesday and Saturday mornings), at 08:00 your time.
- It's one plain email: the people you read who wrote something you haven't read yet, their titles with a line each and a link to the writing. Never a number. If nothing is new, nothing comes.
- It goes only to a confirmed email address, so nobody can have it sent to someone else.
- Its last line has a link that turns it off with one press, even when you're signed out.
