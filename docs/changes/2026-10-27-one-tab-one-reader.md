## 27 October 2026: A tab left open never acts for the next reader

- If someone else signed in on the same browser (say, with an emailed link) while your tab stayed open, that tab could still save its waiting marks, such as a star made offline, to their account. Now nine turtles refuses anything that tab sends, and the tab says "Someone else signed in on this browser. Reload to continue." with Reload. Marks it left waiting on the device are never sent as the new reader's.
