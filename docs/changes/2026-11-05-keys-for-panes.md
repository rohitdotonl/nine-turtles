## 5 November 2026: Keys for Edit, folders and panes

- E opens Edit for the person, publication or folder you're in, and O opens or closes a folder (E used to).
- The arrow keys ← and → move between the sidebar, the list and the article; ↑ and ↓ move inside the one you're in: the sidebar's rows, the list's articles, or scrolling the article. In a text field, a menu or the podcast player they work as always.
