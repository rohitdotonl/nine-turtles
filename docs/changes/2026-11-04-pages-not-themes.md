## 4 November 2026: Themes named by the page they give

- One setting, **Page**, replaces the four themes and Appearance: **White**, **Paper** (a warm cream page), **Night**, or **Match device** (White by day, Night by night), which is where everyone starts.
- In the Aa panel the page is four small swatches, each with its name under it. In Settings it sits in Look and feel, where Appearance was.
- Each page has one color for the unread dot and search matches: ink blue on White, old-ink brown on Paper, pale blue on Night.
- "Font, theme and line width" is now **Font and line width**. Links in articles are the text's own color on every page (Plain's blue links went with Plain).
- Original, Plain, Fountain pen and Signal are gone. A device that had chosen one opens on Match device, keeping its font, size and width.
