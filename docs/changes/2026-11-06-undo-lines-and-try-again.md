## 6 November 2026: Every Undo line works like the list's note

- The lines that say what you just did in Everyone you follow, Hiding, Passkeys and Newsletters now look and work like the note at the bottom of the list: the words on the left, then Undo and a × to close it, as underlined words rather than a bordered button. Each goes when you do something else there, press ×, or leave the page.
- A failure on those lines is in red with the warning sign.
- Anything that couldn't load now has a Try again button where trying again helps: the article list, Everyone you follow, Not working, Hidden articles, Hiding, Connected assistants and Passkeys.
