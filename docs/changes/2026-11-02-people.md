## 2 November 2026: People

- Each person you read is now one row in the sidebar, under **People**, however many places they have: their blog, their YouTube channel, their newsletter. Their list holds everything they made, newest first, each labelled Blog, Video or Newsletter.
- Beside their list, their page: their name, their places with links to their own sites, and since when you follow them.
- **Add another place** on their page joins someone you already follow, or a new address, into one person. If two places are on the same website, nine turtles asks once whether they're the same person; it never joins them by itself.
- **Edit feed** is now **Edit person**, with their places in it, and a folder you made for one person can become that person with one tick (**This is one person**).
- Every join and separation can be undone straight afterwards. Places you never join look exactly as before.
