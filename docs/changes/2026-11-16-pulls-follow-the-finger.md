## 16 November 2026: Pulling to the next article follows your finger

- In an article on a phone, a pull past the end now moves the article with your finger, and the next one comes up under it, named with its writer (or feed). Past the top, the previous one comes down the same way.
- Once you've pulled far enough it says "Let go for the next", and letting go carries straight on into it; a short pull slides back.
- A quick flick counts, as it does sideways.
