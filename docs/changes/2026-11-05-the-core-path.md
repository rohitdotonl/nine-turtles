## 5 November 2026: The core path

- Adding someone now asks one question: "Maya Chen is…", with their site, their newest title and two tiles, A person or A publication.
- The add field is "Their website's address"; a site with no feed suggests writing to them to ask for one.
- Lists name a publication's writer: "Lena Fischer · Garden Letters · 1 Nov". A summary says "Only the beginning is shared here."
- Write back is a solid button with a paper plane and uses full names. Their page has the same button, "Write to …", and no longer shows the muted line or an Email row.
- Edit is shorter: Kind of source, Check for new articles, Open articles on the website and how often they post are gone (with what readers had chosen). Someone with one source gets a quiet "+ Add another place".
- A folder chosen in Edit opens in the sidebar, and unsubscribing from the last person in a folder removes the folder too (Undo brings both back).
