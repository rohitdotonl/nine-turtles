## 11 November 2026: Write back in Gmail, a simpler box, quotes like a letter

- If your email lives in Gmail or Outlook.com in the browser, press **Write in Gmail** (or Outlook.com) under Write back once. The email opens there in a new tab, and that computer remembers it.
- The box that asks for an address has one clear button. "Look on their site" is now a link in its sentence.
- Highlighted words start your email in “quotation marks” instead of "> " signs.
