## 10 November 2026: Ways back

- Disconnecting an assistant in Settings now has **Undo**: it stops at once, and Undo brings the same connection back, with no need to connect it again from inside the assistant.
- After importing a file of feeds (OPML or YouTube subscriptions), "Wrong file? **Undo the import**" takes away exactly what that import added, and nothing you followed before.
- **Mark all as read**'s note now names the list ("Marked 3 articles as read in Reading Room.") and its Undo follows you to the next list, until you do something else that can be undone.
