## 9 November 2026: Dates in words, honest article endings, tidy lists

- An article's date now says "Today", "Yesterday" or the weekday ("Thursday") for the past week, then "20 Oct", instead of a clock time like "06:00". Hover over a date to see the exact day and time.
- Whole articles no longer end with "Only the beginning is shared here." That note now appears only when the feed really sends just the start, and its link says "Read the rest on {site} ↗".
- On a computer, a numbered or bulleted list in an article lines its words up with the paragraphs, its numbers sitting in the margin.
