## 4 November 2026: Add from anywhere in one tap

- Settings › Add from anywhere now has an **Add to nine turtles** button to
  drag to your computer's bookmarks bar, instead of an address to copy
  (Copy is still there for anyone who can't drag).
- On iPhone and iPad, a ready-made Shortcut replaces the steps to build one,
  once its link is in the app.
