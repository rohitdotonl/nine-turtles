## 2 November 2026: Write back

- Every article of someone you follow ends with **Write back to {their name}**. It opens your own email app with their address and "Re: " and the title filled in. nine turtles never sends or reads that email.
- The address comes from their feed or newsletter when it gives one. When it doesn't, you type it once and it's kept on their page.
- Their page shows the address; Edit person can change it. Write back is in the article's More menu too, and on the keyboard as `w`.
