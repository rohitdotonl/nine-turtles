## 3 November 2026: Feeds found in more places, and easier to choose

- A website that doesn't name its feed in its pages is now often followed anyway: nine turtles also tries the site's home page and the usual feed addresses, one at a time, only when you ask.
- When a site offers several feeds, each one shows its newest title and how often it posts, so you can tell the writing from the comments. Repeats of the same feed are left out, and if only one is left, it's followed straight away.
- The website's feed finder shows the same.
