## 4 November 2026: Clearer icons

- Twelve icons in Settings are redrawn as outlines with less clutter, each one easy to tell apart at a glance: Feed health is a heart with its beat, Newsletters an envelope with a feed's sign, Hiding an eye with a line through it, Install the app a phone with an arrow down, and Unread a page with a new dot.
- The brand sheet now has seven rules for icons, so new ones follow the same thinking.
