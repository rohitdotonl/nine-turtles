## 19 November 2026: The rest of the speed check

- A list you open just after reading or saving an article no longer waits for that to be saved first.
- A long list of people on Home or in the sidebar shows its first rows at once and the rest a moment later.
- The sign-in and sign-up pages load only for those who need them, so the app itself is a little smaller.
- Names that are cut short with "…" are measured once, not every time a list opens.
- The articles kept for reading offline are chosen in two quick rounds instead of eight.
