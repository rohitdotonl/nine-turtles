## 10 November 2026: Watch your list arrive

- After bringing your list from another app, Unread says how many feeds are still to check ("Bringing in your list: 5 feeds still to check.") instead of "Nothing unread.", and fills in by itself as each feed is read, until you open an article.
- In the sidebar, each feed not read yet says "checking…" after its name, and takes the writer's own name as soon as it's read.
