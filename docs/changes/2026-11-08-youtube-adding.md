## 8 November 2026: Following from YouTube, fixed and plainer

- Pasting a YouTube video's link no longer ends in "The feed couldn't be added": adding now gives up plainly after a minute instead of being cut off, and Follow uses the channel nine turtles just looked at rather than asking YouTube again.
- Channels with a video that has no description (Rick Astley's, for one) can be followed.
- A video asks "Follow {channel}?" and shows the channel's name, its newest video and its youtube.com/@ address, not the feed's technical address.
- A playlist offers Only "…" (chosen) or Every video.
- YouTube channels start as a publication; you can change it to a person.
