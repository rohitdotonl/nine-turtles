## 11 November 2026: Your reading, built to last

- **Download everything** in Settings › Your data replaces the three exports. It gives one .zip that opens as a folder: open its index.html in any browser, with or without nine turtles or the internet, to find who you follow and every article you starred or highlighted, each with its text, link and your notes. The OPML, JSON Feed and Markdown files are inside too, for other apps.
- **On an iPhone or iPad, the whole app follows your Text Size setting,** as Mail and Messages do: lists, buttons and Settings grow with it, not only the article. Aa still sets the article's size on top.
