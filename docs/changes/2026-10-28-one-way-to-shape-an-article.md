## 28 October 2026: One way to shape an article

- Behind the scenes, the screen, sync apps, AI assistants and the starred and collections downloads each had their own copy of how to read an article and take trackers out of older ones. They now share one copy.
- One small difference you might notice: a downloaded starred or collections file no longer carries tracking pictures or tracking tags on links for articles stored before October's privacy change, matching what the screen already showed.
