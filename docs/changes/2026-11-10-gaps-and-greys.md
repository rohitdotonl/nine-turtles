## 10 November 2026: Every gap on the scale, four greys

- Every space between things in the app now comes from one short list of sizes, so the same relationship always gets the same space. Most changes are 2px: a field's name now sits 8px over its field, cards and grouped rows have 16px inside on every side, and a list's rows have 24px above and below.
- The app's 22 slightly different greys are now four, each with one job: a fill, a line, a strong mark and an edge. Fields share the search field's fill, and their edges are a little clearer.
- On a computer, an article and a person's page keep a little more paper beside the text.
- The fine line under a highlight, a searched word and the welcome's example links is a little darker, so it is always clear to see, not only the tone behind the words.
- The automatic check now refuses any gap or grey that isn't in the design rules, instead of only counting them.
