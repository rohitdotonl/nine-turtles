## 17 November 2026: Reading on never jumps to the next article by itself

- On a phone, scrolling quickly to an article's end now stops there; it no longer carries on into the next article. To go on, pull again from the end.
- A pull to the next (or previous) article has to go a little further, and a quick flick no longer counts.
- A list you flick up or down now settles with a row at the top, never half a title. With reduce motion on, it stops where it stops, as before.
