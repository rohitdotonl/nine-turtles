## 3 November 2026: Notes and highlights

- Choose words in an article and press **Highlight** (or <kbd>H</kbd>): they're drawn in soft pencil, and listed at the article's end with your notes on them.
- Write a note on a whole article (More › Add a note…, or Shift+H), and a note on each person, on their page.
- **Notes** in the sidebar lists everything you marked; search finds your notes' words, and `is:noted` shows only marked articles.
- On a video or podcast, a note starts with the time playing; press it to go back there.
- Without a connection, highlights and notes are kept on the device and sent once you're back, with a quiet line until then.
- Unsubscribing asks whether to keep what you starred or marked there.
- Settings › Your data › Export notes and highlights saves them as Markdown. AI assistants can read and edit them; other apps never see them.
