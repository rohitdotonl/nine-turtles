## 9 November 2026: Other scripts read as they should

- nine turtles works out an article's language from its letters when the feed doesn't say, or says the wrong one, so screen readers use the right voice.
- Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Hebrew articles read right to left, title and all.
- Hindi, Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese and Japanese get more room between lines, and no fake italics.
- Names and titles in other scripts keep their own direction in the list, sidebar and byline; notes and searches type in any direction.
- A new Help page: Read in other languages.
