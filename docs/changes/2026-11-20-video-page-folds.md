## 20 November 2026: A calmer video page

- A YouTube video now waits in a box drawn in ink, with just Play: no title repeated inside it and no extra line. Pressed, it turns charcoal while YouTube's player starts.
- Under it, **Chapters ⌄** and **Description ⌄**, both closed, so sponsor lines and links stay out of the way. Open one and the other closes; your choice is remembered for the next video on that device.
- Chapters read like a book's contents, names first and times quiet at the end; the one playing is in semibold.
- A long link in a description shows just its site, such as higgsfield.ai.
