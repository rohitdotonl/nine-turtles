## 9 November 2026: Every list opens the same quiet way

- Unread now opens just like All articles and every other list. On a phone, the newest article is simply the first row, with its dot; there's no bigger title, paragraph or Start reading button above the rest.
- On a computer, with nothing open, the reading area shows "Time to read something beautiful." until you choose an article. Choosing the first one is still one press.
- "You were reading…", for the article you left part of the way through, is unchanged.
