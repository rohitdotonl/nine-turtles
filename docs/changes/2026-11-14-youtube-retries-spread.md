## 14 November 2026: Stuck YouTube channels are asked again through the day

- **YouTube channels that stopped updating get more chances.** YouTube turns nine turtles away in short spells, often at the same hour each day. A stuck channel used to be asked again exactly a day later, so it kept landing in the same spell. Now it's asked again within 3 to 6 hours, at a different time each time. Channels stuck for weeks should come back on their own once YouTube answers (decision 0273).
