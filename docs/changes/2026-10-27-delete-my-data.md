## 27 October 2026: Delete my data

- Settings › Account has a new **Delete data…** for every reader, you too. It empties your account (feeds, folders, articles, stars, collections, saved searches, rules) and keeps you signed in. It offers your downloads first and asks once; there's no undo, but the nightly backups still hold it.
- **Delete account…** (already there for every reader but you) now offers Export collections too before deleting.
