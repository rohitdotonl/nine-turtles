## 10 November 2026: One system, written down

- The whole look of the app (spacing, text sizes, titles, labels, icons, corners, shadows, the top of a phone screen) is now one table in the design rules, and an automatic check stops any change that drifts from it.
- Every screen below home on a phone starts the same way: Back at the very top, then the title at the same height and size, so nothing jumps as you move between screens.
- A group's name is one label everywhere: the sidebar's bold words, in place of the small grey capitals in Settings, the welcome, a person's page and the Aa panel.
- Icons come in two sizes, with the same line weight at both; buttons and fields share one corner, panels and sheets another, and everything that floats one shadow.
- A person's name and the welcome's title are now the same size as every other title (24px).
