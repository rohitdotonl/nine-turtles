## 6 November 2026: Sign-up keeps accounts private, and opens your inbox

- Signing up never says whether an email already has an account. It always
  says "Check your email"; if the address already has an account, that
  email says so and how to sign in, instead of sending a link.
- Up to 100 people can sign up in an hour (was 20), so a busy day doesn't
  turn anyone away.
- "Check your email" now has a quiet **Open Gmail** (or Outlook, iCloud
  Mail, Proton Mail) link when your address is one of those.
