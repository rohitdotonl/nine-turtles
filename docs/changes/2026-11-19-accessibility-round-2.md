## 19 November 2026: Names in full at the largest text

- At an iPhone's largest text, people's names on Home and who wrote an article in a list now show in full, on a second line when needed, instead of being cut short with "…".
- At the largest text, a list's Search and More icons stay a comfortable size and no longer touch the screen's edge.
- On a phone, Settings' Help link is easier to tap.
- Screen readers no longer read some articles' byline boxes out as tables.
