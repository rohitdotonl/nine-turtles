## 19 November 2026: Everything fits at the largest text

- At an iPhone's largest text, every screen now fits: rows and buttons rearrange, titles never split mid-word, and nothing runs off the edge. A new automatic check opens every phone screen at twice the size.
- Pressing Tab past Text and page closes it, so the keyboard never lands on something hidden under it.
- At 400% zoom, Everyone you follow's Choose bar no longer covers the rows.
- Footnote numbers are easier to tap, and the assistant key field is a full-size row.
- Screen readers jump to the list and article as the page's main part, and the "One person?" and "isn't working" boxes have headings. On the website, long code lines wrap on small phones.
