## 1 November 2026: Our own design principles

- nine turtles has eight design principles of its own, "Nothing pulls" first (decision 0133). They replace the ones that came with Apple Books.
- A principle can be broken only on purpose, and every break is written down with its reason.
- One system for spacing, type sizes and names; no feedback through motion.
- The goal: cited for taste and coherence, recognisable from one screenshot.
- Colors, type, the accent, the turtles, the brand idea and the tiebreaker are decided from mockups next.
- Nothing in the app changed.
