## 17 November 2026: The article's top and end, tidied

- On a computer, Save and View original are no longer repeated at the end of an article: they're always in the bar at the top.
- The ◆ that ends an article now sits on its own line, centred.
- More room above an article's title, on every screen.
- On a computer, the title in the top bar is in the title's own typeface, and slides up into the bar as you scroll past the title.
