## 14 November 2026: An article chosen beside someone's page now opens

- **A bug fixed:** on a computer, with a person's or publication's page (or their settings) open beside their list, choosing an article in the list marked it read but left their page showing, so the article never appeared. Now the article takes the page's place, just as if you had closed the page first: a click leaves the keyboard on the row, Enter puts it on the article's title, and the browser's Back brings their page back. Phones and "Read one thing at a time" are unchanged.
