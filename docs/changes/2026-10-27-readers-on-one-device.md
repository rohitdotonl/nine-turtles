## 27 October 2026: One reader's offline marks no longer land on another's account

- When someone opened an emailed link (sign in, new password, new email) on a device another reader had used, that other reader's offline copies stayed on the device, and their stars and read marks still waiting to be sent were saved on the new reader's account. Now, when nine turtles opens, it notices the reader changed and clears the device first.
- Signing out now clears the device straight away, so a dropped connection at that moment can't reopen the signed-in screen.
