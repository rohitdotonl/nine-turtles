## 6 November 2026: Change your password in Settings

- **Settings › Account** has a **Password** row: press **Change…**, type
  your current password and a new one. Your other devices are signed out.
- **Delete account…** and **Delete data…** now ask for your password, so a
  device you left signed in can't delete anything.
- Every email with a link now opens and ends the same way.
