## 3 November 2026: People and publications

- Everyone you follow is now a person or a publication. The sidebar lists them under two headings, People and Publications; folders hold both. nine turtles guesses which from the feed, asks when you add someone, and lets you change it in Edit.
- Press a name at the top of their list (or press I) to open their page beside the list: what they are, which folder, since when, their sources and their email. Edit on that page holds everything: name, email, sources, folders, and Unsubscribe from all their sources at once, with Undo.
- "Add another place" is now "+ Add a source". It shows a site's feeds to choose from, and asks which folders to keep when the two are in different ones. A source's own settings end in "Take out of …", with Undo.
- "Add feed" at the foot of the sidebar is now "+ Add" ("Add a person or publication").
- nine turtles no longer asks "Is this the same person?". You decide who is who.
- New addresses: /people/12 and /publications/7. Old /feeds/12 links still work.
