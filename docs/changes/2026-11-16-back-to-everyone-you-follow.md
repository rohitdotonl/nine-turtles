## 16 November 2026: Back where you came from

- **Fixing a broken feed brings you back to Everyone you follow.** Open someone from the list of feeds that aren't working and press Unsubscribe: you're back on Everyone you follow, with "Unsubscribed from …" and Undo, instead of on Unread. Close (×, Escape, or ‹ on a phone) goes back there too, and the list of feeds that aren't working stays open, so you can see to the next one (decision 0279).
