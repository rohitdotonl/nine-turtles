## 16 November 2026: Back where you came from

- **Closing someone's page takes you back where you opened it.** Opened from the list of feeds that aren't working, Close (×, Escape, or ‹ on a phone) goes back to Everyone you follow, the list still open and your place kept, and Unsubscribe lands there too, with "Unsubscribed from …" and Undo at the top, instead of on Unread. Opened from an article's byline, Close goes back to that article (decision 0284).
