## 6 November 2026: Everyone you follow, on one page

- Settings › Your feeds and Feed health are now one page, **Everyone you follow**. Anyone not working is at the top, with Find new address and Unsubscribe; Retry is gone, since nine turtles keeps trying by itself.
- Below them, everyone A to Z, one row per person (someone followed in several places is one row), with People and Publications apart. Each row shows the title of their newest piece and its day, or "Quiet since …" after six months, and opens their page.
- Edit on that page is now called Choose…; it still moves several to a folder or unsubscribes from them in one go.
