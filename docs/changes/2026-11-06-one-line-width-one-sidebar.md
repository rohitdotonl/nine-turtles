## 6 November 2026: One line width, one sidebar, shorter words

- **Font** is now on Settings itself, beside Page and Text size. The line width choice is gone from Settings and from Aa: every article has the same comfortable width.
- **Show in the sidebar** is gone. The sidebar always shows Unread, Starred and All articles, and Notes once you've written one.
- **Keep articles for reading offline** is gone from Your data: every device keeps your newest unread and starred articles by itself. Signing out still deletes them.
- Shorter words under Look and feel, Hiding and Pause for a while, and every list now ends "That's everything."
