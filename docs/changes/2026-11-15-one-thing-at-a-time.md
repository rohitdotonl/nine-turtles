## 15 November 2026: Read one thing at a time, for everyone

- **On a computer, nine turtles now reads the way a phone does.** With nothing open you see the sidebar and the list, its rows in a calm centred column. Open an article and it has the whole window, with **‹ Unread** (the list's name) at the top left; that, Escape or the browser's Back takes you back to the list just as you left it. A person's page fills the window the same way. This is for every reader, with nothing to switch on (decision 0275).
- **The trial switch is gone** from Settings, and so is full-screen reading (the F key and its menu item): an article always has the whole window now.
- **The list no longer has a width to drag.** The sidebar's edge still drags as before.
- **No empty reading area.** "Time to read something beautiful." and the one-time tip "Press J to read. Press W to write back." lived there, so they're gone; "You were reading …" now sits at the top of the list, as on a phone.
- **In the list, J and K choose an article; Enter opens it.** While reading, J and K still go to the next and previous article.
- **What matters while you read is said at the article's foot:** Undo or Try again for that article, and "nine turtles was updated. Reload to continue." when a tab is left open across an update.
