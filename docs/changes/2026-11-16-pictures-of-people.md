## 16 November 2026: A picture beside each byline

- **A face for every writer.** A small round picture now sits beside the byline under an article's title, and a bigger one tops each person's or publication's page. It's the picture their feed gives, else their website's own icon, else their initials (decision 0285).
- **Your own picture for anyone.** On their page, press their picture and choose **Choose a picture…**; **Use the automatic picture** goes back. Both have Undo. Only you see it.
