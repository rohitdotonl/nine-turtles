## 26 October 2026: Your Readers page, and fair limits

- Settings › Readers › **All readers** opens a page only you see: how many readers, feeds and articles there are, how big the database is, whether backups and email are on, anything that needs a look, and each reader's email, when they joined, how many feeds they follow and the day they were last here. Never which feeds, and nothing about what anyone reads.
- Each reader can follow up to **1,000 feeds**. Adding one more says so calmly; an OPML file that would go past it imports nothing.
- Feeds whose readers have all been away for a month are checked **once a day** until one of them comes back, which is kinder to publishers and to the server.
