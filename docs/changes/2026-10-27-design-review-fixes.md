## 27 October 2026: Small fixes from the design review

- Only Unread ends with "You're all caught up." Every other list ends with
  "That's everything.", and search results with "No more results."
- On a small phone, the welcome's "Paste a website or feed URL" hint steps
  down a size so it's never cut off.
- The podcast player: the length beside the speed steps aside once an
  episode starts; the 15 and 30 in the skip buttons are larger; on a phone,
  the Now playing bar steps aside while the same episode's own player is on
  screen.
- The link at the end of a summary-only article is now a full-height row,
  "Read on {publisher} ›" ("Read the episode page on {podcast} ›" for an
  episode).
- The keyboard starts on the list's title, not the sidebar's name; a title
  never shows a focus ring; and "Skip to articles" is the first Tab stop.
- "Articles", not "items", everywhere: "Marked 8 articles as read", "Show
  older articles", "New articles · press r".
- Recently read has quiet day headings: Today, Yesterday, the weekday, then
  the date.
- The chosen side of every segmented control (Inbox / River, Light / Dark
  and the rest) is now easy to see: a thin edge by day, a strong fill by
  night, checked automatically at 3 : 1.
- On a window about 1024px wide the article list is a little wider (352px),
  and it's never dragged narrower than 280px.
- The sidebar's "Other feeds" is now just "Feeds".
- Search results are titled "Results in All" (or the feed or folder), and
  the filter suggestions float over the list, so the results never move.
- Each feed on the feed health page is now a card: its name, what happened, the address tried and when it last worked, then Find new address (the main button), Retry and Unsubscribe.
- An empty collection says what collections are for: "Articles you put
  here stay, even after a year."
