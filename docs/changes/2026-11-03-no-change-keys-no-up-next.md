## 3 November 2026: Fewer settings in the way

- Every keyboard shortcut stays, but keys can't be changed any more: Change keys… and Settings › Keyboard are gone. If you had chosen your own keys, the `?` list says so once, and they're back to the standard ones.
- Up Next is gone: you play the episode you opened, and when it ends it says "Finished." Your place in each episode is still kept, and the player, the Now playing bar and the lock-screen controls stay.
