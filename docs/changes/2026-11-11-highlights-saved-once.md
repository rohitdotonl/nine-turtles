## 11 November 2026: A highlight is saved once

- Reloading or closing the page in the instant a highlight is being saved no longer saves it twice: when it's sent again, nine turtles knows it already has it.
- Changes to highlights and notes left waiting on your device are sent as soon as you open an article again, not only when the connection comes back, and a note written on a waiting highlight, or its removal, always reaches your account.
- Unsaved notes on different words in one article are each kept; a second one no longer replaces the first.
- Words with an unsaved note on them, highlighted with h or Highlight, take the note with them, so pressing Save doesn't highlight them a second time.
