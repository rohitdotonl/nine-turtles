## 10 November 2026: Easier to reach on a phone

- In an article on a phone, the More (…) menu now starts next to your finger: **Share** (or **Copy link**) is nearest the button and **Hide articles about…** is furthest away.
- Editing a person or publication on a phone now finishes the way + Add does: an **×** at the top in place of Cancel, and **Done** along the bottom of the screen, where your thumb is.
