## 17 November 2026: Titles centred and sized by their length

- An article's title is centred, like a book's chapter. Short titles are set large; long ones smaller, with more room between lines, so a long newsletter title no longer fills a phone's first screen.
- Under it: the writer's picture, "by" and their name in the reading font, and the day on its own line, small and faint, so the name leads (decision 0294).
