## 6 November 2026: Errors you can see, hear and retry

- "Your feeds couldn't be loaded" and "This article couldn't be loaded" are now read out by screen readers, and each has a **Try again** button instead of asking you to reload the page or wait.
- Every red error line now has its warning sign, so none relies on colour alone (Settings, Readers, Rules, Assistants, Passkeys).
- Two wording slips fixed: "Notes need a connection" and "Your browser didn't offer to install it".
