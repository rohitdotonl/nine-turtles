- The plan now says what "YouTube as a first-class citizen" means: paste any YouTube link to follow the channel, Shorts and live streams left out unless you switch them on, bring your YouTube subscriptions from Google, chapters you can press, a folded description, picking up where you stopped, and nothing YouTube shows after a video ends.
- Videos sit in Unread with everything else, count as read once opened, and still show no picture until you press Play.
