## 4 November 2026: The blog checkup

- A new free page on the website for people who write a blog: [nineturtles.app/blog-checkup/](https://nineturtles.app/blog-checkup/), "Is your blog easy to follow and write to?"
- Paste a blog's address and it shows, in words, whether there's a feed, whether the feed names its writer, and the ways a reader can write to them: a contact page (enough), an email address in the feed (best), and a "Reply by email" link on each post (optional).
- Under anything missing, how to fix it on Bear Blog, WordPress, Ghost or your own site, the blog's own platform first.
- It visits only the page given, the blog's home page and one contact or about page, and shares the feed finder's limits, so it never hammers anyone's site. It's in the website's footer and in the Write back Help page.
