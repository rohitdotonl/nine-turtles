## 28 October 2026: One way to mark, rename and unsubscribe

- Behind the scenes, marking articles (read, unread, starred, muted),
  renaming a feed or changing its folders, and unsubscribing now each work
  through one piece of code, whether you do it on the screen, from a sync
  app such as Reeder, or through an AI assistant. Before, each had its own
  copy.
- Nothing you see or do in the app changed: an assistant's unsubscribe
  still keeps the feed 30 days, and a sync app's still leaves earlier ones
  their ten minutes.
