## 14 November 2026: Three screens made calmer, from the design critique

- **Their page is two pages now.** A person's or publication's page is about them only: their name, where they publish, Write to (now an outlined button, so their name leads), and one card holding your note and your highlights. One row at its foot, **Settings**, opens their settings as a page of their own, with ‹ and their name to go back. The settings themselves are the same and still change as you touch them. `E` opens them directly (decision 0267).
- **One line after Write back.** On a computer, pressing Write back now adds one line, "Opening your email app…", ending in **Didn't open?**. Press that to see Write in Gmail, Write in Outlook.com and Copy their address. A phone is unchanged (decision 0265).
- **Everyone you follow is one press from home.** It has a row of its own in the sidebar, under All articles (on a phone, on Home), and is no longer inside Settings. Its old address still opens it (decision 0266).
- **A bug fixed on the way:** the line under Write back was being drawn in the wrong typeface, far below the button. It now sits small and close under the address, as designed.
