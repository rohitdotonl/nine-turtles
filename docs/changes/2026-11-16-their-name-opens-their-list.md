## 16 November 2026: A writer's name opens what they've written

- In an article, pressing the writer's name under the title now opens their list: everything they've written, newest first. "‹ Article" at the top takes you back to where you were reading.
- At the top of their list, their picture sits before their name. Press either one to open the page about them.
- An article's ⋯ menu has **Info about** (their name), which opens that page directly. The I key still does the same.
