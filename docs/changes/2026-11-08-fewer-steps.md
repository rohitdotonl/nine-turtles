## 8 November 2026: Fewer steps for what you do most

- Right-click an article in a list (on a phone, press and hold it, or press Shift+F10) for its own small menu: Star, Mark as read or unread, Write back, and Info about the writer, without opening it.
- At the end of a list that still has unread articles, "Mark all as read" waits under "That's everything."
- Write back is now a paper-plane button beside Star at the top of an article (in the bottom bar on a phone), for people only; it's no longer in the More menu.
- The writer's name under an article's title opens their page, and the I key opens the chosen article's writer's page from any list.
