## 11 November 2026: One page for someone

- A person's or publication's page is now one page: no Edit button, no Cancel or Done, and no separate page for a source's settings.
- Their name is set large, as an article's title is, with where they publish under it as links to their sites, then Write to, your note and your highlights.
- Their settings sit at the foot as Settings' rows, and each changes the moment you touch it: Name ("Saved." under it), This is, Folders (opening in place), Pause, Hide articles about…, and, for someone with several sources, Take out on each source's row.
- The email address is changed with Change, right where it's shown under Write to.
- On a computer an X at the top right closes the page; Hide articles about… brings you back to it; their name under an article is underlined so it reads as a link.
- Old Edit addresses and the E key open the page with Name ready to type.
