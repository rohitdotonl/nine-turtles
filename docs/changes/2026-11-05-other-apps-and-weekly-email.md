## 5 November 2026: No more syncing with other apps; the weekly email is once a week

- Settings › Other apps is gone: Reeder, NetNewsWire and other feed reader apps can no longer sync with nine turtles, and their app passwords were deleted. Help's questions page says why, with "If you miss it, write to hello@nineturtles.app."
- The weekly email is now one switch in Settings › Account: on (Saturday mornings) or off. Twice a week is gone; anyone who had chosen it gets it on Saturdays, and their next email says so once.
