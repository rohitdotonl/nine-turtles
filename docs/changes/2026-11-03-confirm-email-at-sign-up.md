## 3 November 2026: Signing up confirms the email address

- Signing up now sends a short email with a link. The account is made only when that link is opened, the password chosen at sign-up is typed and **Confirm email** is pressed, so nobody can sign up with someone else's address (decision 0154).
- After pressing Sign up, the page says **Check your email**, with Send again and a way back to fix a mistyped address.
- Everyone who signed up before this counts as confirmed, and nothing changes for them.
