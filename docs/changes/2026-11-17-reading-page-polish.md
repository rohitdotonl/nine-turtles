- On a computer, pictures and code use a little more of the width (never blurry), and captions are small and centred.
- Videos wait on a dark screen that says nothing loads from YouTube until you press Play; chapters are quieter, in two columns; the podcast player has a fine frame; times show plain zeros.
- Code keeps whole lines and shows when it scrolls.
- The top bar on a computer is two and two, ‹ and View original ↗ on the left, Save and More on the right (Copy link moves into More); it stays put, nothing in it moves, and once you've scrolled past the title it shows the title in its exact middle (decision 0299).
- Write back lives at the end of the article (and on W), smaller and softer, with no email address under it; link posts end with a clear "Read the rest" button.
