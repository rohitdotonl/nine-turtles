## 19 November 2026: Speed, round two

- **A bad signal no longer means a blank screen.** nine turtles opens at once, and if the server hasn't answered within 2.5 seconds, it shows the articles already kept on your phone, saying "The connection is slow." Newer articles arrive once the connection catches up.
- **Reopening is quicker on slow phone data,** because the app asks for your articles while it is still starting.
- **Tapping right after opening no longer waits** behind the articles being saved for reading offline. That saving now happens in small pieces and pauses while you tap.
- **People and folders open a little sooner,** because nine turtles starts asking for them the moment your finger touches the name.
- A new version of nine turtles now reaches you at your next tap after opening, not the moment you open it.
