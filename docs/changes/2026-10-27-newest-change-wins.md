## 27 October 2026: A quick second change is never undone by a slow first one

- Changing a reading setting (sort, counts, reading mode and the like) twice quickly, or moving an episode in Up Next twice, could briefly show the older choice, and if the first save failed it undid the second too. Saves now go to nine turtles in the order you made them, and a failed one only goes back when nothing newer is on its way.
- A feed you just unsubscribed from can no longer flash back into the sidebar from a slow, older answer; the same for saved searches and collections.
- "New items" no longer appears just after a refresh already brought them in.
