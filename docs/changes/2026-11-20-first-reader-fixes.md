## 20 November 2026: Adding your first feeds fits a short window

- Adding your first person on a short window (a 1280×720 laptop, a big iPhone): once they're found, the "Hello" greeting steps aside, so the question and **Done** stay in view.
- **New to feeds?** keeps its buttons in view: on a small phone the names no longer cover **Add all five**, and on a short laptop the sheet scrolls under **Cancel** and **Add all five** instead of cutting them off.
- **Highlight…** in an article's More menu and **Write back about a line…** in Write back's arrow menu are gone again (the owner's call); Notes' picture of how to highlight stays (decision 0326).
