## 27 October 2026: Simpler sign-in pages

- Sign-in and sign-up now show each field's name above it: **Email**, then **Password**.
- A small **Forgot?** beside Password replaces "Email me a sign-in link" and "Forgot your password?". It emails a link that signs you in; once in, you can choose a new password, or press **Not now**.
- **Sign in with a passkey** is now a quieter button under Sign in, and "New here? Sign up" sits at the foot. Sign-up ends with "Have an account? Sign in".
- A password link emailed before this change still works for its hour.
