## 6 November 2026: Following someone no longer floods Unread

- When you follow someone, only their **five newest articles** land in Unread. Older ones are on their page, already marked read.
- Everything they post after you follow them arrives unread, as before.
- The same goes for feeds brought in from a file (Feedly, Inoreader and so on). Feeds you already follow are unchanged.
