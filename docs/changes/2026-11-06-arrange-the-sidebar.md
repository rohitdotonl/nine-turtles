## 6 November 2026: Arrange your folders, and drag people into them

- Folders now stay in the order you choose. Drag a folder up or down in the sidebar; a new folder goes at the bottom, and renaming keeps its place. The order is the same on every device.
- Drag a person or publication onto a folder to move them there. Hold Option on a Mac (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) to keep them in both. Drag them onto People or Publications to take them out of the folder.
- On a phone, press and hold a row until it lifts, then move it. A quick swipe still scrolls.
- **Edit**, beside Folders, gives every row a handle: press a folder's to move it up or down, or a person's to choose their folder. It works with the keyboard and screen readers too.
- A folder left with nobody in it goes, and every change has Undo at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Folders, People and Publications are now bold words instead of small capitals.
