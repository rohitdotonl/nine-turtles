## 17 November 2026: The podcast controls sit in the middle, and Write back has room

- A podcast's Back 15, Play and Forward 30 now sit in the middle of the player, with the episode's length on the left and the speed on the right (decision 0301).
- At the end of an article on a phone, Write back has the same space above it as below it, instead of sitting tight under the last words.
