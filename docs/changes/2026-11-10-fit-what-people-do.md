## 10 November 2026: Working the way you already expect

- Typing in **Find a person or folder** at the top of the sidebar now offers **Search articles for “…”** (or press Enter), which searches every article for those words.
- On a computer, Command Z (Ctrl Z on Windows) undoes, just as U does.
- On a phone, Settings has **‹ Home** at the top left instead of an X, like every other screen. Everyone you follow, Hidden articles and All readers have **‹ Settings**, **‹ Hiding** and **‹ Readers**.
- While an article is open, the browser tab (and your browser's History) shows its title.
