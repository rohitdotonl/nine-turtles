## 3 November 2026: The phone's home screen stays put

- On a phone (or a narrow window), going back to the home screen right after opening nine turtles could bounce you to the welcome a moment later, if your feeds were still arriving. It now only opens on the welcome; once you've moved, you stay where you went.
- This was also why one browser test ("at 200% zoom nothing overlaps") failed now and then. A new test holds the feeds back on purpose, so this can't come back unnoticed.
