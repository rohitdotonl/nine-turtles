## 3 November 2026: An even politer feed checker

- No website blocks nine turtles: the feeds that kept failing had moved, gone or lapsed.
- nine turtles now checks a feed at most once an hour (was every 30 minutes), waits as long as a site says its feed stays the same (up to a day), and never asks one site for two feeds at the same moment.
- A new page, nineturtles.app/publishers, tells site owners how nine turtles treats them, and the checker names it in every request.
