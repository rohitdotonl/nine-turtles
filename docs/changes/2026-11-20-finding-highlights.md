## 20 November 2026: Highlighting is easier to find

- An article's More menu (⋯) starts with **Highlight…**. With words chosen it highlights them; with none it says "Choose any words to highlight them." Pressing H with nothing chosen says the same.
- The arrow beside **Write back** starts with **Write back about a line…**, which says how: choose words, then press Write back about this.
- Notes, while empty, says "Nothing here yet. Select text in any article to highlight it or add a note." over a small picture of chosen words and their menu (decision 0326).
