## 18 November 2026: Replies you started, and a calmer page for each person

- A person's page now lists **Replies you started**: each article you pressed a way back at (Write back, Gmail, Outlook.com, Copy address or Comment), with the day. Never what you wrote, and never whether you sent it. Remove a line with the bin (Undo brings it back), or switch it off with Record replies in Settings › Reading.
- Their settings are the cog at the top right of their page, instead of a row at its foot. On a computer, ‹ at the top left takes you back to their list, as it does from an article, in place of the ×.
- Your note on them has the note icon beside it, and each highlight the new highlight marker.
- Write back at an article's end is in the full ink at the usual size, like Write to on their page.
