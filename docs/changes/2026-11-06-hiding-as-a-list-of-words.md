## 6 November 2026: Hiding is a list of words

- **Settings › Hiding** now starts with one field: type a word, choose **For a week**, **For a month** or **For good**, and press **Add**. Unread articles already about it are hidden at once, with **Undo**.
- Each word is one row saying whose articles it hides and for how long, with **Remove**. The on/off switch, the Edit sheet and the "Also hide…?" question are gone.
- A word for a week or a month stops hiding by itself when its time is up.
