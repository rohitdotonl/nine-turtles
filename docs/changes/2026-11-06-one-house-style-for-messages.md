## 6 November 2026: Every message in one house style

- Every message the app shows after you do something, or when something fails, now follows one set of rules (written in BRAND.md under "Messages"). What you did is a full sentence ("Starred.", "Unsubscribed from Mira Okafor."). A failure says what couldn't be done and what to do next ("The star couldn't be saved. Try again in a moment.").
- Undo always says "Undone." (no more "It's back.", "The passkey is back." or "Your old address works again."), and a failed Undo always says "That couldn't be undone. Try again in a moment."
- Without a connection, every place starts the same way: "You're offline." and then what that means there.
- Messages no longer use technical words like OPML, Google Takeout, "private network" or a command's name.
