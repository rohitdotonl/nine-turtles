## 6 November 2026: Confirm sign-up with one press

- Opening the "Confirm your email" link in the browser you signed up in
  no longer asks for your password: press **Confirm email** and nine
  turtles opens. On another browser or device, it still asks for your
  password, so nobody else can claim your address.
- After confirming, nine turtles opens straight away, with no extra
  "You're signed up" screen.
