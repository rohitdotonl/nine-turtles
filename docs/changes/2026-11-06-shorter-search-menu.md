## 6 November 2026: A shorter search menu

- **Search options** now holds two things: where to search (**This list** or **Everything**) and **Starred**. To search one person or folder, open it, then search.
- **Unread**, **With notes** and the dates are gone from the menu. Search from the Unread or Notes list to look only there; the sort order puts the oldest or newest first.
