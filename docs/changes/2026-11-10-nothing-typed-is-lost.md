## 10 November 2026: Nothing you type is thrown away

- A note you started and didn't save (on an article, on highlighted words or on a person) is kept on your device. Come back and its box is open again with your words and "Not saved yet." **Discard** throws them away, with Undo.
- A note that couldn't be saved stays in its box with your words, a line under them saying so, and **Try again**.
- Changes on **Edit** for a person or publication, and in **Edit folder** and **Add**, are kept if you leave by Escape, Back or a stray click. Coming back shows them with "Not saved yet. Going elsewhere keeps these changes here for you." **Cancel** throws them away.
