## 4 November 2026: A moved feed stays moved, and a steadier weekly email test

- When you moved a feed to its new address on Feed health while nine turtles happened to be checking the old address, that check could finish afterwards and put the feed back on the "Having trouble" list. Now a check of an address the feed no longer uses is ignored.
- The automatic test for the weekly email choice in Settings now waits for each choice to be saved before reloading the page, so it no longer fails at random.
