## 18 November 2026: Fewer words

- About twenty of the app's longer lines are shorter, mostly in Settings, saying the same in fewer words.
- An article with only a title in its feed now ends with "Read it on {site}", instead of pointing at View original.
- The sidebar's Keys is now just the keyboard icon; Copy, a passkey's Rename and the quiet Remove buttons are icons too, still named for screen readers.
