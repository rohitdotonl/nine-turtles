## 10 November 2026: Getting out of the way

- **On a phone, tap the text to hide the bars.** While reading, one tap on the words puts the top bar and the bottom toolbar away, so the page is only the writing. Tap again (or reach the end of the article) and they're back.
- **One search field at a time on a computer.** The list's search now waits behind a magnifier beside More, as on a phone; press it or `/` to search. The list starts right under its title.
- **One dot in the sidebar.** Folders, people and publications no longer carry an unread dot; Unread shows one while anything is new. Articles in a list still show theirs.
- **A quieter Write back.** At the end of an article, Write back is an outlined button instead of a solid dark one, so the writing stays the strongest thing on the page.
