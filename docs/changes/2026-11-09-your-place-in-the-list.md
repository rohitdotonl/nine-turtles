## 9 November 2026: Your place in the list, a clearer sidebar and a reordered phone toolbar

- An article you left part of the way through now says so in its row: "Half read", "Two-thirds read", "Nearly finished" and so on, after the date. The words go once you finish it or scroll back to its top. A place kept on another of your devices says "Part read".
- In the sidebar, only the people in a folder are set a step in; people and publications in no folder now line up with the folders.
- On a phone, the bar under an article now reads More, Aa, Write back, Star, then a thin line, then Previous and Next on the right, where your thumb rests.
