## 1 November 2026: Design first, and a warmer voice

- Design is now the most important thing in nine turtles: agents bring up design problems on their own, and try design choices as mockups (a safe and a bold one) before anything changes (decision 0132).
- Accessibility is never traded, whatever the design.
- The "book or feed" tiebreaker is retired; a new one gets tested first.
- The voice: plain, warm, a little dry, never selling. "I" when Rohit speaks, never "we". Honesty is a public promise; easter eggs are found, never shown.
- The new design principles are a draft, still being discussed (`docs/RnD/design-principles.md`).
- Nothing in the app changed.
