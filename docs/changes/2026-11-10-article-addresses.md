## 10 November 2026: Every article has its own address

- While an article is open, the address bar shows its own address. Reload, bookmark it or send it to yourself, and it opens straight to that article.
- Command-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) or middle-click an article in the list to open it in a new tab.
- Right-click an article in the list for **Open in a new tab** and **Copy link** (on a phone, press and hold it for **Share**). The link you copy or share is always the writer's own page.
