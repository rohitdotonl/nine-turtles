## 16 November 2026: An article's next and previous are pulls

- In an article, pull up past its end for the next one, which rises from below; pull down past its top for the previous one, which comes down from above.
- While you pull, the card names the article and who wrote it (or the feed's name).
- A pull down at the top no longer refreshes the page on Android, so it always opens the previous article.
- Swiping left in an article no longer opens the next one: sideways is only for going back (and a row's marks), up and down for moving through articles.
