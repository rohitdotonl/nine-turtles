## 3 November 2026: YouTube, first-class

- Paste any YouTube link into Add a feed: a channel follows at once; a video asks "This video is by … Follow …?"; a playlist asks whether to follow the whole channel or only the playlist.
- Shorts and live streams are left out. Edit person for a YouTube channel has Include Shorts and Include live streams to bring them in.
- Settings › Your data › Import YouTube subscriptions… takes the file Google Takeout gives you.
- A video's page shows its title in the grey Play box, its chapters as buttons, and a long description folded. It picks up where you stopped, on any device, and closes back to the box when the video ends, so YouTube's suggestions never show.
- Videos show a small screen mark in the list.
