## 27 October 2026: Anyone can sign up

- Sign-up is now always open. Anyone can press **Sign up** on the sign-in page, type an email and a password, and they're in at once. No invite link and no confirmation email.
- Settings › Sign-up (Closed, Invite link, Open) is gone, and so are invite links.
- At most 20 new accounts can be made in any hour, as before, to keep bots out.
