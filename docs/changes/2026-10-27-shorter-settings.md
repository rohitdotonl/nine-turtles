## 27 October 2026: A shorter, clearer Settings

- Settings now shows only what you change often: Reading, and Look and feel (Appearance, Text size, and a row for font, theme and line width).
- Everything else is a row ending in › that opens its own page: Your feeds, Feed health, Show in the sidebar, Your data, Keyboard, Add from anywhere, Rules, Newsletters, Other apps, Connected assistants, Passkeys and, for you, Readers. Each page has "‹ Settings" at the top; Escape and the browser's Back go back too. Nothing was removed.
- Each row has a small grey icon, lined up with its name.
- A setting's name is a little bolder, with a short description straight under it, in Settings and in Edit feed.
