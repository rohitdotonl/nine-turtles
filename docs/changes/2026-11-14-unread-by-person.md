## 14 November 2026: Unread, by person

- **Unread shows each person once.** Their name heads what's new from them, with each title's day beside it (under it, when the title needs the room), instead of a small grey name under every title. The name is the bold line; the titles under it are in plain type, each new one still with its dot. Someone with three new articles takes four lines, not six. People come first, the most recent at the top; publications follow under **Publications**. Every title is still one press away, j and k go title to title, and everything else (marking, Undo, the row's menu) works as before. Only Unread changes: All articles, Starred, Notes, folders, a person's own list and search stay in order of time (decision 0271).
- **A check made steadier:** one automatic browser test (a video that takes a while to start) loaded YouTube's real player and failed when that was slow. It now uses a stand-in page, as the other video tests do, so the checks never leave their own computer.
