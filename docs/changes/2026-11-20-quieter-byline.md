## 20 November 2026: A quieter byline

- Under an article's title the writer's name now stands alone ("Daring Fireball"), or as "John Gruber / Daring Fireball" when someone writes in another's publication, instead of "by John Gruber in Daring Fireball". Screen readers still hear "by" and "in".
- The slash has a little more room on each side, so the two names stand clearly apart.
