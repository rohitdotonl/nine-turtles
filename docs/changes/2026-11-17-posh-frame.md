## 17 November 2026: A finer frame for every article, video and episode

- Above each title, a small line in spaced capitals says where it's from, and "Video" or "Episode" when it's one. The byline under the title now just names the writer, so nothing is said twice.
- Videos and the podcast player are wider on a computer, and the player is drawn with two fine lines instead of a grey box. Its 15 and 30 no longer touch their arrows.
- A video's description and chapters sit a step smaller, so the video leads.
- An article ends once, with the ◆, then Save and View original in small capitals.
