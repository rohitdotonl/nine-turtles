# 15. Folders and managing feeds

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- Folders are the only grouping. A feed can be in more than one folder.
- **Their settings** (it was Edit feed, then Edit person, then Edit; decisions 0163 and 0254): a person's or publication's settings are at the foot of their own page, with no Edit mode (section 36 has the whole of it; `/people/3/edit` still opens the page there). Each changes the moment it's touched, with no Save, Cancel or Done: rename them (kept on Enter or on leaving the field, "Saved." under it), choose their folders, pause them, and unsubscribe. A failing source's Find new address sits under their name. **Folders** says which they're in ("1 and Friends", "None"); pressed, it opens in place to a checklist (44px rows, each folder ending in a tick box, a rounded square, filled with a check when they're in it and empty when they aren't, square rather than round because they can be in several folders), each tick counting at once, whose first row is "New folder…" (above the folders, so it's found without scrolling past them, decision 0261), a text box (Enter makes it, with them in it, and opens it in the sidebar, point 14). At the foot, **Unsubscribe from Alpha** as words in oxblood (the words say what it does, so oxblood is never the only sign): it leaves every source of theirs, removes their articles, keeps the starred ones, and can be undone straight afterwards. A problem saving appears in oxblood words after a warning mark, under the settings.
- **A feed that isn't working** (decision 0106): only while Everyone you follow lists it as not working, their page shows, under their name, the same quiet line as its list, naming the source ("garden.example hasn't worked since 12 Oct."; above its list, "This feed hasn't worked since 12 Oct."), and a text button, **Find new address**, which works exactly as in Everyone you follow, in place under the line. Using an address moves the feed at once, keeping its name, folders, articles and stars; the line then says so. If others follow the old address too, the feed gets a new number and the page goes to it, still the same page: the line still says so. A working source shows nothing of this.
- **How often it posts** (decision 0105) is no longer said anywhere: not on their page (the owner's core path, point 11, decision 0183), nor in the choice among several feeds (section 3), the website's feed finder or assistants' feed health (decision 0206). How often nine turtles checks each feed still follows how often it posts (section 11).
- **Each source's own settings** (decision 0082), among their settings on their page, built as Settings' rows (each name on the left with a short description straight under it in the muted grey, decision 0120): **Pause for a while**, a switch, for every source of theirs at once (a YouTube channel also has **Include Shorts** and **Include live streams**, section 38, among their settings for a channel on its own, and under its row in **Sources** for one of several). There's no reading mode of its own: Unread is on or off for everything at once (section 10, decision 0172). Its description says what it does: "Their new articles arrive already read while this is on." It starts off, and changes as it's switched. Following a feed again after unsubscribing starts it afresh. Three settings went in the owner's core path (point 11, decision 0183), their saved choices with them: **Kind of source** (Blog, Video, Newsletter or Podcast, which nine turtles still reads from the feed for its own labels), **Check for new articles: Automatic · Daily** (every feed is checked on its own rhythm, section 11) and **Open articles on the website** (every article opens here).
- **Pause for a while** (decision 0095), for a holiday or a noisy week: while it's paused, nothing from it piles up. Its new articles still arrive (it's checked as usual, so nothing it posts is missed) and appear in its own list, its folders, All articles and search, but already read, so they never reach Unread or give it a dot. What was unread before you paused it stays unread. So a pause isn't forgotten, it also says so where the reader already looks (decision 0239): above its list and on their page, one quiet line in the second ink, "Paused since 23 Oct. Their new articles arrive already read." ("Paused today." on the day), with **Resume** after it, an underlined word that switches Pause for a while off. For a person with several sources, each paused one has its own line, named ("Garden Letters: Paused since 23 Oct. Its new articles…"). No color, no badge, no count. Resumed, the line says "Resumed." (the keyboard stays on it) until the reader goes elsewhere; if it can't be saved, "The pause couldn't be ended. Try again in a moment." in oxblood with the alert icon. Switching it off brings back only its newest three articles from while it was paused to Unread (your rules act on them again, so one a rule marks as read stays read); the rest stay read in its list. Pausing is yours alone: other readers of the feed see no change. Following it again after unsubscribing starts it unpaused.
- Viewing a folder, **Edit folder…** in its list's More (…) menu opens a sheet over the list (`/folders/2/edit`; Cancel goes back unchanged; Escape or a press on the dimmed page goes back too, keeping what was changed for next time, which then opens with "**Not saved yet.** Going elsewhere keeps these changes here for you." at the top; decision 0235) to rename it, make it one person (**This is one person**, section 36) or delete it, built the same way: Name, the tick box row, then one row with **Delete** in oxblood at the far left (its tooltip says the feeds stay) and Cancel and Save at the right end. On a phone it's an iOS page sheet (DESIGN.md → "One phone sheet"): an X (which stands for Cancel, so it throws changes away), Delete as an oxblood row of its own, Save at the foot.
- You can create, rename and delete folders. A folder is always made with someone in it (decision 0261): while adding them (+ Add's Folder, section 3), from Folders on their page, or from Move to…; there's no button for an empty folder. Two folders can't share a name. Deleting a folder never deletes its feeds.
- **The last person in a folder** (the owner's core path, point 15): unsubscribing from the last one in a folder removes the folder too. It leaves the sidebar at once, and Undo brings both back; the line still says only "Unsubscribed from Tomas Berg" with Undo. Once their 30 days are over, the folder is deleted with their folder pairs; until then Bring back brings both back. A folder that was already empty (one made for a move, say) isn't touched.
- Unsubscribing and deleting a folder happen at once and take you to Unread, where the line says "Unsubscribed from …" or "Deleted the folder …" with Undo (or `u`), for as long as section 8 says the note stays. Undo after deleting a folder brings it back with its feeds and its rules. Someone can also be dropped on **Unsubscribe** or **Pause** at the sidebar's foot (section 5, decision 0248): the line then shows there, and you stay where you were unless it was their list or page.
- Unsubscribing from a feed keeps its starred articles in Starred, and those you highlighted or wrote a note on; when there are any, Unsubscribe on their page first asks whether to keep them (section 42); the bulk Unsubscribe, Everyone you follow's cards and assistants keep them without asking. Undo brings the feed back whole, read history and folders included. After that, it's kept for 30 days under **Recently unsubscribed** (below), and Bring back, or adding it again, brings it back just as whole; after 30 days its other articles are gone for good from your lists (decision 0240). Following it again later starts it afresh, its newest five unread (section 3).
- **Several readers on one nine turtles** (decision 0086): your feeds' names, folders, settings and your read, starred and muted marks are yours alone. Another reader who follows the same feed shares only its articles, fetched once: unsubscribing removes only your marks and leaves their feed exactly as it was, and its articles go only once nobody follows it (anyone's starred ones stay). Following a feed someone else already follows brings in its stored articles: the newest five the publisher still lists unread, everything else read (section 3, decision 0194).

- **Everyone you follow** (decision 0186; it joins what were Your feeds, decision 0080, and the feed health page, decision 0071): in Settings, under **Your library**, an **Everyone you follow** row (ending in ›) opens a page of its own at `/settings/feeds` (the old `/settings/feed-health` opens it too), with **‹ Settings** on a line above its title (decision 0231; or Escape) back to Settings, with the keyboard on its row, and one centred column.
  - **Who could be either** (decision 0264, section 36) comes after who isn't working, while there's anyone nine turtles couldn't sort and the reader hasn't settled: a group headed "8 could be either" ("1 could be either"), with under it "Their feeds don't say who writes them. Tick the people, anyone you might write back to. The rest stay with Publications." Each is a tick row, A to Z: their name, then where they publish (their site, or "YouTube channel") and their newest title and day ("dynomight.net · “Scattered thoughts”, 26 Oct"). A tick counts at once: they're a person, the sidebar follows, and they join People below; unticked, they could be either again. There's no Save, and a ticked row stays where it is, ticked, until the page is left, so nothing moves under the finger. Under the rows, **The rest are publications** settles every one still unticked; the group goes, and the page's note says "6 are publications now." with Undo. They're listed only here, not under Publications too; while Choose… is on, this group hides and they're under Publications, to choose like anyone. The ticks are one group, named by the heading and described by the sentence under it.
  - **Who isn't working comes first**, worst first, in two groups under words: **Not working** (dead), then **Having trouble**; within each, the longest failing first. A feed is listed once two checks in a row have failed: one failure is usually a blip the next check clears. It's **dead** after 7 days of "not found", or 3 weeks of any failure, and still checked once a day in case it comes back. No status colors: the group's name says how bad it is (decision 0071). With nothing wrong, nothing shows here.
  - Each is a card (rounded, a tone off the page, a little apart; decision 0118): their name, a little larger and semibold; then one sentence, leading in bold with since when: "**Nothing has come through since 1 Nov.** The site says the feed isn't there any more. It may have moved." When a guard in front of the site turns nine turtles away, it says so: "The site's guard against bots turns nine turtles away. Only the site can let it in." (decision 0258). No address, no error codes, ever. Under a hairline, **Find new address** (the filled main button) and, at the right end, **Unsubscribe** (in the destructive oxblood words, with Undo straight afterwards, in its place); each at least 44px tall, and on a narrow screen Unsubscribe has a line of its own. There's no Retry: nine turtles keeps checking by itself. For a person (not a publication) in Not working, the sentence ends "Ines Duarte may not know.", and under the buttons **Write to Ines Duarte**, an everyday bordered button with the paper plane (the solid one stays Find new address), working as Write to on their page (section 37): it opens the email app when their address is known, or asks for it in place, the box's Write an everyday button too (decision 0212). Once Find new address has it working again, the sentence and Write to go. Under the group, a line: "Most come back on their own; nine turtles keeps trying." (Having trouble) or "Nothing has come through from these for weeks. They may have moved, or stopped writing." (Not working).
  - **Find new address** visits the feed's site once, because you asked: the home page the feed names, or else its address's own home page, and lists the feeds that page names (never guessing other addresses, decision 0016), leaving out the broken one and any you already follow. What it found shows in place, the same way wherever Find new address is offered (here, above a failing feed's list in section 6, and on their page; decision 0106): **one feed** is one plain question, "Use https://… instead?", with "Its name, folders, articles and stars stay." under it and **Cancel** and **Use it**; **several** are a choice of one (a round mark at each row's end, the first chosen) with **Cancel** and **Use this address**; **none** is a plain sentence ("The site doesn't name another feed. You could try again another day, or unsubscribe."). One status line says each step to screen readers ("Looking on the site…", the question, "Moving…", the result); the keyboard moves to **Use it** (which screen readers hear with the question) or to the chosen feed, and Cancel puts it back on the button that started it. Using one: the feed moves there and keeps its name, folders, articles, read marks and stars, and the card says "Working again." An address you already follow, or one kept for the stars of a feed you unsubscribed from, is refused with a plain sentence saying why; feeds are never merged quietly. If another reader follows the same feed, its address stays as it is for them: you follow the new address instead, with your name, folders, settings and rules for it, and every article you had and every mark (read, starred, muted) comes with you. Nothing of yours is cleared later; the old address simply leaves your list. Nothing moves while you look: a card stays where it is, saying what happened, until you open the page again.
  - **Then everyone, A to Z**, in two grouped lists as in the sidebar: **People**, then **Publications** (a group with nobody in it isn't shown), each headed by its count ("5 people", "1 publication"). One row per person, as in the sidebar: someone followed in several places (section 36) is one row under their name, in People, with their newest piece across every place and every folder any place is in; Choose moves or unsubscribes all their places together. Each row is two lines: their name, then, in the muted grey, the title of their newest piece and when it came ("“On walking”, 4 Nov", "“On walking”, today"; with the year when it isn't this one), or, after six months with nothing new, "Quiet since 14 Mar: “On walking”", or "Nothing written yet"; then their folders after a dot. A title the reader hid (a rule or Hide, section 27) isn't shown: the row says "Last wrote on 4 Nov" instead. No color or badge marks a quiet one: the words say it. A row opens their page (section 36), to write back, keep a note or edit. No line under the lists explains them: the chevrons and Choose… say it (decision 0241).
  - **Choose…**, as Edit in Apple Books, at the right of the first group's heading: each row gets a tick box (the same rounded square as the folders on their page), and **Choose all** / **Choose none** appears beside it; Choose… becomes **Done**. A bar stays at the page's foot while the list scrolls, solid in the page's color with a hairline above: **Unsubscribe** in the destructive oxblood words alone at its left, then, at its right, how many are chosen ("Choose who" with none) and **Move to…**, so the frequent action and the one that removes things sit at opposite ends, as in every sheet (decision 0217).
  - **Move to…** opens a sheet, "Move to a folder", with a choice of one, as a grouped list with a round mark at each row's end: **No folder**, **New folder…** (with a text box for its name, under it), then each folder (New folder… above the folders, as in + Add, decision 0261), none chosen at first, so Move never takes anyone out of their folders by default; pressing Move with none chosen says "Choose where they go first." in oxblood words after a warning mark (decision 0137). Each one leaves the folders they were in and goes to the one chosen. Cancel or Escape closes only the sheet. The same sheet opens from a person's handle in the sidebar's Edit, for that one person (section 5, decision 0207).
  - Moving and unsubscribing happen at once. A line above the lists says what was done ("Moved 3 to Later.", "Unsubscribed from 3.", naming them when there's one) with **Undo** and a **×**, as the list's note has (section 8), which the keyboard moves to, until your next action there (pressing Choose… or another move or unsubscribe ends it). Undo puts everyone back where they were in one go (a folder made just for the move goes too) or brings everyone back whole, as unsubscribing from one does. Choose ends after each action.
  - **Recently unsubscribed**, the last group (decision 0240): everyone you, or an assistant, unsubscribed from in the last 30 days, newest first, one row per person (a person's sources left together are one row): their name, then "Unsubscribed today · kept until 8 Dec" (or "Unsubscribed by an assistant 4 Nov · kept until 4 Dec") in the small muted line, and **Bring back** at the right. Under the group: "Kept for 30 days, with their articles, so you can bring them back." Bring back follows them again whole and says "Hal Brennan is back." with Undo, on the same line as the page's other actions; one already cleared says "… can't be brought back: they're already gone.", and the keyboard moves to the next Bring back. Bring back counts towards the 1,000 people and publications one reader can follow, and says so when it would pass them. A folder hidden because everyone in it is waiting here never blocks its name: renaming another folder to it takes its place, and Bring back lands in that one. The group isn't shown while it's empty.

**How to check it**
1. Put a feed in two folders. It appears under both.
2. Rename a folder: it keeps its place among the folders (decision 0207).
3. Delete a folder: its feeds remain (under other folders or the "no folder" group).
4. Unsubscribe from a feed, then undo: it's back with its read history.
5. Star an article, then unsubscribe from its feed: the article is still in Starred.
6. Open Settings › Everyone you follow, press Choose…, tick two people and Move to… a new folder: both are in it, and nowhere else. Press Undo: they're back where they were, and the new folder is gone.
7. Tick two people and press Unsubscribe, then Undo: both are back, with their read history.
8. In Everyone you follow, each row names their newest piece and its day; someone with nothing new for six months says "Quiet since …". People and Publications are listed apart, each with a line under its heading saying how nine turtles files them ("Filed here when one name signs everything they write, or the site is their own."; "Filed here when several names sign it, or it writes under its site's own name."), and a row opens their page.
9. On the page of a blog on its own, its settings: Name, This is, Folders, Pause for a while and Hide articles about…, then Unsubscribe, with no Edit, Cancel or Done; the email address is under Write to, with Change. No Kind of source, no Check for new articles, no Open articles on the website, and no line saying how often it posts.
10. On the page of a blog on its own, switch on "Pause for a while". When it next posts, the new articles are in its own list as read, Unread doesn't grow, and no dot appears beside it. Its list and its page say "Paused today. Their new articles arrive already read. Resume". Switch it off (or press Resume): its newest three new articles are in Unread, and the line goes.
11. Unsubscribe from the only person in a folder: the folder leaves the sidebar with them. Press Undo: both are back. Unsubscribe again and do something else: they're under Recently unsubscribed; Bring back brings both back.
12. Open the page of a blog on its own that Everyone you follow lists as not working: under its name, "blog.example hasn't worked since …" and **Find new address**. For a working feed, neither shows.
13. Follow a feed whose site later moves it (the browser tests use a made-up one). After two failed checks, it's at the top of Everyone you follow under "Having trouble", as a card with a plain sentence, Find new address and Unsubscribe (on a phone, Unsubscribe on its own line), and no Retry.
14. Press Find new address. When the site names one feed, it asks "Use … instead?": press Use it, and it works again, with its articles and stars. (When it names several, choose one and press Use this address.)
15. Open /settings/feed-health: Everyone you follow opens.
16. Unsubscribe from someone, then do something else: Settings › Everyone you follow lists them last, under Recently unsubscribed, "Unsubscribed today · kept until" a day 30 days on. Press Bring back: they're back in the sidebar with their read history.
17. Import a list with someone whose feed names no writer: Everyone you follow lists them under "1 could be either". Tick them: they're under People in the sidebar at once, and the row stays ticked. Press **The rest are publications** with others unticked: the group goes, the note says so, and Undo brings it back.
