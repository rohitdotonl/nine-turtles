# 15. Folders and managing feeds

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- Folders are the only grouping. A feed can be in more than one folder.
- **Edit** (it was Edit feed, then Edit person; decision 0160): a person's or publication's settings are on their own page, turned into Edit by its **Edit** button or `Shift+E` (`/people/3/edit`; section 36 has the whole of it). There you rename them, choose their folders, and unsubscribe. A source's own settings (below), **Add a rule…** (a link that opens Settings › Rules with a new rule set to that source; section 27) and Find new address sit in Edit itself for one source, and behind each source's › for a person with several. The folders are a checklist (a grouped list of 44px rows; each folder ends in a tick box, a rounded square, filled with a check when they're in it and empty when they aren't, square rather than round because they can be in several folders), whose last row is "New folder…", a text box (made when you press Done, with them in it). At the foot, **Unsubscribe from Alpha** as words in oxblood (the words say what it does, so oxblood is never the only sign): it leaves every source of theirs, removes their articles, keeps the starred ones, and can be undone straight afterwards. **Done** saves and turns the page back; **Cancel**, Escape or ‹ turns it back unchanged. A problem saving appears in oxblood words after a warning mark.
- **A feed that isn't working** (decision 0106): only while the feed health page lists it, a source's settings (or Edit, for one source) start with the same quiet line as its list ("This feed hasn't worked since 12 October.") and a text button, **Find new address**, which works exactly as on the feed health page (section 17), in place under the line. Using an address moves the feed at once, not on Save, keeping its name, folders, articles and stars; the line then says so, and the keyboard moves to the sheet's title. If others follow the old address too, the feed gets a new number and the page goes to it, still the same settings: the line still says so, and anything you typed stays. A working source's settings show nothing of this.
- **How often it posts** (decision 0105): in Edit, a quiet helper line under the name (13px, muted grey, as a field's helper line) says how often the feed posts, in plain rounded words: "Posts about 12 a day.", "Posts about once a day.", "Posts about 3 times a week.", "Posts about twice a month.", "Posts less than once a month.", "Hasn't posted in 4 months.", "Hasn't posted in over a year." or "Too new to tell." (fewer than 3 articles, or under two weeks since its oldest one). The yearly clean-up (section 23) removes old unstarred articles, so a silence of a year or more is only ever "over a year", and a feed with no articles left is counted from when nine turtles started fetching it ("Too new to tell." only in its first two weeks). No chart, no decimals, no judgement. It's worked out on the server from the feed's articles of the last 90 days, by their published time (or when they arrived, if the feed gives none), over those 90 days or the feed's own history if shorter. Its line is kept from the moment Edit opens, so nothing moves when the words arrive.
- **Each source's own settings** (decision 0082), in a source's settings page (or in Edit, for one source), as a grouped list, built as Settings' rows (each name on the left with a short description straight under it in the muted grey, decision 0120; a segmented choice at the right end, under them on a narrow screen): **Reading mode: Inbox · River** (section 10), **Check for new articles: Automatic · Hourly · Daily** (section 11), **Open articles on the website** and **Pause for a while**, each a tick box row (a YouTube channel also has **Include Shorts** and **Include live streams**, section 38; every source also has its **kind**, Blog, Video, Newsletter or Podcast, read from the feed and correctable, section 36). Each row's description says what it does: "New articles go to Unread." (or, for River, "Its articles skip Unread and show no dot."; while the whole app is in river mode, "The whole app is in river mode for now (Settings › Reading)."), "As often as it usually posts." (Hourly: "No more often than once an hour."; Daily: "No more often than once a day."), "Opens the publisher's page instead of the text here." and "New articles skip Unread until you untick it; then its newest three come back." (with since when, once paused). Each starts as every feed behaves (Inbox, Automatic, unticked), and Done keeps them. Following a feed again after unsubscribing starts them afresh.
- **Pause for a while** (decision 0095), for a holiday or a noisy week: while it's paused, nothing from it piles up. Its new articles still arrive (it's checked as usual, so nothing it posts is missed) and appear in its own list, its folders, All and search, but already read, so they never reach Unread or give it a dot. What was unread before you paused it stays unread. The helper line says since when ("Paused today.", "Paused on 23 Oct."). Unticking it brings back only its newest three articles from while it was paused to Unread (your rules act on them again, so one a rule marks as read stays read); the rest stay read in its list. Pausing is yours alone: other readers of the feed see no change. Following it again after unsubscribing starts it unpaused.
- **Open articles on the website**, for feeds that share only a summary: opening one of its articles (`Enter` or `o`, or a tap on a phone) opens the publisher's page in a new tab instead, and the article counts as read. Choosing it in the list (a click, `j` / `k`) still shows its summary beside the list, so moving through the list never opens tabs. Nothing is fetched from the website by nine turtles.
- Viewing a folder, **Edit folder…** in its list's More (…) menu opens a sheet over the list (`/folders/2/edit`; Escape, Cancel or a press on the dimmed page goes back unchanged) to rename it, make it one person (**This is one person**, section 36) or delete it, built the same way: Name, the tick box row, then one row with **Delete** in oxblood at the far left (its tooltip says the feeds stay) and Cancel and Save at the right end. On a phone it's an iOS page sheet (DESIGN.md → "One phone sheet"): an X, Delete as an oxblood row of its own, Save at the foot.
- You can create (from Edit's folders), rename and delete folders. Two folders can't share a name. Deleting a folder never deletes its feeds.
- Unsubscribing and deleting a folder happen at once and take you to Unread, where the line says "Unsubscribed from …" or "Deleted the folder …" with Undo (or `u`), until your next action. Undo after deleting a folder brings it back with its feeds, its rules and the saved searches limited to it, as Undo after deleting a collection does.
- Unsubscribing from a feed keeps its starred articles in Starred. Undo brings the feed back whole, read history and folders included; after your next action (or ten minutes) its other articles are gone for good from your lists. Following it again later starts it afresh, all unread.
- **Several readers on one nine turtles** (decision 0086): your feeds' names, folders, settings and your read, starred and muted marks are yours alone. Another reader who follows the same feed shares only its articles, fetched once: unsubscribing removes only your marks and leaves their feed exactly as it was, and its articles go only once nobody follows it (anyone's starred ones stay). Following a feed someone else already follows brings in its stored articles: the ones the publisher still lists unread, older ones read.

- **Your feeds** (tidying many feeds at once; decision 0080): in Settings, under **Your library**, a **Your feeds** row (ending in ›) opens a page of its own at `/settings/feeds`, built like the feed health page: the same top line with an X ("Close your feeds", or Escape) back to Settings, and one centred column.
  - It lists every feed A to Z in one grouped list, each on two lines: its name, then, in the muted grey, when it last posted ("Last posted today", "Last posted on 3 Oct 2025", or "Nothing posted yet") and the folders it's in. The group's small heading counts them ("42 feeds"). Out of Edit, a row opens that feed's list.
  - **Show**, a segmented choice above the list: **All feeds**, **Quiet for 6 months** or **Quiet for a year**. A quiet feed is one with nothing published for that long (counted from when you added it, if it has never posted). The feed health page lists broken feeds; this lists working feeds that have gone silent, so you can let them go. With none, it says "Every feed has posted within the last 6 months" (or year).
  - **Edit**, as in Apple Books, at the right of the list's heading: each row gets a tick box (the same rounded square as Edit's folders), and **Choose all** / **Choose none** appears beside it; Edit becomes **Done**. A bar stays at the page's foot while the list scrolls, solid in the page's color with a hairline above: how many are chosen, **Unsubscribe** in the destructive oxblood words, and **Move to…**.
  - **Move to…** opens a sheet ("Move 3 feeds") with a choice of one, as a grouped list with a round mark at each row's end: **No folder**, each folder, and **New folder** (with a text box for its name), none chosen at first, so Move never takes feeds out of their folders by default; pressing Move with none chosen says "Choose where they go first." in oxblood words after a warning mark (decision 0137). Each feed leaves the folders it was in and goes to the one chosen. Cancel or Escape closes only the sheet.
  - Moving and unsubscribing happen at once. A line above the list says what was done ("Moved 3 feeds to Later.", "Unsubscribed from 3 feeds.", naming the feed when there's one) with **Undo**, which the keyboard moves to, until your next action there (pressing Edit, changing Show or another move or unsubscribe ends it). Undo puts every feed back where it was in one go (a folder made just for the move goes too) or brings every feed back whole, as unsubscribing from one feed does. Edit ends after each action.

**How to check it**
1. Put a feed in two folders. It appears under both, with its unread dot in each.
2. Rename a folder: it moves to its new alphabetical position.
3. Delete a folder: its feeds remain (under other folders or the "no folder" group).
4. Unsubscribe from a feed, then undo: it's back with its read history.
5. Star an article, then unsubscribe from its feed: the article is still in Starred.
6. Open Settings › Your feeds, press Edit, tick two feeds and Move to… a new folder: both are in it, and nowhere else. Press Undo: they're back where they were, and the new folder is gone.
7. Tick two feeds and press Unsubscribe, then Undo: both are back, with their read history.
8. Choose "Quiet for 6 months": only feeds with nothing new for six months are listed.
9. In Edit for a blog on its own, tick "Open articles on the website" and press Done. Choose one of its articles and press Enter: the publisher's page opens in a new tab.
10. In Edit for a blog on its own, tick "Pause for a while" and press Done. When it next posts, the new articles are in its own list as read, Unread doesn't grow, and no dot appears beside it. Open Edit again: it says when you paused it. Untick it and press Done: its newest three new articles are in Unread.
11. Open Edit for a blog that posts every day: under its name, "Posts about once a day." For a feed added today with two articles: "Too new to tell."
12. Open Edit for a blog on its own that the feed health page lists: it starts with "This feed hasn't worked since …" and **Find new address**. For a working feed, neither shows.
