# 15. Folders and managing feeds

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- Folders are the only grouping. A feed can be in more than one folder.
- **Edit** (it was Edit feed, then Edit person; decision 0163): a person's or publication's settings are on their own page, turned into Edit by its **Edit** button (`/people/3/edit`; section 36 has the whole of it). There you rename them, choose their folders, and unsubscribe. A source's own settings (below) and Find new address sit in Edit itself for one source, and behind each source's › for a person with several. The folders are a checklist (a grouped list of 44px rows; each folder ends in a tick box, a rounded square, filled with a check when they're in it and empty when they aren't, square rather than round because they can be in several folders), whose last row is "New folder…", a text box (made when you press Done, with them in it), with the note "Type a name to make a folder." under the list (the owner's core path, point 13). A folder made or newly chosen there opens in the sidebar when you press Done, to show them in it (point 14). At the foot, **Unsubscribe from Alpha** as words in oxblood (the words say what it does, so oxblood is never the only sign): it leaves every source of theirs, removes their articles, keeps the starred ones, and can be undone straight afterwards. **Done** saves and turns the page back; **Cancel**, Escape or ‹ turns it back unchanged. A problem saving appears in oxblood words after a warning mark.
- **A feed that isn't working** (decision 0106): only while Everyone you follow lists it as not working, a source's settings (or Edit, for one source) start with the same quiet line as its list ("This feed hasn't worked since 12 October.") and a text button, **Find new address**, which works exactly as in Everyone you follow, in place under the line. Using an address moves the feed at once, not on Save, keeping its name, folders, articles and stars; the line then says so, and the keyboard moves to the sheet's title. If others follow the old address too, the feed gets a new number and the page goes to it, still the same settings: the line still says so, and anything you typed stays. A working source's settings show nothing of this.
- **How often it posts** (decision 0105) is no longer said anywhere: not in Edit (the owner's core path, point 11, decision 0183), nor in the choice among several feeds (section 3), the website's feed finder or assistants' feed health (decision 0206). How often nine turtles checks each feed still follows how often it posts (section 11).
- **Each source's own settings** (decision 0082), in a source's settings page (or in Edit, for one source), as a grouped list, built as Settings' rows (each name on the left with a short description straight under it in the muted grey, decision 0120), with no heading: **Pause for a while**, a tick box row (a YouTube channel also has **Include Shorts** and **Include live streams**, section 38). There's no reading mode of its own: Unread is on or off for everything at once (section 10, decision 0172). Its description says what it does: "Their new articles wait until you untick this." (with since when, once paused). It starts unticked, and Done keeps it. Following a feed again after unsubscribing starts it afresh. Three settings went in the owner's core path (point 11, decision 0183), their saved choices with them: **Kind of source** (Blog, Video, Newsletter or Podcast, which nine turtles still reads from the feed for its own labels), **Check for new articles: Automatic · Daily** (every feed is checked on its own rhythm, section 11) and **Open articles on the website** (every article opens here).
- **Pause for a while** (decision 0095), for a holiday or a noisy week: while it's paused, nothing from it piles up. Its new articles still arrive (it's checked as usual, so nothing it posts is missed) and appear in its own list, its folders, All articles and search, but already read, so they never reach Unread or give it a dot. What was unread before you paused it stays unread. The helper line says since when ("Paused today.", "Paused on 23 Oct."). Unticking it brings back only its newest three articles from while it was paused to Unread (your rules act on them again, so one a rule marks as read stays read); the rest stay read in its list. Pausing is yours alone: other readers of the feed see no change. Following it again after unsubscribing starts it unpaused.
- Viewing a folder, **Edit folder…** in its list's More (…) menu opens a sheet over the list (`/folders/2/edit`; Escape, Cancel or a press on the dimmed page goes back unchanged) to rename it, make it one person (**This is one person**, section 36) or delete it, built the same way: Name, the tick box row, then one row with **Delete** in oxblood at the far left (its tooltip says the feeds stay) and Cancel and Save at the right end. On a phone it's an iOS page sheet (DESIGN.md → "One phone sheet"): an X, Delete as an oxblood row of its own, Save at the foot.
- You can create (from Edit's folders), rename and delete folders. Two folders can't share a name. Deleting a folder never deletes its feeds.
- **The last person in a folder** (the owner's core path, point 15): unsubscribing from the last one in a folder removes the folder too. It leaves the sidebar at once, and Undo brings both back; the line still says only "Unsubscribed from Tomas Berg" with Undo. Once Undo's time is over, the folder is deleted with their folder pairs. A folder that was already empty (one made for a move, say) isn't touched.
- Unsubscribing and deleting a folder happen at once and take you to Unread, where the line says "Unsubscribed from …" or "Deleted the folder …" with Undo (or `u`), for as long as section 8 says the note stays. Undo after deleting a folder brings it back with its feeds and its rules.
- Unsubscribing from a feed keeps its starred articles in Starred, and those you highlighted or wrote a note on; when there are any, Unsubscribe in their Edit first asks whether to keep them (section 42); the bulk Unsubscribe, Everyone you follow's cards and assistants keep them without asking. Undo brings the feed back whole, read history and folders included; after your next action (or ten minutes) its other articles are gone for good from your lists. Following it again later starts it afresh, its newest five unread (section 3).
- **Several readers on one nine turtles** (decision 0086): your feeds' names, folders, settings and your read, starred and muted marks are yours alone. Another reader who follows the same feed shares only its articles, fetched once: unsubscribing removes only your marks and leaves their feed exactly as it was, and its articles go only once nobody follows it (anyone's starred ones stay). Following a feed someone else already follows brings in its stored articles: the newest five the publisher still lists unread, everything else read (section 3, decision 0194).

- **Everyone you follow** (decision 0186; it joins what were Your feeds, decision 0080, and the feed health page, decision 0071): in Settings, under **Your library**, an **Everyone you follow** row (ending in ›) opens a page of its own at `/settings/feeds` (the old `/settings/feed-health` opens it too), with the usual top line and an X ("Close everyone you follow", or Escape) back to Settings, with the keyboard on its row, and one centred column.
  - **Who isn't working comes first**, worst first, in two groups under words: **Not working** (dead), then **Having trouble**; within each, the longest failing first. A feed is listed once two checks in a row have failed: one failure is usually a blip the next check clears. It's **dead** after 7 days of "not found", or 3 weeks of any failure, and still checked once a day in case it comes back. No status colors: the group's name says how bad it is (decision 0071). With nothing wrong, nothing shows here.
  - Each is a card (rounded, a tone off the page, a little apart; decision 0118): their name, a little larger and semibold; then one sentence, leading in bold with since when: "**Nothing has come through since 1 November.** The site says the feed isn't there any more. It may have moved." No address, no error codes, ever. Under a hairline, **Find new address** (the filled main button) and, at the right end, **Unsubscribe** (in the destructive oxblood words, with Undo straight afterwards, in its place); each at least 44px tall, and on a narrow screen Unsubscribe has a line of its own. There's no Retry: nine turtles keeps checking by itself. For a person (not a publication) in Not working, the sentence ends "Ines Duarte may not know.", and under the buttons **Write to Ines Duarte**, an everyday bordered button with the paper plane (the solid one stays Find new address), working as Write to on their page (section 37): it opens the email app when their address is known, or asks for it in place (decision 0212). Under the group, a line: "Most come back on their own; nine turtles keeps trying." (Having trouble) or "Nothing has come through from these for weeks. They may have moved, or stopped writing." (Not working).
  - **Find new address** visits the feed's site once, because you asked: the home page the feed names, or else its address's own home page, and lists the feeds that page names (never guessing other addresses, decision 0016), leaving out the broken one and any you already follow. What it found shows in place, the same way wherever Find new address is offered (here, above a failing feed's list in section 6, and in Edit; decision 0106): **one feed** is one plain question, "Use https://… instead?", with "Its name, folders, articles and stars stay." under it and **Cancel** and **Use it**; **several** are a choice of one (a round mark at each row's end, the first chosen) with **Cancel** and **Use this address**; **none** is a plain sentence ("The site doesn't name another feed. You could try again another day, or unsubscribe."). One status line says each step to screen readers ("Looking on the site…", the question, "Moving…", the result); the keyboard moves to **Use it** (which screen readers hear with the question) or to the chosen feed, and Cancel puts it back on the button that started it. Using one: the feed moves there and keeps its name, folders, articles, read marks and stars, and the card says "Working again." An address you already follow, or one kept for the stars of a feed you unsubscribed from, is refused with a plain sentence saying why; feeds are never merged quietly. If another reader follows the same feed, its address stays as it is for them: you follow the new address instead, with your name, folders, settings and rules for it, and every article you had and every mark (read, starred, muted) comes with you. Nothing of yours is cleared later; the old address simply leaves your list. Nothing moves while you look: a card stays where it is, saying what happened, until you open the page again.
  - **Then everyone, A to Z**, in two grouped lists as in the sidebar: **People**, then **Publications** (a group with nobody in it isn't shown), each headed by its count ("5 people", "1 publication"). One row per person, as in the sidebar: someone followed in several places (section 36) is one row under their name, in People, with their newest piece across every place and every folder any place is in; Choose moves or unsubscribes all their places together. Each row is two lines: their name, then, in the muted grey, the title of their newest piece and when it came ("“On walking”, 4 November", "“On walking”, today"; with the year when it isn't this one), or, after six months with nothing new, "Quiet since 14 March: “On walking”", or "Nothing written yet"; then their folders after a dot. A title the reader hid (a rule or Hide, section 27) isn't shown: the row says "Last wrote on 4 November" instead. No color or badge marks a quiet one: the words say it. A row opens their page (section 36), to write back, keep a note or edit. A line under the lists says so: "Each one opens their page, to write back, keep a note about them, or edit how you follow them. Choose lets you move several to a folder, or unsubscribe from them, in one go."
  - **Choose…**, as Edit in Apple Books, at the right of the first group's heading: each row gets a tick box (the same rounded square as Edit's folders), and **Choose all** / **Choose none** appears beside it; Choose… becomes **Done**. A bar stays at the page's foot while the list scrolls, solid in the page's color with a hairline above: how many are chosen ("Choose who" with none), **Unsubscribe** in the destructive oxblood words, and **Move to…**.
  - **Move to…** opens a sheet, "Move to a folder", with a choice of one, as a grouped list with a round mark at each row's end: **No folder**, each folder, and **New folder** (with a text box for its name), none chosen at first, so Move never takes anyone out of their folders by default; pressing Move with none chosen says "Choose where they go first." in oxblood words after a warning mark (decision 0137). Each one leaves the folders they were in and goes to the one chosen. Cancel or Escape closes only the sheet.
  - Moving and unsubscribing happen at once. A line above the lists says what was done ("Moved 3 to Later.", "Unsubscribed from 3.", naming them when there's one) with **Undo** and a **×**, as the list's note has (section 8), which the keyboard moves to, until your next action there (pressing Choose… or another move or unsubscribe ends it). Undo puts everyone back where they were in one go (a folder made just for the move goes too) or brings everyone back whole, as unsubscribing from one does. Choose ends after each action.

**How to check it**
1. Put a feed in two folders. It appears under both, with its unread dot in each.
2. Rename a folder: it moves to its new alphabetical position.
3. Delete a folder: its feeds remain (under other folders or the "no folder" group).
4. Unsubscribe from a feed, then undo: it's back with its read history.
5. Star an article, then unsubscribe from its feed: the article is still in Starred.
6. Open Settings › Everyone you follow, press Choose…, tick two people and Move to… a new folder: both are in it, and nowhere else. Press Undo: they're back where they were, and the new folder is gone.
7. Tick two people and press Unsubscribe, then Undo: both are back, with their read history.
8. In Everyone you follow, each row names their newest piece and its day; someone with nothing new for six months says "Quiet since …". People and Publications are listed apart, and a row opens their page.
9. In Edit for a blog on its own: Name, This is, Email address (for a person), Folders and Pause for a while, then Unsubscribe. No Kind of source, no Check for new articles, no Open articles on the website, and no line saying how often it posts.
10. In Edit for a blog on its own, tick "Pause for a while" and press Done. When it next posts, the new articles are in its own list as read, Unread doesn't grow, and no dot appears beside it. Open Edit again: it says when you paused it. Untick it and press Done: its newest three new articles are in Unread.
11. Unsubscribe from the only person in a folder: the folder leaves the sidebar with them. Press Undo: both are back. Unsubscribe again and do something else: the folder is gone for good.
12. Open Edit for a blog on its own that Everyone you follow lists as not working: it starts with "This feed hasn't worked since …" and **Find new address**. For a working feed, neither shows.
13. Follow a feed whose site later moves it (the browser tests use a made-up one). After two failed checks, it's at the top of Everyone you follow under "Having trouble", as a card with a plain sentence, Find new address and Unsubscribe (on a phone, Unsubscribe on its own line), and no Retry.
14. Press Find new address. When the site names one feed, it asks "Use … instead?": press Use it, and it works again, with its articles and stars. (When it names several, choose one and press Use this address.)
15. Open /settings/feed-health: Everyone you follow opens.
