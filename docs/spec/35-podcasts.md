# 35. Podcasts

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

*Built 27 October 2026 (decision 0113), as the owner approved.*

**What it does**
- **A podcast is a feed like any other.** Follow it by pasting its feed (or its website) into + Add. An article whose feed item carries an audio file (an RSS `enclosure`, an Atom `enclosure` link or a JSON Feed `attachment` whose type is `audio/…`, or, with no type, whose address ends in .mp3, .m4a, .aac, .ogg or .opus) is an **episode**. nine turtles keeps only the file's web address (http or https), its type and size, its length (from `itunes:duration` or the feed's own media details) and its picture (the episode's, else the podcast's). **It never downloads, keeps or passes on the audio:** the reader's browser plays the publisher's file from the publisher's address, as YouTube videos play in YouTube's own player (decision 0072). A podcast followed before 27 October 2026 shows its episodes as episodes once its feed next changes (usually with its next episode): a feed that hasn't changed isn't read again.
- **In the list,** an episode's meta line ends with a small headphones icon and its length ("45 min", "1 hr 5 min"), after the time; screen readers hear "Episode, 45 min".
- **The player** sits under the byline, where a YouTube video's box would, in a calm panel in a faint tone of the text with softly rounded corners: **Back 15 seconds**, a round **Play / Pause** in the text color, **Forward 30 seconds** (the 15 and the 30 drawn inside their arrows large enough to read, about 11px, and each button's name in its tooltip), then the episode's length (only until it starts playing: then the times under the slider say it, so it steps aside) and the **speed** (1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2×; each press moves to the next, and this device remembers it). Under them, a slider for the position, with the time played on the left and the time left ("−12:34") on the right, in figures that keep their width. Every control is a real button or slider of at least 44px, named for screen readers; the slider says its time in words ("12 minutes 3 seconds of 45 minutes"). Nothing moves except the slider's mark and the times: no waveform, no animation. While the file starts, a quiet line says "Loading from the publisher…". If it can't be played: "This episode couldn't be played. Open the original to listen on the publisher's site." The show notes are the article's text; an episode with none ends with the closing rule. When the feed shares only a summary of them, the link under the closing rule says "Read the episode page on {podcast} ›" (section 7).
- **Keys:** none new. Space keeps scrolling the article, as everywhere (section 19); the player's buttons and slider answer to Enter, Space and the arrow keys only while the keyboard is on them.
- **It keeps playing while you read.** One episode plays at a time, for the whole app. Once one is playing (or paused), a slim **Now playing** bar sits along the bottom of the app, taking its own room so it never covers the list or the article: Back 15 seconds, Play / Pause, the episode's title and its podcast's name (a button that opens the episode) and **Close the player**. On a phone, while an article is open, it sits just above the article's toolbar, and it steps aside while the same episode's own player is on screen, so the episode never shows twice; scrolled past the player, or on another article, it's back. Closing it stops the episode and keeps your place.
- **Your place is kept, on all your devices.** Where you are is saved about every 15 seconds while playing, and whenever you pause, move the slider or leave nine turtles: on this device at once, and on the server, for you alone. Opening the episode anywhere shows where you stopped, and Play carries on from there; the newer of the two saves wins. Played (or paused, or moved with the slider) to within 30 seconds of its end, an episode is **finished**: it's marked read, and it starts from the beginning if you play it again. **Signing out** stops the episode, closes the bar and forgets this device's places, so whoever signs in next on it starts from nothing (as offline copies are deleted, section 32).
- **The lock screen and headphones** show the episode's title, its podcast and its picture, and their play, pause and skip buttons work (where the browser offers this, the Media Session API).
- **You play the episode you opened** (decision 0170). When an episode ends, nothing else starts: a quiet line says "Finished.". Up Next, a queue of episodes (decision 0113), was removed in step 5 of the owner's review; every episode stays in its podcast's list.
- **Offline** (section 32): a kept episode's notes show, and its player says "The episode plays here when you're back online."

**How to check it**
1. Add a podcast's feed. Its episodes show a headphones icon and their length in the list.
2. Open one and press Play: it plays, the times count, the length beside the speed steps aside, and the Now playing bar appears at the bottom (on a phone, only once the episode's own player is off the screen). Move to another article: it keeps playing.
3. Pause, then reload nine turtles (or open it on your phone): the episode opens where you stopped, and Play carries on from there.
4. Press the speed until it says 1.5×: it plays faster. Back 15 and Forward 30 move by those amounts.
5. Play an episode to its end (or move the slider near the end): it's marked read, the bar says "Finished." and nothing else starts. An episode's More menu has no Up Next, and neither does the sidebar.
6. Lock your phone while one plays: the lock screen shows the title and picture, and pause works there.
7. Sign out while an episode plays: it stops and the bar goes. Sign in as another reader: none of your places are there.
