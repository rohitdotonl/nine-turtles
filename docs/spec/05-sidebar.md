# 5. Sidebar

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- Fixed order, top to bottom:
  1. **Unread · Starred · Notes · All articles** (Notes from the first day, empty or not, section 42; decision 0222)
  2. **Folders**, in your own order (a new folder goes at the bottom; decision 0207)
  3. **People and publications not in any folder**, alphabetically, under two headings, **People** then **Publications**: one row per person, however many sources they have (section 36)
  4. **+ Add** ("Add a person or publication", section 3), then **Keys** (icon and word) and **Settings** as an icon, in one row
- Folders stay in the order you give them; people and publications are always A to Z. Renaming a folder keeps its place. See "Arranging the sidebar" below.
- At the top of the sidebar: the nine turtles logo and the "nine turtles" wordmark (a name, not a button: Tab never stops on it; the keyboard's place starts there only on a phone's feeds screen, which has no list, and otherwise on the list's title when the app opens, so screen readers begin with what there is to read), and below it a quiet **field to find a person or folder by name** ("Find a person or folder") (a search icon and its words, the same slim, faintly filled field as the list's search: 36px tall with a mouse on a wide window, 44px on a touch screen or a phone, softly rounded, with no edge), shown whenever you have a feed. Folders and people sit under section names in bold words in full ink, sentence case ("Folders", then "People", then "Publications"): the one label, 13px on a computer and 15px on a touch screen or a phone (decisions 0207 and 0244, replacing the spaced capitals of decisions 0123, 0146 and 0163), and every folder and feed name is in full ink; a name in Devanagari is set a touch larger, so it sits level with the others. A long feed name ends in "…" on one line, so the sidebar stays tidy; its whole name shows as a tooltip, is read in full by screen readers, and is the list's title when you open it (one line, then "…", with the whole name as its tooltip).
- The names of Unread, Starred, Notes and All articles, and the words in the find field, start at one left edge, after a column that holds their icons and the find field's search icon. The section names (Folders, People, Publications), folder names, and the people and publications in no folder stand further out, at the sidebar's margin, where the icons start. On a computer the names after icons start 46px in, and the rest at the 20px margin; a folder's feeds start one step in, on the 46px edge under it. **An indent means "inside a folder"** and nothing else, so the hierarchy shows without relying on the arrow (decisions 0123, 0225). Found by name (the find field), folders and feeds all start on the 46px edge.
- Folders appear once at least one exists. Each folder's name starts where the icons of Unread, Starred and All articles start (the 20px margin), with no icon of its own, and a small arrow button at the row's right end (named "What's in Friends" for screen readers) shows or hides what's in it: the arrow shows, in place of the folder's dot, while the pointer is on the row or the keyboard is on the arrow, and always on a touch screen (44px to press). Its feeds are listed beneath on the names' edge (46px), with a faint line down their left under the folder's name (decision 0123). Folders start closed, and this device remembers which you opened. Choosing a folder's name lists the articles of all its feeds (`/folders/2`) and opens it if it was closed; choosing the folder you're already in folds or unfolds it, so the whole row works as its toggle. On a phone's feeds screen there's no arrow (every row there already ends in a chevron): a folder's feeds are always listed beneath it, one step in.
- **Arranging the sidebar** (decision 0207):
  - **Edit**, at the end of the Folders heading's line: small words in the muted grey (the label's size, 13px on a computer and 15px on a touch screen, regular weight, 44px to press there). With a mouse it shows only while the pointer is on the sidebar or the keyboard reaches it; on a touch screen it always shows. Pressed, it says **Done** in full ink, and every folder, person and publication gets a **handle** (three short lines, the "grip") at its row's end, in place of its dot and arrow. Done ends it.
  - **A folder's handle** opens a small menu: Move to the top, Move up, Move down, Move to the bottom (only the ones that move it). The keyboard stays on the handle, and screen readers hear where it is now ("Friends is 2 of 5.").
  - **A person's handle** opens "Move to a folder" (section 15's sheet), for that one person: they leave the folders they're in.
  - **Dragging** works without Edit. With a mouse, a row drags once it moves a few pixels; with a finger, press and hold a row for half a second until it lifts (a quick swipe still scrolls); a handle drags at once. The row stays in place, faded, and a small copy of its name follows the pointer. Nothing slides or animates. Escape, or letting go anywhere else, changes nothing. Held near the sidebar's top or bottom edge, the sidebar scrolls.
    - A **folder** dragged up or down shows a 2px line in the text color, with a small ring at its start, where it will land.
    - A **person or publication** dragged onto a folder files them there: the folder takes a darker tone (never an outline, which is the keyboard's). Dropped from another folder, they leave that one; holding **Option** on a Mac (**Ctrl** on Windows and Linux) keeps them in both, and the pointer shows a +. Dropped on **People** or **Publications**, they leave the folder they were dragged from and become what they were dropped on; one in no folder dragged between People and Publications becomes the other ("Lena Fischer is now a publication."). While someone is held, People or Publications shows even when it's empty ("Drop someone here."), so there's always somewhere to take them out of a folder. A folder that Undo makes again is open if it was before. Someone in more than one place stays a person (section 36): held over Publications, a line under its heading says "Maya Chen writes in more than one place, so stays under People."; let go there anyway, the note says it too.
  - **Onto someone else**: a person or publication let go on another's row becomes one person with them (section 36, decision 0250); the row under the pointer takes the drop tone and the lifted name says "One person with …". Lists' headings and their empty space still take someone out of a folder or between People and Publications, as above.
  - **An article onto Starred**: a row of the list dragged onto Starred is starred (section 6, decision 0250).
  - **Pause and Unsubscribe at the foot** (decision 0248): while a person or publication is held, the sidebar's foot shows, in place of + Add, Keys and Settings, two places to drop them, side by side, each a faint tile the full height of a touch target: **Pause** in the second ink and **Unsubscribe** in the oxblood words, as words alone (no icon). The one under the pointer turns a step darker (the line grey; the folders' drop tone would take the oxblood words below AA contrast). They're for the pointer only; the keyboard and screen readers pause and unsubscribe in Edit (section 15).
    - Dropped on **Unsubscribe**, they're unsubscribed from at once, every source of theirs, as Edit's Unsubscribe does (section 15): the note says "Unsubscribed from Theo Lindqvist." with Undo, they wait 30 days under Recently unsubscribed, and a folder they were the last in goes too. If the reader starred, highlighted or noted any of their articles, or wrote a note on them, the foot first asks, in a card at the sidebar's foot, the same question Edit asks (section 42): "Keep what you saved from …?", **Keep them** (where the keyboard goes) and Cancel (or Escape; the keyboard goes back to their row), then, under a hairline, the oxblood button naming exactly what it removes. Cancel leaves them followed. If their list or page was open, Unread opens instead.
    - Dropped on **Pause**, every source of theirs not already paused is paused, as Pause for a while does (section 15): "Paused Theo Lindqvist." with Undo, which resumes them. Dropped there when they're all paused already, nothing changes and the note says "Theo Lindqvist is already paused."
  - **A folder left empty goes**: when a drag or a person's handle moves the last one out of a folder, the folder is deleted too, as unsubscribing from its last person does ("Moved Asha Rao to Cooking. Friends went too, as it's empty.").
  - **The note**: each arranging is said in one line at the sidebar's foot, in the list's note's look (BRAND.md → "Messages"), with **Undo**, which puts everything back as it was, a deleted folder in its old place. It stays until it's closed or the next arranging. "Undone." after Undo.
  - The order is each reader's own and kept on the server, so it's the same on every device. Settings › Everyone you follow, the Move sheet and the exported OPML file list folders in it too.
- The search field filters the sidebar as you type: the folders, people and publications whose name contains what you typed (with none, "No person or folder is called “soup”.") (capitals and accents don't matter), in one list. Escape empties it.
- **It also searches articles** (decision 0231): the box at the top is where people look first to find anything, as in Mail or Finder. Under what it found by name (or under "No person or folder is called …"), one row like a sidebar row, with the search icon in the icons' column: **Search articles for “soup”**. It, or Enter in the field (whether a name matched or not), empties the sidebar's field, opens All articles and searches every article for those words at once, as the list's own search does (section 14), with the words in the list's search field and the keyboard there; on a phone, the list's search field is out. Escape there goes back to All articles' own list.
- **No unread counts** (decision 0148): the sidebar never shows a number. **Unread** alone has a **small dot** at its row's end while anything anywhere is unread, in the theme's accent: the article list's unread dot a step smaller (6px). It says something is new, never how much; screen readers hear it described as "Something new", while the link's name stays "Unread" for voice control. Folders, people and publications carry no dot (decision 0242, replacing decision 0148's dot on every row with something new), so nothing calls from every row; their articles keep their own dots in the list. None with Unread off (section 10). Never a badge.
- Your current place is marked as in the list (section 6), a rounded pill of soft neutral grey set a little in from the sidebar's edges, with no color (decision 0057), its name semibold in full ink, and, its icon in full ink. Starred's star stays an outline there: a filled star only ever means an article is starred (decision 0182). When your place changes, its row scrolls into view in the sidebar if it was out of sight, at once and only as far as needed, never smoothly; it doesn't move when dots come and go. On a phone's feeds screen nothing is marked: it's where you start, not a place you're in. Nor while the welcome shows (section 2): you're on the welcome, not in Unread (decision 0191). Under the pointer a row gets a fainter, neutral tone, never the accent.
- While Settings is open, its button at the foot of the sidebar shows as your place, with the same pill, and screen readers hear it as the current page. The shortcut list's button, while its list is open, sits on a faint ink tile instead (section 19). The fill is never the only sign (decision 0054).
- A broken feed looks like any other feed in the sidebar; it just doesn't get new items. Everyone you follow (section 15, in Settings › Your library) lists the feeds that aren't working at its top, and the daily check mentions them too.
- **The same lists for everyone** (decision 0187): Unread (while it's on), Starred, Notes and All articles always show; none can be hidden. So Help, the screenshots and the keys always match what a reader sees.
- Recently read, saved searches and collections were removed in favour of Starred (decision 0174); their old addresses lead to "Wrong turn?".
- If the feeds can't be loaded, the sidebar says "Your feeds couldn't be loaded." (announced to screen readers) with a **Try again** button, which loads them again and, once they're back, puts the keyboard on the sidebar's first row.

**How to check it**
1. Add people and publications whose names start with different letters. They're in alphabetical order under their headings, below the fixed views. A new folder appears at the bottom of Folders.
2. With Unread on and something unread, "Unread" shows a small dot at its row's end, and no number; no folder or person shows one. Read the last unread article (or Mark all as read in Unread): the dot goes.
3. Nowhere in the sidebar is there a number, however many articles are unread.
4. Settings has no Show in the sidebar row, and `/settings/sidebar` opens Settings itself. The sidebar shows Unread, Starred and All articles.
5. Go to `/recent`: it says "Wrong turn?" (decision 0174).
6. Drag a folder above another with the mouse: a line shows where it will land; let go and it's there, with "Moved the folder Friends. · Undo" at the sidebar's foot. Reload: it's still there. Undo puts it back.
7. Drag someone from one folder onto another: they move. If that left the first folder empty, it's gone too, and Undo brings it back in its place, with them in it. Hold Option (Ctrl off a Mac) while dragging: they're in both.
8. On a phone, press and hold a folder until it lifts, then move it. A quick swipe over the same row only scrolls.
9. With only the keyboard: Tab to Edit, press Enter, Tab to a folder's handle, choose Move to the top. The keyboard stays on the handle.
10. Drag someone in several places onto Publications: the line under Publications says why they stay a person, and nothing changes.
11. Type "soup" in Find a person or folder: it says "No person or folder is called “soup”." and offers **Search articles for “soup”**. Press it (or Enter): All articles opens with "soup" searched, the words in its search field.
12. Drag someone down: the foot shows Pause and Unsubscribe instead of + Add and Settings. Let go on Unsubscribe: they're gone, with "Unsubscribed from …" and Undo; Undo brings them back. Star one of someone's articles and do it again: the foot asks "Keep what you saved from …?" first, and Cancel leaves them followed. Let go on Pause: "Paused …" with Undo. Escape while holding someone: the foot is as it was, and nothing changed.
