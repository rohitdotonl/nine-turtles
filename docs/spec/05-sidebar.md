# 5. Sidebar

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- Fixed order, top to bottom:
  1. **Unread · Starred · Notes · All articles** (Notes once there's a highlight or note, section 42)
  2. **Folders**, alphabetically
  3. **People and publications not in any folder**, alphabetically, under two headings, **People** then **Publications**: one row per person, however many sources they have (section 36)
  4. **+ Add** ("Add a person or publication", section 3), then **Keys** (icon and word) and **Settings** as an icon, in one row
- You can't reorder things by hand; order is always alphabetical.
- At the top of the sidebar: the nine turtles logo and the "nine turtles" wordmark (a name, not a button: Tab never stops on it; the keyboard's place starts there only on a phone's feeds screen, which has no list, and otherwise on the list's title when the app opens, so screen readers begin with what there is to read), and below it a quiet **field to find a person or folder by name** ("Find a person or folder") (a search icon and its words, the same slim, faintly filled field as the list's search: 36px tall with a mouse on a wide window, 44px on a touch screen or a phone, softly rounded, with no edge), shown whenever you have a feed. Folders and people sit under small muted section names in spaced capitals ("FOLDERS", then "PEOPLE", then "PUBLICATIONS", on a computer and a phone alike; decisions 0123, 0146 and 0163), and every folder and feed name is in full ink; a name in Devanagari is set a touch larger, so it sits level with the others. A long feed name ends in "…" on one line, so the sidebar stays tidy; its whole name shows as a tooltip, is read in full by screen readers, and is the list's title when you open it (one line, then "…", with the whole name as its tooltip).
- Every name in the sidebar but a folder's (Unread, Starred, Notes and All articles, feeds, and the words in the find field) starts at one left edge, after a column that holds the icons of Unread, Starred, Notes and All articles and the find field's search icon; the section names (Folders, Feeds) and folder names stand further out, at the sidebar's margin, where the icons start. On a computer the names start 46px in and the section names and folder names at the 20px margin, where the icons start; a folder's feeds start on the 46px edge under it, so the hierarchy shows without relying on the arrow (decision 0123).
- Folders appear once at least one exists. Each folder's name starts where the icons of Unread, Starred and All articles start (the 20px margin), with no icon of its own, and a small arrow button at the row's right end (named "What's in Friends" for screen readers) shows or hides what's in it: the arrow shows, in place of the folder's dot, while the pointer is on the row or the keyboard is on the arrow, and always on a touch screen (44px to press). Its feeds are listed beneath on the names' edge (46px), with a faint line down their left under the folder's name (decision 0123). Folders start closed, and this device remembers which you opened. Choosing a folder's name lists the articles of all its feeds (`/folders/2`) and opens it if it was closed; choosing the folder you're already in folds or unfolds it, so the whole row works as its toggle. On a phone's feeds screen there's no arrow (every row there already ends in a chevron): a folder's feeds are always listed beneath it, one step in.
- The search field filters the sidebar as you type: the folders, people and publications whose name contains what you typed (with none, "Nothing in the sidebar has that in its name.") (capitals and accents don't matter), in one list. Escape empties it.
- **No unread counts** (decision 0148): the sidebar never shows a number. A person, feed or folder with something unread (for a folder, any of its feeds) has a **small dot** at its row's end, in the theme's accent: the article list's unread dot a step smaller (6px), so every dot, folder or person, sits in one column. It says something is new, never how much. Screen readers hear "unread" after the name ("Alpha unread"), never a number. No dot on Unread, Starred, All articles or anything else, and none with Unread off (section 10). Never a badge.
- Your current place is marked as in the list (section 6), a rounded pill of soft neutral grey set a little in from the sidebar's edges, with no color (decision 0057), its name semibold in full ink, and, its icon in full ink. Starred's star stays an outline there: a filled star only ever means an article is starred (decision 0182). When your place changes, its row scrolls into view in the sidebar if it was out of sight, at once and only as far as needed, never smoothly; it doesn't move when dots come and go. On a phone's feeds screen nothing is marked: it's where you start, not a place you're in. Nor while the welcome shows (section 2): you're on the welcome, not in Unread (decision 0191). Under the pointer a row gets a fainter, neutral tone, never the accent.
- While Settings is open, its button at the foot of the sidebar shows as your place, with the same pill, and screen readers hear it as the current page. The shortcut list's button, while its list is open, sits on a faint ink tile instead (section 19). The fill is never the only sign (decision 0054).
- A broken feed looks like any other feed in the sidebar; it just doesn't get new items. Everyone you follow (section 15, in Settings › Your library) lists the feeds that aren't working at its top, and the daily check mentions them too.
- **The same lists for everyone** (decision 0187): Unread (while it's on), Starred, Notes (once there's a highlight or note) and All articles always show; none can be hidden. So Help, the screenshots and the keys always match what a reader sees.
- Recently read, saved searches and collections were removed in favour of Starred (decision 0174); their old addresses lead to "Wrong turn?".

**How to check it**
1. Add people and publications whose names start with different letters. They're in alphabetical order under their headings, below the fixed views.
2. With Unread on, a person with unread articles shows a small dot at the end of their row, and no number. "Unread" itself shows no dot. Read their last unread article: the dot goes.
3. Nowhere in the sidebar is there a number, however many articles are unread.
4. Settings has no Show in the sidebar row, and `/settings/sidebar` opens Settings itself. The sidebar shows Unread, Starred and All articles.
5. Go to `/recent`: it says "Wrong turn?" (decision 0174).
