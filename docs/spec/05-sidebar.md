# 5. Sidebar

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- Fixed order, top to bottom:
  1. **Unread · Starred · All · Recently read**
  2. **Saved searches**, alphabetically, once there is one (section 14)
  3. **Collections**, alphabetically, once there is one (section 34)
  4. **Folders**, alphabetically
  5. **Feeds not in any folder**, alphabetically
  6. **Add feed**, then **Keys** (icon and word) and **Settings** as an icon, in one row
- You can't reorder things by hand; order is always alphabetical.
- At the top of the sidebar: the nine turtles logo and the "nine turtles" wordmark (a name, not a button: Tab never stops on it; the keyboard's place starts there only on a phone's feeds screen, which has no list, and otherwise on the list's title when the app opens, so screen readers begin with what there is to read), and below it a quiet **field to find a feed or folder by name** (a search icon and its words, the same slim, faintly filled field as the list's search: 36px tall with a mouse on a wide window, 44px on a touch screen or a phone, softly rounded, with no edge), shown whenever you have a feed. Folders and feeds sit under small muted section names in spaced capitals ("FOLDERS", then "FEEDS", on a computer and a phone alike; decision 0123), and every folder and feed name is in full ink; a name in Devanagari is set a touch larger, so it sits level with the others. A long feed name ends in "…" on one line, so the sidebar stays tidy; its whole name shows as a tooltip, is read in full by screen readers, and is the list's title when you open it (one line, then "…", with the whole name as its tooltip).
- Every name in the sidebar (Unread, Starred, All and Recently read, folders, feeds, and the words in the find field) starts at one left edge, after a column that holds the icons of Unread, Starred, All and Recently read, a folder's arrow and the find field's search icon; the section names (Folders, Feeds) stand further out, at the sidebar's margin. On a computer the names start 46px in and the section names and folder names at the 20px margin, where the icons start; a folder's feeds start on the 46px edge under it, so the hierarchy shows without relying on the arrow (decision 0123).
- Folders appear once at least one exists. Each folder's name starts where the icons of Unread, Starred and All start (the 20px margin), with no icon of its own, and a small arrow button at the row's right end shows or hides the feeds in it: the arrow shows, in place of the folder's count, while the pointer is on the row or the keyboard is on the arrow, and always on a touch screen (44px to press). Its feeds are listed beneath on the names' edge (46px), with a faint line down their left under the folder's name (decision 0123). Folders start closed, and this device remembers which you opened. Choosing a folder's name lists the articles of all its feeds (`/folders/2`) and opens it if it was closed. On a phone's feeds screen there's no arrow (every row there already ends in a chevron): a folder's feeds are always listed beneath it, one step in.
- The search field filters the sidebar as you type: the folders and feeds whose name contains what you typed (capitals and accents don't matter), in one list. Escape empties it.
- **Unread counts:** small, muted grey numbers next to each feed and folder (a folder's is its feeds' added up), right-aligned at the row's end, so every count, folder or feed, sits in one column. No count on "Unread", "Starred" or "All", and never a total. Above 999 shows "999+". Never colored, never a badge. A reader who'd rather not see numbers can hide them all in Settings › Reading (**Unread counts: Hide**, section 18); Unread, the dot and read styling stay.
- Your current place is marked as in the list (section 6), a rounded pill of soft neutral grey set a little in from the sidebar's edges, with no color (decision 0057), its name semibold in full ink, and, for Unread and All, its icon in full ink (Starred's star fills in green). When your place changes, its row scrolls into view in the sidebar if it was out of sight, at once and only as far as needed, never smoothly; it doesn't move when counts change. On a phone's feeds screen nothing is marked: it's where you start, not a place you're in. Under the pointer a row gets a fainter, neutral tone, never the accent.
- While Settings is open, its button at the foot of the sidebar shows as your place, with the same pill, and screen readers hear it as the current page. The shortcut list's button, while its list is open, sits on a faint ink tile instead (section 19). The fill is never the only sign (decision 0054).
- A broken feed looks like any other feed in the sidebar; it just doesn't get new items. The feed health page (section 17, in Settings › Your library) lists the feeds that aren't working, and the daily check mentions them too.
- **Recently read** (`/recent`, decision 0105), with a small clock whose rim turns back, a history sign, as its icon (Let unread go's plain clock face is another thing): the last 50 articles you opened, the one opened last first, whatever the sort order. An article counts as opened each time the reading area shows it (or, for a feed set to open on the website, its page opens), whether that marked it read or it was read already. It's a plain list and nothing more: no count, no number read, no streak, nothing about your reading habits. Only here, quiet day headings part it by when you opened each article, by your device's calendar: **Today**, **Yesterday**, then the weekday for the rest of the last week ("Sunday"), then the date ("12 October", with the year if it isn't this one); small, semibold and muted grey, as the sidebar's section names are, and screen readers hear them as headings. They're worked out when the list loads, so they never change while you look. Its More menu has only Refresh (no Mark all as read, no sort order), it never says "New articles", and searching from it searches everything, as from All. Articles of a feed you've unsubscribed from, and muted ones, aren't listed. It's yours alone: another reader of the same feeds never sees it. It isn't kept on the device for reading offline: without a connection it says "Recently read needs a connection. It shows once you're back online.", and articles opened offline don't join it. `g` then `r` opens it (section 19). Empty, it says "Articles you open will show here."
- **Up Next** (section 35, decision 0113), with a small list icon, after Recently read, only while it holds an episode: the episodes you've queued, in your order, with no count.
- **Collections** (section 34, decision 0108): under a small **Collections** heading after the saved searches, A to Z, each with a small shelf of books where the saved searches have their search icon, and no count. Screen readers hear "(collection)" after each name. The find field doesn't look in them.
- **Hiding lists** (decisions 0105, 0108, 0113): Starred, All, Recently read, Up Next, the saved searches and the collections can each be left out of the sidebar in Settings › Show in the sidebar (section 18); all show by default. Unread, folders and feeds always show, and in river mode All does too (it's home there). A hidden list still works: its address opens it, and `g s`, `g a` and `g r` still go to Starred, All and Recently read. The choice is the reader's, on all their devices.

**How to check it**
1. Add feeds whose names start with different letters. They're in alphabetical order, under the fixed views.
2. In inbox mode, a feed with unread items shows a grey number. "Unread" itself shows no number.
3. A feed with more than 999 unread items shows "999+".
4. Open two articles, then choose Recently read: the second is first, then the first. It shows no number.
5. In Settings › Show in the sidebar, untick Recently read: it leaves the sidebar, on your other devices too. Go to `/recent`: it still opens. Tick it again.
6. Put an article on a new collection (section 34): it appears under Collections in the sidebar, with a shelf icon and no number.
