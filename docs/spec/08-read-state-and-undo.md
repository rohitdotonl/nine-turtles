# 8. Read state, marking unread, "mark all as read", undo

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- An item counts as read as soon as it's shown in the reading pane, by click or by `j`/`k`.
- `m` marks the current item unread again. **Mark unread** in the article's More (…) menu does the same (in the top bar on desktop, in the bottom bar on phones).
- **Mark all as read** (`Shift+A`, or **Mark all as read** in the More (…) menu beside the list's title, section 6): happens instantly, no "are you sure?". It affects **only the items shown on screen right now** (every item listed, including older ones loaded as you scrolled). Items that arrived in the background but aren't displayed yet are never touched.
- **Undo** (`u`, or a visible **Undo**): after an undoable action, a note such as "Marked 23 articles as read" with an **Undo** button floats at the bottom of the list (nothing in the list moves), in a band of the list's own tone that the rows end above, so it never covers them (decision 0048). It stays until your next undoable action or until you leave the list. Pressing `r` doesn't clear it. After an undo it says "Undone."; there's no undoing an undo. Moving through articles doesn't clear it.
- **Catch me up** (decision 0097), in Unread's More (…) menu only, in inbox mode: every article in Unread from before the start of today (midnight on your device) counts as read at once, whether it's listed or not, with no "are you sure?". Today's articles stay unread. The rows on screen turn grey where they are and the counts drop; the note says "Marked 230 articles from before today as read" with **Undo**, which makes exactly those articles unread again. With nothing to catch up, it says "Nothing from before today is unread." If nine turtles' answer doesn't arrive, it says "Catching up couldn't be confirmed. Refresh to see where things stand."
- **Unread lets go by itself** (decision 0097), if you choose it in Settings › Reading (**Let unread go after: Never · 1 week · 2 weeks · 1 month**; Never is the default): an unread article older than that counts as read. It leaves Unread and the counts, and stays in its feed, All, its folders and search, read. Nothing is removed. "Older" goes by the date the list shows, so the old posts of a blog you've just added don't fill Unread either. It starts once your choice has stood for a minute (so moving through the choices with the arrow keys, past "1 week" on the way to "1 month", lets nothing go), then keeps up every half minute; choosing Never again doesn't make anything unread again. An article you mark unread again lets go once more at the next check. It carries on in river mode too, where its row is hidden, so back in inbox mode Unread is as you chose.
- **Mark all above as read** and **Mark all below as read** (decision 0103), in the list's More (…) menu while an article is chosen, and **Mark older as read** (`Shift+O`): every unread article on that side of the chosen one counts as read at once, with no "are you sure?", **including those further down the list that haven't loaded yet**. Above and below follow the list's order; older goes by date, so it is "below" when the list is newest first and "above" when oldest first, and the menu shows `Shift+O` beside that one (in search results, ordered by how well they match, older goes by date and neither shows it). The chosen article itself is left as it is. Only articles that were there when the list was loaded count: one that arrived in the background since is never touched. The list follows the same rules as everywhere (muted articles and feeds that flow past stay out of Unread), and each reader marks only their own. Rows on screen turn grey where they are; the note says "Marked 14 articles as read" with **Undo**, which makes exactly those articles unread again. With nothing unread there it says "Nothing older is unread." (or "Nothing above it is unread.", "Nothing below it is unread."). Without a connection, when part of the list isn't loaded yet it says "Marking articles not yet listed needs a connection. Try again once you're back online." Not in river mode.
- Undo reverses **the last action only**, of these: mark all as read, mark above / below / older as read, catch me up, mark unread, star/unstar, unsubscribe, delete folder.
- Read state is recorded in both reading modes, so switching modes never loses it.
- Each feed's unread number in the sidebar changes at the same moment.
- If nine turtles can't be reached when a mark is made (the connection dropped, or a new version is going live and the server is restarting), it quietly tries again for about a minute, so a short gap loses nothing and says nothing. With no connection at all, the mark waits on the device and is sent once the connection is back, the latest mark winning (section 32).
- If a mark still can't be saved, it's taken back and the note at the bottom of the list says so plainly, such as "The star couldn't be saved.", with a **Try again** button in the Undo's place. The message goes as soon as a later mark is saved (the connection is back), or when you press Try again. A failure never takes back a later action on the same article.

**How to check it**
1. Click an unread item: it turns grey and the feed's unread number drops by one.
2. Press `m`: it's unread again and the number goes back up.
3. Press `Shift+A` in a feed: everything shown turns grey at once. Press `u`: they're unread again.
4. Wait until "New articles · press r" appears, then mark all as read *without* pressing `r`. Press `r`: the new items are still unread.
5. Turn the Wi-Fi off, open an unread article, and turn it back on within half a minute: the article stays read and nothing is said. Leave it off longer: "An article couldn't be marked as read. It's still unread." appears with Try again; once the Wi-Fi is back, opening another unread article (or pressing Try again) clears it.
6. In Unread, open More (…) and choose Catch me up: everything from before today turns grey and the counts drop; today's articles stay unread. Press `u`: they're all unread again.
7. In a feed with more than 50 articles, choose the tenth and press `Shift+O`: the rows below it turn grey, the feed's count drops to 9 (the older ones not yet listed are counted too), and `u` brings them all back. Open More (…): **Mark all above as read** marks the nine above.
8. In Settings › Reading choose Let unread go after: 2 weeks. Unread now starts at two weeks ago; the older articles are still in their feeds, read. Choose Unread counts: Hide: the numbers in the sidebar go, and Unread stays.
