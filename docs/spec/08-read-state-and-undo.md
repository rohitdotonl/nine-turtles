# 8. Read state, marking unread, "mark all as read", undo

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- An item counts as read as soon as it's shown in the reading pane, by click or by `j`/`k`.
- `m` marks the current item unread again. **Mark unread** in the article's More (…) menu does the same (in the top bar on desktop, in the bottom bar on phones).
- **Mark all as read** (`Shift+A`, or **Mark all as read** in the More (…) menu beside the list's title, section 6): happens instantly, no "are you sure?". It affects **only the items shown on screen right now** (every item listed, including older ones loaded as you scrolled). Items that arrived in the background but aren't displayed yet are never touched.
- **Undo** (`u`, or a visible **Undo**): after an undoable action, a note such as "Marked 23 articles as read in Reading Room." with an **Undo** button floats at the bottom of the list (nothing in the list moves), in a band of the list's own tone that the rows end above, so it never covers them (decision 0048). The words sit on the left (wrapping when long) and the buttons on the right, centred beside them, with a **Close** button (×) last. The note goes when you close it, when you open another article with a keyboard to hand (you've moved on; a touch screen has no `u`, so there it stays), when you do something else it then describes, or when you leave the list; never on a timer. Pressing `r` doesn't clear it. Once it's gone, `u` still undoes until you leave the list. **Mark all as read's Undo outlives the list** (decision 0235): its note names the list it was done in, and follows you to the next list you open (or, if you closed it, waits there on `u`), until you do something else that can be undone, or undo it; a slip is often noticed one step later, in another list. After an undo it says "Undone."; there's no undoing an undo. If the undo can't reach nine turtles, it says "That couldn't be undone. Try again in a moment." A save that failed (with **Try again**) stays when you open another article: it still matters. When it failed because the reader is signed out (signed out on another device, or the session ran out), the note says the real reason, "You're signed out, so the star wasn't saved.", with **Sign in** in Try again's place, which loads nine turtles again: the sign-in page, then back to the same list (decision 0230). `u` with nothing to undo says "Nothing to undo." (decision 0229).
- **While the list is out of sight** (an article on a phone, or the list hidden on a computer), the same note shows at the foot of the article instead, floating 12px above it (above a phone's toolbar), with the same words, Undo or Try again and ×, appearing at once (decision 0229). It shows there only on the article it was made on (a failed save, everywhere); opening another article puts it away from the article, while the list keeps it as above. It is heard by screen readers there too: the hidden list's own note is hidden with it.
- **The other Undo lines work the same way** (Everyone you follow, Hiding, Passkeys, Newsletters, Connected assistants, and an import in Your data, section 16): the words on the left, then **Undo** and a **×** (Close the note), drawn as the list's note draws them (the same underlined words, never a bordered button). Each goes when you do something else in that place, press ×, or leave the page; never on a timer. × moves the keyboard's place to the group's heading (Everyone you follow: to Choose…). A refusal on such a line is in oxblood with the alert icon (BRAND.md → "Messages"). An Undo that belongs to one row rather than a line (Not working's cards, Hidden articles' rows, a highlight's own menu) stays the row's own button, beside what it undoes.
- **A list or group that couldn't load** says so with **Try again** whenever trying again is the fix (the article list, Everyone you follow, Not working, Hidden articles, Hiding, Connected assistants, Passkeys); the article list, without a connection, says when it works again instead. In Settings and its pages the line is oxblood with the alert icon. Pressing Try again, the keyboard waits on the page's or group's heading while the list is asked for again, and a second failure says so again.
- **One way to set a backlog down** (decision 0171): Mark all as read, with Undo, is the only one. There's no "Catch me up", no setting that lets unread go by itself, and no marking above, below or older than an article. With no counts anywhere (decision 0148), a backlog weighs little; mark what's shown as read, or simply leave it.
- Undo reverses **the last action only**, of these: mark all as read, mark unread, star/unstar, unsubscribe, delete folder.
- Read state is recorded whether Unread is on or off, so switching never loses it.
- The feed's dot in the sidebar comes or goes at the same moment (decision 0148).
- If nine turtles can't be reached when a mark is made (the connection dropped, or a new version is going live and the server is restarting), it quietly tries again for about a minute, so a short gap loses nothing and says nothing. With no connection at all, the mark waits on the device and is sent once the connection is back, the latest mark winning (section 32).
- If a mark still can't be saved, it's taken back and the note at the bottom of the list says so plainly, such as "The star couldn't be saved.", with a **Try again** button in the Undo's place. The message goes as soon as a later mark is saved (the connection is back), or when you press Try again. A failure never takes back a later action on the same article.

**How to check it**
1. Click an unread item: it turns grey and the feed's unread number drops by one.
2. Press `m`: it's unread again and the number goes back up.
3. On a phone, open an article and choose More › Mark unread: "Marked as unread." with Undo shows above the toolbar. Press `u` with nothing done: "Nothing to undo."
4. Press `Shift+A` in a feed: everything shown turns grey at once, and the note says "Marked 3 articles as read in" the feed's name. Press `u`: they're unread again. Press `Shift+A` again and open All articles: the note is still there, and Undo still brings them back. Star something, then go to another list: the note has gone.
5. Wait until "New articles · press r" appears, then mark all as read *without* pressing `r`. Press `r`: the new items are still unread.
6. Turn the Wi-Fi off, open an unread article, and turn it back on within half a minute: the article stays read and nothing is said. Leave it off longer: "An article couldn't be marked as read. It's still unread." appears with Try again; once the Wi-Fi is back, opening another unread article (or pressing Try again) clears it.
7. Open a list's More (…) menu: it has Mark all as read and Refresh, and nothing else for marking. Settings › Reading has Unread and Sort order only. `Shift+O` does nothing.
