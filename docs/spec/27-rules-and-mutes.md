# 27. Hiding articles about…

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

*Built in V2 (25 October 2026, decision 0077); made one sentence in the
owner's plan, step 8 (4 November 2026, decision 0173); made a plain list of
words that can last a while (6 November 2026, decision 0201).*

Hiding is a list of words. Each one hides articles about it, from everyone or from one person, for a week, a month or for good. Never code, no guessing, no ranking: only what the feed holds and what the reader wrote are used.

**What it does**
- **About:** a word or phrase, found as whole words in an article's title or text, whatever their capital letters ("AI" never matches "said"; the HTML's own words never count). Chinese and Japanese characters are found side by side anywhere in a sentence, and Arabic and Hebrew with or without vowel marks, as search finds them (decision 0228).
- **From:** **everyone**, or one person or publication. A person's rule covers every one of their sources, as their row does in the sidebar.
- **Hidden** means the article is kept, counts as read, and waits in no list: not Unread, All articles, their list or a folder's; only search, **Hidden articles** and, if it's starred, Starred show it. Rules act on articles as they arrive, a new person's first ones included.
- **Settings › Hiding ›** (`/settings/hiding`; decision 0120) starts with one field, **Hide articles about…** (its placeholder; screen readers hear the same as its name), with **Add** beside it, and under them how long, as three choices: **For a week**, **For a month** and **For good**, the usual one, chosen each time the page opens and again after each word. Return in the field adds too. Quote marks around a word are dropped.
- **Before Add, it shows what the word would hide** (decision 0216). A moment after typing rests, under the length choices, in the interface's text: “garden” would hide 5 unread articles. (“1 unread article”; “matches nothing unread now.” when none), semibold; then the newest five of them, each its title in the reading face and under it, muted, who wrote it and where the word was found ("Hal Brennan · Garden Letters · in the text", "Ravi Iyer · in the title"); "And 12 more." when there are more; and "Nothing is hidden until you press Add." It uses Add's own matching, from everyone or, with "Only from", from that person, and hides nothing. Only its first line is spoken, politely; it goes when the field is empty, so after Add too. When it can't say (offline, a word too long), it shows nothing, on purpose: it only helps, and Add still answers for itself.
- **Adding** hides, at once, the unread articles already about the word, with no question first, and new ones as they arrive. The line under the group says what it did: "Hid 3 unread articles about “football”." (", for a week" or ", for a month" before the full stop when it lasts a while), or "Hiding “football”. Nothing unread is about it yet.", with **Undo**, which removes the word and puts back exactly those articles, unread, and says "Undone.". The field empties and keeps the keyboard. An empty field is refused in words under the group, in oxblood with its icon: "Type a word or phrase first."; the same word again for the same people says "“football” is already hidden." and adds nothing.
- **Each word is one row:** the word in quotes, under it in muted words whose articles it hides and for how long ("Everyone · for good", "Maya Chen · 5 days left", counting whole days, rounded up), and **Remove**. There's no switch to pause a word and no Edit: Remove it, and add it again. A word paused before the switch went (decision 0201) still hides nothing and says "paused" in place of how long.
- **Remove** happens at once, with **Undo** on the line under the group ("Removed “sponsored”."), which brings the word back for the time it had left. What a word already hid stays hidden. The keyboard moves to the next word's Remove, or to the field.
- **When its week or month is up,** a word stops hiding and leaves the list. What it already hid stays hidden: Hidden articles has it.
- **For one person:** an article's More menu, **Hide articles about…**, and the same words as a quiet link on a person's or publication's page (section 36) open Settings › Hiding with the keyboard in the field and, above it, "Only from Maya Chen" and **Everyone instead**; the field is named "Hide articles from Maya Chen about" for screen readers. The word's row then says "Maya Chen · for good".
- **Hidden articles ›** appears in the group once anything is hidden, with no count (the list itself shows what's there). Its page (`/settings/hidden`, Everyone you follow's build) lists the newest 200, each with who wrote it, its time and **Unhide**; an unhidden article comes back unread and stays on the page saying so, with Undo, so nothing moves. **‹ Hiding** above its title (decision 0231) or Escape goes back to Hiding, to its row. Under the group, one line: "Hidden articles are kept, and search still finds them."
- **Search still finds hidden articles,** with "· Hidden" after the time. Marking a hidden article unread unhides it.
- **When the person goes:** unsubscribing from one of a person's sources for good moves their word to a source of theirs still followed. Once none is left, the word stays, matching nothing, and its row says "Someone you no longer follow"; Remove takes it away. It never spreads to everyone.
- **Rules from before (decision 0173):** rules that already fit (mute, words, every feed or one feed) carry on, reworded. An old rule that looked in titles only still does, and says so: "“x” (in titles)". A rule for a folder became one rule per person in that folder. Rules with an author, or that starred or marked articles read, were switched off, then deleted (decision 0174). What any rule already did stays done.

**How to check it**
1. Open a person's page and press **Hide articles about…**: Settings › Hiding opens with the keyboard in the field and "Only from" them above it. Type "sponsored": under the field, before anything is hidden, it says how many unread articles it would hide and lists the newest five, each with who wrote it and "in the title" or "in the text". Press Return. If unread articles already match, the line says "Hid N unread articles about “sponsored”." Press Undo: they're back.
2. Add it again. Its row says "{their name} · for good". Choose For a week and add "election": its row says "Everyone · 7 days left".
3. When an article about "sponsored" arrives, it's in no list and not counted. Search for it: it's found, marked "· Hidden". Settings › Hiding › Hidden articles lists it; press Unhide and it's back in Unread.
4. Press Remove on a word, then Undo: it's back as it was.
5. In an article, open More: **Hide articles about…** opens the same field, set to whoever wrote it; **Everyone instead** makes it for everyone.
