# 30. The feed finder (on the website)

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

*Built 26 October 2026 (decision 0099). A free page at nineturtles.app/feed-finder/, for anyone, with no account.*

- One field, **A website's address**, and **Find its feed**. Leaving off `https://` is fine. The line under it says the home page usually works, or a blog's own page inside a bigger site.
- While it looks, a line says "Looking at example.com…". Then it shows **Its feed** (or **Its feeds**): each feed's name, and for a site with several, its newest title (decision 0165; how often it posts isn't said, decision 0199), then its address, with a **Copy address** button (where copying isn't possible, the address is selected instead), and "Found 2 feeds. The first is usually the main one." when there are several. Under them, **Newest in** the first feed's name (linking to the publisher's site), its five newest titles, each linking to the article on the publisher's own website, with its date. A pasted feed address is its own answer.
- Problems are a plain sentence in the same line: not an address, a private address, the site not answering, the page not there, the page too large, no feed found ("No feed was found on that page or in the usual places. Look on the site for a link called RSS, Atom or Feed. Some sites don't have one."), the finder busy ("Try again in a minute"), or that one site looked up a lot lately ("Try again in half an hour").
- It looks exactly as + Add does (section 3, decision 0165): the page given, once; the site's home page and the usual feed addresses, one at a time, if it names none; and each of a site's several feeds once, which also gives the first one's titles. It never visits articles. The same answer is remembered for half an hour (a failure for five minutes), and one site is looked up at most six times in half an hour (counting the blog checkup's lookups too, section 43), so asking again doesn't bother the publisher; nothing about who asked is kept. At most ten of a page's feeds are shown.
- The page's own address carries what was asked (`?address=…`), so a lookup can be shared or reloaded. **Find feed**, a button to drag to the bookmarks bar, opens the finder with the page you're on (pressed on the finder's own page, it says how to keep it). Its address is shown too, with **Copy bookmark address**, for a bookmark made by hand without dragging.
- The app's "Follow this person?" uses the same lookup when a page is shared to it (section 3, "Adding from anywhere"; decisions 0104 and 0138), with the same limits.
- Below: what it looks at, then "Paste the feed's address into any feed reader. In nine turtles it goes in + Add," with a link to "New to feeds?". Linked from the website's footer and from "New to feeds?".
