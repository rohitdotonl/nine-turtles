# 2. First visit (empty state)

*Part of the spec ([index](../SPEC.md)). `docs/DESIGN.md` takes precedence.*

**What it does**
- Until you add your first feed, the reading area shows a welcome, centred like a page: **"Hello. Who would you like to read?"** in the reading serif, "Who would you like to read?" on a line of its own (decision 0142), one sentence ("Paste the address of someone's blog, newsletter or channel.", the owner's core path, point 2), then a wide field that says **"Their website's address"** inside it (its label for screen readers; on a small phone, or with a larger text size, these words step down from 16px to as little as 10.5px so they're never cut off (with a larger text size the field has less room, so they can be smaller than at the usual size; the owner chose fitting over size), ending in "…" only if even that doesn't fit, while what you type stays 16px) with **Add** beside it, and just under the field, centred like everything else in the welcome, a link, **"Bring your list from another app…"**, the other way in: underlined, in the text color, a full 44px tall to touch, its words on the same margin as the heading's (on a phone, the screen's 20px margin; decision 0055) (a message about the address, when there is one, appears between the two and moves the link down, never anything above), which opens the device's file chooser at once for the list another feed reader exports (an OPML file, section 16; decision 0137): once anything comes in, Unread (All articles with Unread off, section 10) opens with the import's summary in its line; a file that brings nothing, or isn't a list, is said in plain words under the link and the welcome stays. All in one centred column about 420px wide. The welcome has the screen to itself: where there's a reading area beside the list, the list steps aside and the welcome takes both, one empty state rather than an empty column beside it (decision 0048); the sidebar shows no find field while there's no feed to find. Where there's no reading area beside the list (a phone, or a medium window with the sidebar shown), the welcome sits in the list's place instead. Starred keeps its own (empty) list. When **+ Add** opens its sheet over the welcome, the welcome stays exactly as it was under the dim, so nothing behind the sheet moves.
- **Or try one** (decision 0198): under "Bring your list from another app…", a small heading, "Or try one", in spaced capitals, then three examples, the same for every reader, as rows parted by thin lines with no fill: the kind in small capitals (Blog, Newsletter, YouTube), the address (www.rohit.onl, jmarriott.substack.com, youtube.com/@TwoCentsPBS) and "Try" at the row's end, each row at least 44px tall. Pressing one follows it at once, exactly as if its address had been pasted (a site offering several feeds gives its first), without asking whether it's a person or a publication (nine turtles' guess stands; Edit changes it). The welcome then stays, and in place of the examples shows **"Newest from {name}"** over that feed's newest title in the reading serif, a link that opens them, with a thin rule in the ink at its left and the line "You're following {name}. What they write next will be waiting for you here."; under it **Start reading**, an outlined button that opens Unread (Add stays the one filled button); then "Try another" with the examples not yet pressed. The person is in the sidebar at once. Going anywhere else in the app ends this: the welcome is gone, as it always is once you follow anyone. If an example can't be added, a plain sentence in red words after an icon says why, and the rows stay. Under them, **"New to feeds? Start here"** (underlined, in the text color, 44px tall; decision 0079).
- **New to feeds?** (decision 0079): the link opens a sheet (`/new-to-feeds`), built as every sheet is (on a phone, a page sheet with its X, and the main action the full width at the foot; its X goes back to the welcome). Its title is "New to feeds?"; two sentences say what a feed is ("A feed is a website's list of its newest writing. Follow a few, and what they publish gathers here for you to read. Nobody else decides what you see."); a link, **"Here's how feeds work"**, opens the website's guide (nineturtles.app/new-to-feeds) in a tab of its own; then **"A few to start with"**: a grouped list of five feeds, each row its writer's name over their site's address (together one link to their site, in a tab of its own, at least 44px tall), one muted line on what they write, and a tick box at the row's end, none ticked; pressing anywhere in the row but the link ticks it. The five are fixed and the same for every reader, five people who write their own blogs (decision 0197): James (jamesg.blog), Derek Sivers (sive.rs), Herman Martinus (herman.bearblog.dev), Jeff Kaufman (jefftk.com) and Ava (blog.avas.space), each by their own feed address, so adding one never looks at a web page. Each one added is filed as a person, someone you might write back to (section 36's choice), not left to the guess. At the foot, **Cancel** and the main button: with nothing ticked it reads **Add all five** and adds all five; once any is ticked it reads **Add 2 chosen** (the number ticked) and adds only those. They are added one after another, exactly as if their addresses had been pasted, with "Adding feeds… 1 of 3 done" in plain text meanwhile. One feed added opens that feed; several open Unread (All articles with Unread off, section 10). If none could be added the sheet says so ("Those feeds couldn't be added. Try again later.") and keeps them ticked; if only some could, the list's note names the others. The welcome, and so this link, is gone once you follow anything; the sheet's address then opens the home view instead. Each reader on a nine turtles with several readers sees the same welcome on their own first visit.

**How to check it**
1. On a fresh installation, sign in. (On a phone, nine turtles opens on the welcome while you follow no one, rather than on the home screen's empty lists; Back goes to the home screen. Decision 0137.) Beside the sidebar there's one welcome, centred, with the field, Add and "Bring your list from another app…"; no empty list beside it, and the sidebar has no find field. Nothing tells you to press `j`. Press `a`: the sheet opens and the welcome behind it doesn't move. Press Escape, then "New to feeds? Start here": a sheet explains feeds, links to the website's guide and lists five feeds, none ticked. Tick two and press Add 2 chosen: both appear in the sidebar and Unread opens.
2. On a phone, the welcome fills the list's screen, left-aligned on the screen's margin about 96px under the top line, and it's the one heading shown: the list's title steps aside (screen readers still hear it as the screen's name).
